    Measure the drawings. Count the work. Price the job.

    1. Open PDFs (or drop them here). They open on this computer; nothing is uploaded.
    2. Give each sheet a scale: pick a Preset, or Calibrate on a dimension you know.
    3. Make an item under Items (linear, area or count), then click on the sheet to take it off.
    4. Add costs and an assembly to the item; the quantities and the job total below update as you go.
    5. Save takeoff keeps everything in a project file that reopens on top of the same PDFs.

          TakeoffCraft settings

          These settings are only for TakeoffCraft, and you can change them only here, in the app.

          Feet and inches

          How lengths read on the sheet, in the panels and in the status bar. Only the display is rounded: quantities, costs and exports are worked out from the exact lengths (exports show 1/16").

          New items

          Every item you make from now on starts with this waste. You can still change it per item, and items you already have keep theirs.

          Auto count

          Where the Match slider is when a search finishes. Lower finds more look-alikes for you to reject; higher offers fewer. 75% is the start.

          Drawing

          Door openings and gaps up to this wide count as closed when the Room tool fills a room. Wider than your widest door lets a room run into the next one.

          While you measure or calibrate, a point near a line of the PDF jumps to its end, its midpoint, a crossing, or the nearest point on it, and a marker shows which. F3 or Snap in the status bar turns it on and off. Scanned sheets have no lines to snap to.

          Runs, outlines and cut-outs then go at 0°, 45°, 90° or 135° from the last point; hold Shift to click at any angle. Arc points and circles are never snapped.

          Corner indicator

          In the 3D check view only. The view cube sits at the top right: click a face, edge or corner to look from there, drag it to orbit, drag the compass ring to turn. The axis icon is a small X, Y, Z marker at the bottom left. The flat sheet has neither.

          Theme

          The theme applies to every CadCraft app and the site.

          Language

          The language applies to every CadCraft app. The app reloads to change it.

          Screen

          Browsers allow full screen only after a click or key press, so it starts on your first click in the drawing.

          Toolbar

          Arrange: drag tools within their group, and drag a group by its name.

          Panels

          You can also drag a panel's inner edge. Double-click the edge to put it back.

          Panels

          Drag a panel by its name to dock it left, right or at the bottom, or drop it over the drawing to float it. A new window can go on your other screen. Reset layout puts every panel back where it started.

          Add-ons

            Add-ons add features to TakeoffCraft. Switching one off takes effect at once; some need a reload to go completely.

            Developer mode is on: add-ons that are not signed can load. Only use add-ons you trust.

            Panel widths apply when the panels sit beside the drawing (wider windows).
