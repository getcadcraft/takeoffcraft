## What you'll do

Draw a curved edge in a linear run, an area or a cut-out, using the arc method that matches the dimensions you have.

## Steps

1. Start a run, an outline or a cut-out and place the point where the curve starts.
2. Turn on {{ui:Arc}} in the toolbar or press {{key:R}}.
3. Open {{ui:Arc method}} in the sheet bar. It shows only while Arc is on. Choose 3-Point, Start, Center, End, Start, Center, Angle, Start, Center, Length, Start, End, Angle, Start, End, Direction, Start, End, Radius, Center, Start, End, Center, Start, Angle or Center, Start, Length.
4. Follow the status bar. It says what the arc waits for: its center, end point, angle, length, direction or radius. Click a point, or type the number (angle, length, radius and direction).
5. When the last input is given, the arc is added to the run. {{key:Esc}} cancels the arc, {{key:Backspace}} takes back its last step, and {{key:R}} turns Arc off.

## Notes

- An arc is refused, with a message in the status bar, when its points coincide or lie in a line, or when the radius cannot reach the end point.
- Its length is the exact arc length, and old files open the same. More: [Measure linear runs](topic:measure-linear#arcs-and-curved-walls).
