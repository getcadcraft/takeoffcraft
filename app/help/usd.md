In the 3D check view, choose {{ui:USD package (.usdz)}} beside {{ui:GLB}}.

Each object (a wall, a slab, a plate, a count post or a run) is one object in the file, named after its item, carrying the item's name and the quantity the view measured. Slabs and plates are see-through as in the view. The file is in metres with Z up.
