## What you'll do

Check that every line of the estimate is priced, then put your name to it. The takeoff file records who signed, when, and for what total.

## Lines with no rate

In **Quantities › By item**, a line that has a quantity and no material or labor rate carries **no rate**, and the bar above the table says how many there are. Such a line adds nothing to the total. An item with no rate of its own is not flagged when one of its components is priced: an assembly is priced through its parts. The exported estimate says **No rate** in the Notes column of each such line and counts them.

## Sign the estimate off

1. Click **Sign off the estimate** above the Quantities table.
2. The dialog states the total you are signing and lists the lines with no rate. Type your name and, if you like, your role.
3. Click **Sign off**. The bar now reads "Signed off by … on …" with the total. Save the takeoff to keep it in the file.

The sign-off belongs to the figures you signed. Any later change to a quantity, a rate or a line clears it, and TakeoffCraft tells you. Undo brings back the estimate you signed, and its sign-off with it. Renaming a line is not a change of a figure. **Remove the sign-off** takes it away.

A takeoff file that was edited outside TakeoffCraft after it was signed opens without the sign-off, with a note saying so. The exported estimate (CSV and XLSX) names the sign-off under the totals.

Only a person signs. An AI assistant can read whether the estimate is signed off and which rates are missing; it has no way to sign.

## Click a figure to see where it came from

Click a quantity or an amount of a line to select the marks it was measured from. When they are on another sheet, that sheet opens. Click a figure of the **Job total** line to select every mark on the sheet. A typed quantity has no marks.

## Good to know

- The sign-off records a name as typed. It is a record in the file, not an electronic signature.
- With bid packages awarded to subcontractors or alternates in the takeoff, the total signed is the base bid shown as **Job total**.
