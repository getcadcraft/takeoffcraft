## Waxa aad samayn doontid

Geeso qalloocan oo socod, aag ama god ah sawir, adigoo isticmaalaya habka qaansada ee la waafaqa cabbirrada aad haysato.

## Tallaabooyinka

1. Bilow socod, khad ama god, oo dhig barta uu curinta ka bilaabmayo.
2. Shid {{ui:Qaanso}} qalabka sare ama riix {{key:R}}.
3. Fur {{ui:Habka qaansada}} bar xaashiga. Waxay muuqataa oo keliya inta Qaanso uu shid yahay. Dooro 3-Point, Start, Center, End, Start, Center, Angle, Start, Center, Length, Start, End, Angle, Start, End, Direction, Start, End, Radius, Center, Start, End, Center, Start, Angle ama Center, Start, Length.
4. Raac status bar-ka. Waxay sheegaysaa waxa qaansadu sugayo: dhexaadkeeda, barta dhammaadka, xagalka, dherer, jihada ama shucaaca. Riix barta, ama qor tirada (xagal, dherer, shucaac iyo jihe).
5. Marka gelinta u dambaysa la siiyo, qaansada waxa lagu daraa socodka. {{key:Esc}} wuu joojiyaa qaansada, {{key:Backspace}} wuxuu soo celinayaa tallaabadii u dambaysay, {{key:R}} wuxuu damiyaa Qaanso.

## Ogeysiisyo

- Qaansada waa la diidaa, fariin status bar-ka ku jirta, marka barooradeeda isku dhacaan ama ku jiraan khad toos ah, ama shucaacu uusan gaari karin barta dhammaadka.
- Dherer qaansada waa dherer qaanso saxda ah, faylashii hore waxay ku furmaan isla sidaas. Dheeraad: [Cabbir socodyada toosan](topic:measure-linear#arcs-and-curved-walls).
