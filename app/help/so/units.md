## Unugyada shayga

| Nooca shayga | Unug | Magac |
|---|---|---|
| Socod | LF | fut socod (caadi) |
| Socod | m | mitir |
| Aag | SF | fut labajibbaaran (caadi) |
| Aag | SY | yaardh labajibbaaran |
| Aag | SQ | saqaf (100 fut labajibbaaran) |
| Aag | ac | acre |
| Aag | m² | mitir labajibbaaran |
| Aag | ha | hektaar |
| Tiro | EA | mid kasta |

Ku beddel {{ui:Unug}} tafatirka shayga wakhti kasta, xitaa ka dib markii la cabbiray; tirada waa la beddelaa isla markiiba.

Liiska {{ui:Unug}} wuxuu muujiyaa unugyada loo shiday {{ui:Dejinta}} › {{ui:Guud}} › {{ui:Unugyada}}, iyo had iyo jeer unugga shayga oo gaar ah. Shay cusub wuxuu bilaabmaa unugga dherer ama aag ee halkaas lagu calaamadeeyay, marka uu ka mid yahay unugyada shayga; haddii kale LF ama SF. Eeg [Unugyada, qorista qiimaha iyo liisaska unugyada](common:unit-lists).

## Unugyada doorsoomayaasha qaacidada

| Unugga shayga | L, P, H | A |
|---|---|---|
| LF | fut | fut labajibbaaran (aagga darbiga) |
| m | mitir | mitir labajibbaaran (aagga darbiga) |
| SF | fut | fut labajibbaaran |
| SY | fut | yaardh labajibbaaran |
| SQ | fut | saqaf |
| ac | fut | acre |
| m² | mitir | mitir labajibbaaran |
| ha | mitir | hektaar |

Shayada SY, `A` waa yaardh labajibbaaran, `P` iyo `H` ayaa fut ku jira. Mugga shay SY ah waa `A * 9 * H / 27` yaardh saddex-geesle; haddii aad SF u isticmaasho dabaqyada, way fududaa.

## Meelaha unugyada aan lagu beddelin

Qaar ka mid ah akhrinta had iyo jeer waxay isticmaalaan fut:

- akhrinta tooska ah ee bar xaaladda markaad sawireyso (fut-inji, SF),
- **Calaamadda la doortay** (fut-inji, LF iyo SF),
- natiijada hubinta (fut-inji, oo leh fut tobanle iyo mitir),
- dhererka qaybaha iyo perimeter-yada xaashiga Marks ee spreadsheet-ka (fut-inji).

Wadarta shayga, miiska {{ui:Tirooyinka}}, qaacidooyinka iyo xaashiga Qiyaas kharash waxay isticmaalaan unugga shayga.

## Miisaanka iyo unugyada waa kala gooyan

Miisaanka xaashi wuxuu sheegaa sida naqshaddu u weyn tahay; unugga shayga wuxuu sheegaa sida loo warbixinayo. Naqshad mitir oo `1:100` ah waxaa lagu xisaabin karaa LF, naqshad imperial ahna waxaa lagu xisaabin karaa m². Hagaajintu waxay aqbashaa unug kasta: `24'-6"`, `7.5 m`, `7500 mm`.
