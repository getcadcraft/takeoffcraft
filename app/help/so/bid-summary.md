Qiyaasta waxay qiimeysaa waxa aad cabbirtay. **Soo koobidka tartanka** ayaa u beddelaa qiime: wuxuu ku daraa cashuurta iibka, overhead, profit iyo bond, wuxuuna sameeyaa **PDF soo jeedin** oo leh waxa ku jira iyo waxa laga reebay.

Fur {{ui:Export}} › {{ui:Bid summary & proposal…}}. Waxa ku jira oo dhan waa la kaydiyaa takeoff-ka, isbeddel kasta waa la dib u celin karaa.

## Kor u kordhinta

Ku qor boqolkiiba mid kasta shanta (ka tag kuwa kale 0). Waxaa lagu xisaabiyaa tallaabadan, mid kasta wuxuu ku salaysan yahay wixii ka sare:

| Safka | Sida loo xisaabiyo |
|---|---|
| Material, Labor | Wadarta qiyaasta (base bid marka takeoff-ku leeyahay [ikhtiyaarro](topic:bid-areas#alternates-base-bid-plus-adds-and-deducts)). |
| Labor burden | Burden % × shaqo: cashuurta mushaharka, caymiska iyo faa'iidooyinka shaqaalahayaga. |
| **Cost** | Agab + shaqo + culayska shaqada. |
| Sales tax on material | Tax % × agab. |
| Overhead | Overhead % × (kharash + cashuur). |
| Profit | Profit % × (kharash + cashuur + overhead). |
| Bond | Bond % × wax kasta oo sare. |
| **Bid** | Wadarta safafka. |

Safka kasta waxaa lagu wareejiyaa sentiga. Xaashida buug-shaqada waxay muujinaysaa safafkan isla hoosta wadarta xaashida Estimate, oo leh qaacido nool. Soo koobidku wuu cusboonaanayaa marka aad qorto: {{key:Enter}} wuxuu qaadaa boqolkiiba. Ikhtiyaar kasta waxaa lagu muujiyaa markups isku mid ah oo ku saabsan kharashkiisa, si milkiiluhu u arko waxa ikhtiyaarku dhab ahaan beddelayo.

## Soo jeedinta

Buuxi waxa soo jeedintu sheegi karto:

- **Project** (magaca takeoff-ka haddii madhan), **To** (macmiilka) iyo **From** (shirkadaada).
- **Inclusions** iyo **Exclusions**: mid safka kasta; dash ama bullet hore waa fiican.
- **Terms**: qoraal xor ah, sida "Valid for 30 days." (Ansax 30 maalmood.)
- **List the items and quantities**: shaqada, walxaha mid mid, tirooyinka, iyadoo aan qiimo lahayn. Ka saar calaamada haddii soo jeedintu tahay lump-sum.

Dooro {{ui:Proposal PDF}}. PDF-ku (`<takeoff> - proposal.pdf`) wuxuu leeyahay taariikhda, sanduuqa qiimaha oo leh markup kasta iyo tartanka, ikhtiyaarrada, shaqada, inclusions iyo exclusions, terms, iyo khadadka saxiixa iyo taariikhda. Haddii tirooyinka qaarkood aan la cabbiri karin (xaashi aan miisaan lahayn), waxay sheegtaa hoosta qiimaha.

## Dhoofinta

Marka takeoff-ku leeyo soo koobid tartan, CSV-gu wuxuu ku dhammaadaa, buug-shaqadana waxay helaysaa xaashi **Bid summary**, oo leh safka kasta, boqolkiiba iyo qadarka. Eeg [Dhoofi qiyaasta](topic:export-estimate).
