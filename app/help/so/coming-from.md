Haddii aad xisaabinta qadarka ku samaysay PlanSwift ama qalabka cabbirka ee Bluebeam Revu, inta badan TakeoffCraft waad ku heli doontaa wax aad la aqoon: xaashiyada qorshaha, miisaan xaashi kasta, waxyaabaha aad cabirto, iyo wadar qiimeysan. Siday qaybaha isugu xidhmaan way ka duwan tahay meelo qaar, meelahaas waa hoos.

Haddii aad PlanSwift ama On-Screen Takeoff ogtahay, dooro qaabka menyuu {{ui:Miiska xisaabinta qadarka}} ee {{ui:Dejinta}} › {{ui:TakeoffCraft}} › {{ui:Menyuyaasha, furayaasha iyo mouse-ka}}: bar menyuu oo leh Fayl, {{ui:Wax ka beddel}}, Muuqaal, {{ui:Miisaan}}, Xisaabinta qadarka, {{ui:Walxaha}}, {{ui:Calaamadin}} iyo {{ui:Dhoofin}}, oo ku dul yaal safka qalabka xisaabinta, iyo diyaarinta miisaanka ee bar xaashiga. Eeg [Menyuyaasha, furayaasha iyo mouse-ka](topic:menus-keys-and-mouse).

## Farqiyaasha dadka qaarkiis ku dhacaan

**Xisaabinta qadarka ma ku jirto PDF-kaaga.** Wax kasta oo aad samayso ma beddelayo PDF-yadaada. Kaydi faylka `.takeoff.json` oo ku hay naqshadaha. Qof u dirta PDF calaamadsan ma siinayo xisaabin la wax ka beddeli karo: u dir faylka mashruuca iyo PDF-yada asalka ah, ama [PDF oo ku jira xisaabinta qadarka](topic:save-and-reopen#keep-the-takeoff-inside-the-pdf).

**PDF-yada waxaa lagu xidhiidhiyaa waxa ku jira, ma aha magacooda.** Xaashi la dib u eegay oo lagu kaydiyay magaca faylka hore, TakeoffCraft wuxuu u arkaa fayl cusub, oo aan miisaan iyo calaamad lahayn. Calaamadahaaga hore waxay ku sii jiraan nuqulka hore, oo weli waa tirinayaan. Eeg [Kaydi oo dib u fur xisaabinta qadarka](topic:save-and-reopen#when-a-pdf-has-changed).

**Calaamadaha aan miisaan lahayn waa laga reebaa, ma aha wax loo tiriyo eber.** Haddii xaashi aan miisaan lahayn, socodyadeeda iyo aagyadeeda ma tirinayaan, miisaanka waxa muuqda "Aan dhammaystirnayn". Barnaamijyo badan waxaad arki lahayd 0. Eeg [Dejinta miisaanka](topic:set-the-scale#why-unscaled-sheets-are-not-counted-as-zero).

**Ma jiro saadaal cabbir bogga.** Diyaaruhu waxay u qaataan in PDF-ka la daabacay cabbir buuxa. PDF nus ama "fit to page" wuxuu u baahan yahay {{ui:Hagaaji}}. Had iyo jeer isticmaal {{ui:Hubi}}.

**Hal walax, hal nooc oo ugu weyn, ka badan haddii aad codsato.** Walax waa socod, aag ama tiro, nooceeda waa la xidhaa markay calaamado yeelato. Shuruud siinaysa aag iyo wareegeeda labadaba waa walax aag ah: wareegeeda waa doorsoomaha `P`. Darbi leh dherer iyo aag labadaba waa walax socod ah oo leh joog. Walax shuruudo badan leh, sida qol leh sagxad, saldhig iyo albaabbo, calaamadee noocyada kale hoos ee **Sidoo kale cabbir**: mid kastaa qiimo gaar ah ayuu safkiisa u helaa. Eeg [Hal walax, dhowr nooc calaamadood](topic:create-items#one-item-several-kinds-of-marks).

**Qaacidooyinku waxay ku shaqeeyaan wadarta.** Qaacidooyinka isku-dhiska waxay isticmaalaan wadarta walaxda xaashiyada oo dhan, ma aha socod kasta gaar ahaan. `N` (tirada socodyada ama aagagga) waxa loo isticmaalaa xarig kasta oo dheeraad ah, sida hal stud dheeraad ah darbi kasta.

**Wareegga wuxuu ku jiraa godadka.** `P` aagga wuxuu ku daraa geesaha godadkiisa. Eeg [Cabbir aagag leh godad](topic:measure-areas#read-the-area).

**Jiidista wuxuu dhaqaajiyaa xaashiga.** Jiidista badhanka bidix ee xaashiga wuxuu dhaqaajiyaa, qalab kasta marka laga reebo {{ui:Tirin otomaatig ah}}. Weligaa uma baahnid inaad u beddesho qalab dhaqaajin. Tiirka wareegga wuxuu weyneeyaa ama yareeyaa; ma wareejiso.

**Nuqul soo celin, ma aha daruur.** TakeoffCraft wuxuu nuqul shaqadaada ku hayaa browser-kan, wuxuuna ku soo celinayaa kadib shil ([Haddii tab-ka ama browser-ku joogsado](topic:save-and-reopen#if-the-tab-or-the-browser-stops)), laakiin xisaabinta qadarka waa adiga kaydinta: {{key:Mod+S}}. Kaydinta daruurta weli ma diyaar.

**Wax walba browser-ka ayay ku jiraan.** Ma jiro wax la rakibo, qorshayaasha weligood ma la soo rarin. Barnaamij desktop ah, TakeoffCraft wuxuu ku jiraa CadCraft Studio, oo leh toddobada app ee kale.
