## Dherarka

Waxaa isticmaala **Masaafada dhabta ah** ee {{ui:Hagaaji}}, iyo {{ui:Joog}} iyo {{ui:Qoto}} tafatiraha shayga.

| Waxa aad qortaa | Macnaha |
|---|---|
| `24'-0"` | 24 feet |
| `24'-6 1/2"` | 24 feet iyo 6½ inch |
| `12' 6 1/2"` | 12 feet iyo 6½ inch (meel bannaan halkii dash) |
| `12'-6-1/2"` | 12 feet iyo 6½ inch (dash u dhexeeya qaybaha) |
| `24'` | 24 feet |
| `30"` | 30 inch |
| `6 1/2"` | 6½ inch |
| `3/4"` | ¾ inch |
| `24 ft 6 in` | 24 feet iyo 6 inch (sidoo kale `foot`, `feet`, `inch`, `inches`) |
| `24.5` | 24.5 unugga caadiga ah (eeg hoos) |
| `24.5'` | 24.5 feet |
| `7.5 m` | 7.5 mitir |
| `750 cm` | 750 sentimitir |
| `7500 mm` | 7,500 millimitir |

- Tiro caadi ah waa **feet** hagaajinta iyo shayada LF, SF ama SY, iyo **mitir** shayada m ama m².
- Calaamadaha qalinka iyo kuwa habaynta (′ ″ ’ ”) waxay u shaqeeyaan sida `'` iyo `"`. Laba apostrophe `''` waxay tirinayaan `"`.
- Commas waa la iska dhaafaa: `1,200 mm` waa 1200 mm.
- Jajabyadu waxay u baahan yihiin tirooyin dhammaystiran labada dhinac: `6 1/2"` way shaqeysaa, `6.5 1/2"` ma shaqeyso.
- Masaafo eber iyo taban waa la diidaa.

Fariimaha:

- "Ku qor masaafo, sida 24'-0" ama 7.5 m." (goobta waa madhan)
- "Ku qor masaafo ka weyn eber."
- "Isticmaal feet iyo inch (24'-6 1/2"), feet tobanle, m, cm ama mm." (unug aan la aqoon)
- "Masaafadaas lama akhriyi karo. Isku day 24'-6 1/2" ama 24.5."

Si aad u tirtirto joogta ama qotada shayga, goobta madhig.

### Xisaabinta

Goobta dherer kasta, iyo sanduuqa qorista intii aad sawirayso, waxay sidoo kale aqbali kartaa xisaab leh `+`, `-`, `*`, `/` iyo qaansooyin:
`4+7/16` (4.4375 inch unugga caadiga ah), `12'6" + 3/4"`, `(24'-0")/3` (8 feet), `2*450mm`.

- Dash la qoro dherer wuxuu weli qayb ka yahay: `24'-6"` waa 24 feet 6 inch, `6-1/2` waa 6½. Dhig meel bannaan dash hareeraha si aad u jarto: `24' - 6"` waa 23'-6".
- Tiro caadi ah wuxuu dherer u dhufaa ama u qaybiyaa sida tiro; marka lagu daro dherer, waa unugga caadiga ah.
- `abs`, `round`, `ceil` iyo `floor` sidoo kale way shaqeeyaan. Xisaab aan la akhriyi karin waa la diidaa, waxaana lagu sheegayaa meesha ay ku qaldantay.

## Sida dherarka loo muujiyo

Dherarka waxa lagu muujiyaa feet, inch iyo sideeda meelood: `12'-6 1/2"`, `0'-0 3/4"`. Qiimayaasha waxa loo wareejiyaa 1/16 inch ee ugu dhow si loo muujiyo; tirooyinka laftooda ma wareejinayn. Qiimayaasha tobanleyda waxay muujiyaan laba tobanle iyo kala-soocaha kumanka, sida `1,234.50 LF`.

## Qiimaha (alaabta, shaqada iyo wadarta)

| Waxa aad qortaa | Macnaha |
|---|---|
| `12.5` | $12.50 |
| `$12.50` | $12.50 |
| `1,250` | $1,250.00 |
| `0.385` | $0.385 (ilaa afar tobanle; kuwa ka badan waa la wareejiyaa) |
| (madhan) | $0 |

Fariin: "Ku qor qiime sida 12.50." ama "Qiimahaas aad ayuu u weyn yahay." Qiimayaasha taban lama aqbalo.

Lacagaha dheeraadka ah (alaabta, shaqada, wadarta) waxa lagu xisaabiyaa ilaa sentiga: tiro oo qashin leh × qiime, oo la wareejiyo fogaanta eber, siday miiska xisaabta u sameeyo `ROUND(x, 2)`.

## Boqolkiiba qashinka

| Waxa aad qortaa | Macnaha |
|---|---|
| `10` | 10% |
| `7.5%` | 7.5% |
| (madhan) | 0% |

Ilaa laba tobanle, 0% ilaa 1000%. Fariimaha: "Ku qor boqolkiiba sida 10 ama 7.5." ama "Qashin ka weyn 1000% lama oggola."

## Magacyada iyo unugyada

- Magaca shayga iyo qaybta: ilaa 200 xaraf.
- {{ui:Unug}} qaybta: qoraal kasta oo ilaa 20 xaraf, sida `EA`, `sheets`, `CY`, `gal`. Waa calaamad keliya; TakeoffCraft ma beddelo.
