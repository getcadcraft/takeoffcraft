{{ui:Cusub}} wuxuu xiraa PDF kasta oo bilaabaa xisaabin madhan bogga isku mid.

1. Kaydi xisaabinta hadda haysa haddii aad rabto inaad ilaaliso: {{ui:Kaydi xisaabinta}} ama {{key:Mod+S}}.
2. Dooro {{ui:Cusub}} koox {{ui:Fayl}}.
3. Haddii isbeddello aan la kaydin jiraan, TakeoffCraft wuxuu weydiinayaa **Xisaabin aan la kaydin**: dooro {{ui:Marka hore kaydi xisaabinta}} (wuxuu soo dejinayaa mashruuca, kadibna nadiifiyaa), {{ui:Ka tag isbeddellada}}, ama {{ui:Jooji}}.
   Daaqaddu waxay ku noqotaa xaaladdeeda madhan: PDF ma jiro, shay ma jiro, calaamad ma jiro, miisaan ma jiro. Saddex-ka hoose wuxuu yidhi: "Xisaabin cusub. Fur PDF-yada si aad u bilowdo."

Waxa la nadiifiyo:

- PDF kasta oo furan, xaashiyadiisa iyo sawirrada yaryar,
- walxaha, isku-dhisyada, calaamadaha iyo miisaanka,
- weynaynta iyo meesha xaashi kasta,
- taariikhda ka noqoshada ({{ui:Cusub}} ma ka noqon kartid),
- habka qaansada, iyo raadinta tirin otomaatig ah ama dib u eegista.

Faylasha diskaaga ma taabtaan. Si aad xisaabintii hore dib u soo qaado, fur `.takeoff.json` iyo PDF-yadeeda: eeg [Kaydi oo dib u fur xisaabinta](topic:save-and-reopen).

:::note
Xisaabin cusub kasta wuxuu bilaabmaa iyada oo aan wax shay lahayn. Si aad shayada u keento, marka hore koobiyee: {{ui:Koobiyee shayga}} hoosta Walxaha (ama koobiyee calaamadaha, kuwaas oo iyaga qaba shayadooda), kadibna {{ui:Dhaji}} xisaabinta cusub. Eeg [Koobiyee, dhaji iyo nuqul calaamadaha](topic:copy-paste). Shayada aad shaqo kasta isticmaasho, ku hay [maktabadda qiimaha](topic:price-library) adigoo ka soo ku dari, ama ku dar [xirmada ganacsiga](topic:trade-packs).
:::
