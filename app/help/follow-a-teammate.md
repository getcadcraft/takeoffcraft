## What you'll do

In a Live session, see each person's tool and sheet, and follow one person's view.

## Steps

1. Start or join a Live session from CadCraft Studio. See [Work live with others](topic:work-live).
2. Choose the {{ui:People}} button to open the people list. Each row has the person's color and name, the tool they are running, the sheet they are on, and when they last edited.
3. Choose {{ui:Follow}} on a name. Your view moves with theirs, and a bar says you are following them; {{ui:Stop}} ends it. Panning or zooming yourself also stops it.
4. As host, choose {{ui:Ask everyone to follow me}} in the list to ask the others to follow you. They get a bar with {{ui:Follow}}. Choose {{ui:Stop asking everyone to follow me}} to end it.

## Notes

- If the person is on another sheet, the bar says which one. If their view cannot be followed in your view, it says that too.
- Presence is never saved in the takeoff or in anyone's undo.
