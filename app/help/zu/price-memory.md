{{ui:Amanani}} kwithuluzi (uphawu lwe-dollar) livula okukhunjulwayo yi-TakeoffCraft ngezintengo osebenzise zona. Iluhlu elilula: ngegama ngalinye lento, amazinga kumabalo akho amuva. Igcinwa kule bhrawuza (ku-Studio, ibhrawuza layo) ecaleni komtapo wezintengo, ingasoze ingene efayeleni lokubala, futhi ayikho ethunyelwa noyiphi indawo. Ayisiyo imodeli eqeqeshiwe.

## Okukhunjuliwayo

Uma ulondoloza ukubala, yonke into enentengo futhi esetshenzisiwe (inamamaki noma inani elithayiwe elingaphezu kwe-zero) ikhunjulwa ngegama nohlobo lwayo: izinga lezinto, izinga labasebenzi, ukulahleka kanye neyunithi. Ukubala kwefayela kuyiwo wonke amapulani okhishwa kuwo, ngakho ukulondoloza ukubala okufanayo futhi kubuyisela izintengo zawo, akwengezi omunye umsebenzi. Ukubala okungu-12 okugcina kugcinwa ngegama ngalinye.

Ukuze uqale ngomsebenzi wakudala, khetha {{ui:Funda kusuka kubalo olwedlule…}} bese ukhetha ukubala okulondoloziwe (.json). Izinto zalo ezinentengo nezisetshenzisiwe zikhunjulwa ngendlela efanayo.

Amagama ayahambisana uma amagama awo efana, nanoma kunjani amacala noma izimpawu ("2x4 Stud, 8 ft" no "2x4 stud 8 FT" zifana). Amanye amagama ohlobo olufanayo (into ye-linear nento yokubala) akuyona intengo efanayo.

## Sebenzisa kukubala olusha

1. Yenza izinto zokubala olusha.
2. Khetha {{ui:Amanani}}. Ngaphansi kwe-{{ui:Lokhu kubalo}} ubona yonke into enentengo okhunjuliwe, okuyo manje kuyo, amazinga okubala kokugcina, amazinga ajwayelekile (phakathi kwemisebenzi ekhunjuliwe) nobubanzi.
3. Khetha {{ui:Sebenzisa okokugcina}} noma {{ui:Sebenzisa ojwayelekile}} kwento eyodwa, noma {{ui:Bela izinto ezingu-N ezingenantengo kusuka enkumbulweni}} ukuze ugcwalise zonke izinto ezingenayo intengo kusuka kubalo lokugcina.

Isinyathelo ngasinye siyisinyathelo esisodwa: {{key:Ctrl+Z}} siyasibuyisela emuva. Igama elifana nalo kuphela (igama elilodwa lingaphakathi kwelinye) liyakubonisa, futhi izinkinobho zisebenzisa izintengo zegama elifana nalo. Intengo ekhunjulwe ngayinye ayikaze ibekwe kwento ngefidi: ingxoxo ithi iyunithi ahlukile futhi iyishiya.

## Bona, shintsha, khohlwa

{{ui:Konke okugcinwe enkumbulweni}} ludweba wonke amagama namazinga nenani lemisebenzi. Inkinobho yemfucuza ikhohlwa igama elilodwa; {{ui:Sula wonke amanani}} ikhohlwa lonke. Vala {{ui:Khumbula amanani ami lapho ngigcina ibalo}} ukuze uyeke ukukhumbula; okusele kuhlala kuze kube yilapho ukukhohlwa. Okubalwa kwakho nomtapo wezintengo akukaze kuthintwe.

## Nomsizi wakho

Uma uvumele abasizi ezilungiselelweni, bangafunda okukhunjuliwe ngezinto zokubala (prices_list) futhi banikeze izinto intengo kusuka kukho (prices_apply, engabonisa kuqala). Iluhlu olufanayo naledayalogi.
