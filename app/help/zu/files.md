## Okuvulayo

| Ifayela | Indlela | Amanothi |
|---|---|---|
| PDF (`.pdf`) | {{ui:Open plans}}, {{key:Mod+O}}, noma uyidonse ekhasini | Eziningi ngesikhathi esisodwa. Ikhasi ngalinye liba yishidi. |
| Iphrojekthi yokubala amanani (`.takeoff.json`) | {{ui:Open takeoff}}, noma uyidonse ekhasini | Izinto zakho, izikali namakwe. Ungayidonsa kanye nama-PDF ayo. |

Amanye amafayela adonselwe ekhasini ayadlulwa, futhi ibha yesimo iwabiza ngamagama.

- **Ama-PDF avikelwe ngephasiwedi** awavuleki: "(igama) livikelwe ngephasiwedi. Vula ikhophi engavikelwe."
- **I-PDF efanayo kabili** ivulwa kanye: "(igama) livulekile vele." I-TakeoffCraft iwazi amafayela ngokuqukethwe kwawo, hhayi ngamagama awo.
- **Ama-PDF askeniwe** avulwa futhi ahlolwe njengawanoma yiziphi ezinye; i-TakeoffCraft ayidingi umbhalo ongaphakathi kwe-PDF.
- **Amasethi amakhulu amapulani**: yonke i-PDF ifundwa iphelele lapho ivulwa (ukuze kuthathwe isigxivizo sayo), ngakho ifayela elikhulu kakhulu lithatha isikhashana. Izithombe ezincane zidwetshwa ngodwa njengoba usuka phansi.

## Okulondolozayo

Ukulondoloza ngakunye kuba ukulanda. Akukho okubhalwa emuva kuma-PDF akho.

| Okukhethayo | Uyathola | Okukuwo |
|---|---|---|
| {{ui:Save takeoff}} | `(igama).takeoff.json` | Izinto, izinhlanganisela, amarethi, izikali zamashidi nawo wonke amakwe, ahlobene nePDF ngayinye ngokuqukethwe kwayo. Akukho amakhasi e-PDF. |
| {{ui:XLSX}} | `(igama).xlsx` | Amashidi amane wokusebenza: Isilinganiso (namafomula aphilayo), Ngeshidi, Amakwe, Izikali. |
| {{ui:CSV}} | `(igama).csv` | Isilinganiso njengamanani avamile, umugqa ngamunye wento nengxenye ngayinye. |
| {{ui:Marked PDF}} | `(igama le-PDF) (takeoff).pdf` | Ikhophi ye-PDF ngayinye evuliwe enamakwe, nokubalwa kwayo kudwetshwe ezikhashini zayo nencwadi yencazelo. Ifayela elilodwa nge-PDF ngayinye. |

Igama litholakala ku-PDF yokuqala oyivule kulokubala. Akukho bhokisi lokuqamba kabusha okwamanje: qamba kabusha ifayela elilandiwe uma ufuna elinye igama.

Bona [Thumela isilinganiso nePDF emakwe](topic:export-estimate) kanye [Amakholomu okuthumela](topic:export-columns).

## Lapho okulandelwa khona

Isiphequluli sakho silondoloza ukulanda kuleyo ndawo esilondoloza kuyo konke ukulanda, ngokuvamile kufolda ye-Downloads. Thola kuma-{{pick:mac=Finder|windows=File Explorer|linux=isiphathi-mafayela sakho}}. Uma isiphequluli sakho sisetelwe ukubuza ukuthi ifayela ngalinye lilondolozwe kuphi, siyabuza.

:::chromium
{{ui:Marked PDF}} nama-PDF amaningi amakwe ilanda amafayela amaningi ngesikhathi esisodwa. I-Chrome ne-Edge zingabuza kanye ukuthi lesi sayithi sivunyelwe yini ukulanda amafayela amaningi. Khetha Vumela.
:::

:::safari
I-Safari ilondoloza ku-Downloads ngaphandle uma ushintshe indawo yokulanda ku-Izilungiselelo ze-Safari, ngaphansi kwe-Okujwayelekile.
:::

## Kungani iphrojekthi nama-PDF kuhlukene

Ifayela le-`.takeoff.json` liqukatha ukubalwa kwakho kuphela. Lithola ama-PDF alo futhi ngokuqukethwe kwawo (isigxivizo sawo ngamunye), ngakho:

- Ukuqamba kabusha noma ukuhambisa i-PDF akukhathalekile.
- I-PDF eshintshiwe noma elondolozwe kabusha yinye uhlelo ibalwa njengefayela elihlukile, noma ngabe linegama elifanayo.
- Amakwe ku-PDF ezingavulwanga aqhubeka ebalwa ezinhlanganisweni.

Bona [Londoloza bese uvula kabusha ukubala amanani](topic:save-and-reopen).

## Ku-inthanethi nangaphandle kwayo

Ama-PDF akho nokubalwa kwakho akusoze kushiye ikhompyutha yakho, ku-inthanethi nangaphandle kwayo. Ukuxhumana kubaluleka kuphela ekulayisheni i-TakeoffCraft ngokwayo.

Ukuze usebenze ngaphandle kokuxhumana, londoloza uhlelo kuqala, uma usuxhunyiwe:

1. Khetha {{ui:Save for offline}} ebhareni yohlelo phezulu.
   Inkinobho iyabala, "Iyalondoloza 12 kwangu-40", ngesikhathi isonga.
2. Linda {{ui:Offline ready}}.

Ukulondoloza i-TakeoffCraft kugcina uhlelo, lesi siqondiso, isifundi se-PDF, umbhali we-PDF osetshenziswa ngu-{{ui:Marked PDF}} nesisebenzi se-{{ui:Auto count}} esisesiphequluluni sakho, cishe ngo-6 MB. Uma kuthi {{ui:Offline ready}}, konke kuyasebenza ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi: ukuvula ama-PDF, ukukala, {{ui:Auto count}}, {{ui:Save takeoff}}, {{ui:XLSX}}, {{ui:CSV}} kanye no-{{ui:Marked PDF}}.

| Inkinobho ithi | Okusho |
|---|---|
| {{ui:Save for offline}} | Akukalondolozwa kulesi siphequluli. Yikhethe ukuze ulondoloze. |
| Iyalondoloza (n) kwangu-(m) | Iyalondoloza manje. |
| {{ui:Offline ready}} | Ilondoloziwe. Yikhethe ukuze wenze {{ui:Save again (get the newest version)}} noma {{ui:Remove the offline copy}}. |
| {{ui:Could not save}} | Amanye amafayela awalandwanga. Hlola ukuxhumana kwakho bese uyikhetha futhi. |
| {{ui:Offline not available}} | Lo window wesiphequluli awukwazi ukugcina ikhophi engaphandle kwe-inthanethi (amawindow angasese namanye amakhompyutha alawulwayo ayivimbela). |

Okwengeziwe, kuhlanganise nokuthi ikhophi elondoliziwe ihlala isikhathi esingakanani kwisiphequluli ngasinye: [Ukusebenza ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi](common:offline).

:::offline
Awusaxhunyiwe. Uma ibha yohlelo ibonisa {{ui:Offline ready}}, konke kuyasebenza. Uma kungabonisi, izingxenye ze-TakeoffCraft ezingakalayishwa kuleli thebhu (umbhali we-PDF we-{{ui:Marked PDF}}, isisebenzi se-auto count) azikwazi ukulayisha manje, futhi i-{{ui:Marked PDF}} imisa ngo-"Akukwazanga ukubhala i-PDF emakwe". Ukubala kwakho kuphephile noma kunjalo: {{ui:Save takeoff}}, {{ui:XLSX}} no-{{ui:CSV}} akudingi kuxhumana. Ungaphindi uvule kabusha ithebhu kuze usuxhunyiwe futhi.
:::

:::online
Usuxhunyiwe. Ngaphambi kokuvakasha esizeni noma ukundiza, khetha {{ui:Save for offline}} beseulinda {{ui:Offline ready}}.
:::

## Izipredishithi

Ifayela le-XLSX ligcina namafomula aphilayo, ngakho uhlelo lwezipredishithi lubala kabusha isilinganiso lapho livula ifayela, futhi lilungisa uma ushintsha izinga noma ukulahleka lapho. Incwadi ihlolwe ku-LibreOffice Calc, eyabala futhi yaphinda yabala onke amasamba ngesenti elifanayo. Ayikaze ivulwe ku-Excel okwamanje.

Ifayela le-CSV linamanani avamile nebhayithi yomakhi (byte order mark), ngakho izinhlelo zezipredishithi zifunda izinhlamvu ezifana no-m² ngendlela efanele.

## Idesktop nelifu

:::desktop
Kusukela ku-0.5, uhlelo lwedesktop yi-CadCraft Studio: i-TakeoffCraft namanye ama-app ayisikhombisa ku-install eyodwa. Akukho i-installer ehlukile ye-TakeoffCraft.
:::

Ukulondoloza kwelifu akutholakali okwamanje. Gcina amafayela wakho `.takeoff.json` ndawonye nama-PDF omsebenzi, futhi wenze ikhophi yawo njengefayela lanoma yiluphi olunye. Bona [Lapho amafayela akho aya khona](common:your-files).
