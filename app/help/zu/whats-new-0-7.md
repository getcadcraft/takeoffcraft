Lokhu yikho okushintshe ku-TakeoffCraft 0.7, uma kuqhathaniswa ne-0.6. Imidwebo manje ivulwa njengoba ibe, ngaphandle kokuyiphrinta kuqala ibe yi-PDF, futhi iphakheji yethenda ingathunyelwa kubasebenzi besabelomsebenzi ibuyele inexabiso.

## Okusha

### Imidwebo ye-DWG ne-DXF

- **Vula umdwebo njengamashidi.** {{ui:Vula amapulani}} kweqembu le-{{ui:Ifayela}} ({{key:Mod+O}}, noma wehlise amafayela) ithatha imidwebo ye-DWG ne-DXF eduze kwama-PDF. Imodeli namalayisheni ngamunye kuba yimashidi kuhlu lwe-{{ui:Amashidi}}, futhi ukubala kungafaka imidwebo namaPDF ndawonye. Ayikho into elayishwa. Bona [Vula umdwebo we-DWG noma i-DXF](topic:open-a-drawing).
- **Isilinganiso sivela emdwebeni.** Ilayisheni iqala ngesilinganiso sevikhwende yalo elikhulu kakhulu, ibizwe njengepresethi uma kunjalo, njengo-1/2" = 1'-0". Imodeli iqala ngamayunithi omdwebo. Umdwebo ongenamayunithi uthi "No scale" size ulinganise. {{ui:Linganisa}}, {{ui:Hlola}} ne-{{ui:Okusethiwe ngaphambili}} sekusebenza kuqala. Bona [Isilinganiso](topic:open-a-drawing#the-scale).
- **Izingqimba nemidwebo exhunyiwe.** {{ui:Ongqimba}} ebhareni leshidi ikhombisa noma ifihle izingqimba zomdwebo. Izingqimba ifayela elizicime noma elizimise ziqala zifihliwe futhi azibalwa ngaphandle uma ukhetha. Umdwebo oxhunyiwe (xref) ovulwe eduze komdwebo osingathayo udwetshwa futhi ubalwe lapho uxhunywe khona; uma ungakavulwa, ibha yesimo itshela ukuthi yini engekho. Bona [Izingqimba](topic:open-a-drawing#layers).
- **I-PDF emakwe isebenza emdwebeni.** {{ui:Thumela}} › {{ui:Marked PDF}} idweba amashidi emakwe omdwebo njenge-PDF kuqala, ngakho ikhophi likhombisa izimaki zakho nencwadi yezimpawu ngaphandle kwesinyathelo sokuphrinta. Idwetshwa ngomnyama noma ngemibala yomdwebo, ngaphandle kwamathebhulu esitayela sokuphrinta. Bona [I-PDF emakwe](topic:export-estimate#a-marked-pdf).

### Izinani nobude obunembile kusuka emdwebeni

- **Bala lokho umdwebo okutshela ukuthi kukhona.** {{ui:Ukubala kwe-CAD}} ebhareni leshidi ibala ibhulokhi ngegama lalo (kanye namabhulokhi agxiwe, izimo zebhulokhi eziguquguqukayo namakhredi webhulokhi), konke kuhlobo olulodwa esingqimbeni, noma umbhalo noma inani lesici. Ibala lokho ibhodlela leshidi noma umdwebo wonke kanye. Ukungeza izimaki kuyisinyathelo sokuhlehlisa esisodwa, nokubala futhi kwengeza okusha kuphela. {{ui:Auto count}} ihlala kuma-PDF nakuzimpawu ezingesizo amabhulokhi. Bona [Bala kusukela kumdwebo](topic:count-from-a-drawing).
- **Linganisa kusukela emigqeni yomdwebo.** {{ui:Linganisa umdwebo}} uthatha imigqa, ama-arc, ama-polyline, amasongo, izigaba zokufiphaza namasolidi oyawachofozayo noma oyawafaka iwindi njengezimaki zento yomugqa noma yendawo. Ama-arc, ama-bulge nezimbobo zesigaba zifika zinembile; ama-ellipse namacala ahlanganisiwe alinganiswa ngezingxenye ezincane, futhi ibha iyakutshela. Bona [Linganisa kusukela kumdwebo](topic:measure-from-a-drawing).

### Amathenda

- **Sebenzisa ithenda lomsebenzisi njengexabiso lephakheji.** Ku-{{ui:Thumela}} › {{ui:Bid packages and leveling…}}, khetha {{ui:Sebenzisa}} kwithenda. Izinto zephakheji zihlala njengezinani, umugqa **Chosen bid** uthwala ixabiso, izinto ithenda elizishiya ngaphandle zihlala ngezindleko zakho, futhi ukuphathwa, inzuzo kanye nebhondi kusebenza kuyo. Ukushintsha amathenda kuyisinyathelo sokuhlehlisa esisodwa; incwadi yomsebenzi nesifinyezo sethenda siyithwala. Bona [Sebenzisa ithenda njengexabiso lephakheji](topic:bid-leveling#use-a-bid-as-the-packages-price).
- **Thumela ephakheji ukuba kuthathwe amathenda.** {{ui:Thumela amathenda…}} ngaphansi kwephakheji ithumela kumsebenzisi ngamunye wesabelomsebenzi ixhumanisi lekhasi elingadingi i-akhawunti: babona isikophu namashidi njenge-PDF, bafake ixabiso kumugqa ngamunye noma babhale ukuthi akufakiwe, bangeza amanothi namafayela amathathu kuya kwangaphezulu, noma banqabe. {{ui:Thola amanani}} idonsa lokho okubuye kwaleli leveli, iphawulwe **web**. Ukuthumela kuvulekele kuma-akhawunti aqinisekiswe yi-CadCraft team kuphela ngenkathi ihlolwa (bona ngezansi). Bona [Thumela ephakheji ukuba kuthathwe amathenda](topic:bid-invitations).
- **Kufihliwe kuze kuvulwe.** Amanani angahlala efihliwe kuze kufike isikhathi sokuvula osisethayo: kuze kufike, akekho obona ixabiso, nawe uqobo. Ekuvulweni wonke ithenda liyabonakala ngasikhathi sinye, futhi ithenda elifike ngemva kwesikhathi lichazwa njengelibhalwe sekwephuzile. Bona [Amathenda afihliwe nokuvulwa](topic:bid-invitations#sealed-bids-and-the-opening).
- **Amarekhodi wesabelomsebenzi.** I-akhawunti yakho ku-getcadcraft.com igcina oxhumana nabo bayo ngayinye, izinhlobo zomsebenzi, impendulo zokuqinisekisa, nezinsuku zeinshurensi nelayisensi (zifakwa uphawu lapho zidlule noma zisesikhathini esingamasonto angu-30), kanye namathenda abo adlule. Ziyangeniswa nokuphuma njenge-CSV, futhi uyazikhetha uma uthumela ephakheji. Bona [Kwiwebhusayithi](topic:bid-invitations#on-the-website).

### Abasizi be-AI

- **Abasizi bangangeza ekubalweni.** Uma i-**Edit** ithikiwe, umsizi wakho ungangeza izinto (`items_add`) nokulinganisa imigqa, izindawo nezinani eshidini (`marks_add`; ubude nezindawo kuphela eshidini elinesilinganiso), ngokubiza ngakunye kuyisinyathelo sokuhlehlisa esisodwa.
- **Amathuluzi omsizi emidwebweni.** Ku-DWG noma i-DXF evulekile umsizi angabala amashidi awo nokubalwa kwawo, abale ngokunembile, engeze izimaki zokubala, futhi alinganise kusukela ejiyomethri yomdwebo. Ngaphandle kokuvula i-TakeoffCraft, i-CadCraft MCP server ivula umdwebo njengokubala okusha futhi ayilondoloze, futhi i-`cadcraft count` ibala kumugqa womyalo. Bona [Okungacelwa umsizi](topic:use-with-ai-assistants#what-an-assistant-can-ask-for).

### USD

- **Ukubuka kokuhlola kwe-3D njenge-USD.** {{ui:Iphakheji ye-USD (.usdz)}} eduze kwe-{{ui:GLB}} ku-3D check view ibhala ngayinye yodonga, ukhonkolo, ipuleti, insika yokubala nombhalo njengento yayo, ibizwe ngegama lento nethwele inani layo, ngamamitha. Umsizi nawo angayithumela (`usd_export`). Bona [Thumela ukubuka kokuhlola kwe-3D njenge-USD](topic:usd).

## Kuwo wonke ama-app

- **Amayunithi.** {{ui:Settings}} › {{ui:General}} › {{ui:Units}} inohlu olulodwa lobude, indawo, ivolumu, i-engeli nokuthambeka, nosayizi abanembile abavela ezichazweni zamayunithi (i-US survey foot iyiyunithi yayo). Vala amayunithi ongawasebenzisi nala awasuka ohlwini; uwenze wangena njengensimbi, futhi umsebenzi omusha uqala kuwo. Ifayela ligcina iyunithi yalo ngokwalo. Ku-TakeoffCraft uhlu lwe-{{ui:Unit}} lwento luyalandela iswishi, into entsha iqala ngobude obunenkanyezi noma indawo, ama-roofing squares (SQ), ama-acre namahektha ayizindawo entsha, futhi ibhokisi lobude lamukela noma iyiphi uphawu lweyunithi, njengo-`3 yd`. Bona [Amayunithi, izinani ozibhalayo nohlu lwamayunithi](common:unit-lists).
- **Ukwakheka okuqinile namaphaneli azicwebe.** {{ui:Settings}} › {{ui:General}} › {{ui:Layout density}} › {{ui:Compact}} ilwanele okwengeziwe esikrinini (izikrini zokuthinta nama-foni zigcina izinkinobho zazo ezinkulu). Iphaneli ngalinye eliseceleni linebha yesihloko elincane: yidonse ukuze uyifake noma uyiyeke ilula, uyilahle ebhareni lephaneli elinye ukuze wabelane ngerafu eyodwa njengamathebhu, uyibophe ukuze ishelele ngasemphethelweni, noma uyifihle bese uyibuyisa emphethelweni. Bona [Hlela izakhiwo zamaphaneli](common:arrange-panels#make-the-layout-denser).
- **Izingxoxo ziyahamba.** Donsa ingxoxo ngebha yayo yesihloko iphume endleleni yomdwebo; ibuyela lapho wayishiya khona. Chofoza kabili ibha yesihloko ukuze uyibeke phakathi futhi. Bona [Hambisa ingxoxo](common:arrange-panels#move-a-dialog).
- **Izindlela zabasizi be-AI.** Izicelo eziyishumi ezisebenziwe zomsizi wakho (hlanza umdwebo, phrinta isethi, hlola imidwebo, buza imodeli imibuzo, bala ukubala okuphelele, zama inguqulo yengxenye…), ngayinye nethuluzi eliyibizayo nezimvume ezidingekayo, futhi ngayinye iphindwa yizivivinyo zethu. Uhlelo alunayo i-AI. Bona [Izindlela zabasizi be-AI](common:assistants-recipes).
- **Amalimi.** Ku-{{ui:Settings}} › {{ui:General}} › {{ui:Language}} kukhona okusha i-**Português (Portugal)**. Yona, i-**Português (Brasil)**, i-**Español (México)** ne-**Bahasa Indonesia** manje zinawo wonke amagama ama-app, njengamadrafti angakabuyekezwa umuntu wendabuko. Izihloko zosizo zisesiNgisi.
- **I-CadCraft Studio** ichaza ukuthi kungani ukuhlola isibuyekezo kuhlulekile (akukho fayela lesistimu yakho okwamanje, akukho inethiwekhi, isiginecha esingalungile) esikhundleni sokuthi "Done" nje, futhi ayiphindi ikhadi elisha elivaliwe size kube nenguqulo entsha ekhishiwe.

## Akukakwenziwa kule nguqulo

- **0.7 ayisayinwanga yikhodi.** Ukusayina, nephakheji yeMicrosoft Store, kwadluliselwa ku-0.8 ngoba ukuqinisekiswa kwesitifiketi akukafiki. Hlola i-SHA-256 eduze kwefayela ngalinye ku-getcadcraft.com/download. Ku-Windows, uma i-SmartScreen imisa isifakeli, khetha {{ui:More info}}, bese {{ui:Run anyway}}. Ku-Mac, okokuqala, chofoza ngakwesokudla i-CadCraft Studio bese ukhetha {{ui:Open}}. I-CadCraft Studio asikakabi esitolo sama-app, futhi asikakabi bukhona kwe-iPad noma i-iPhone.
- Uhlu lwe-engeli nokuthambeka ku-{{ui:Units}} lugciniwe, kodwa abukho bhokisi elikufundayo okwamanje.
- **Izimemo zamathenda zicimiwe kwabaningi bama-akhawunti.** Ngenkathi isici sihlolwa, i-imeyili eya kubaqashi bangaphandle iphuma kuma-akhawunti aqinisekiswe yi-CadCraft team kuphela. Amakhasi ababhidi asebenza kuzo zonke izimemo ezisevele zithunyelwe. Abanye bonke: landa i-{{ui:Scope CSV}}, uyithumele nge-imeyili ngokwakho, bese ufaka amathenda ngesandla.
- Iwindi le-CadCraft Studio alikwazi ukuthumela izimemo zamathenda okwamanje: lisho ukuthi usebenzise i-TakeoffCraft kusiphequluli. Ukuvula ukubala okunezicelo ezithunyelwe, ukuhlanganisa nokukhetha ithenda kusebenza ku-Studio.
- Izengezo, izincwadi zokunikezwa, ishidi lesikophu elisayinwa yi-bidder ngamunye nezikhumbuzo ezizenzakalelayo ngaphambi kokuphela kwesikhathi seinshurensi azakhiwanga. Ithenda elikhethiwe alishintshi ixabiso lendawo ye-alternate.
- Imidwebo: amathebhulu esitayela sokuphrinta namafayela e-CTB ayingenwa, i-DXF yebhayinari iyenqatshwa (yigcine njenge-DXF ye-ASCII noma i-DWG), imidwebo exhunyiwe itholwa kuphela phakathi kwamafayela avulekile ngegama lefayela, futhi ama-PDF angaphansi, izithombe namathangi e-OLE emdwebeni ayadwetshwa noma abalwa. Izinani nezilinganiso zihlolwe kumidwebo yesampula, hhayi okwamanje emidwebweni esuka emsebenzini wangempela evulwe kuhlelo olwayidala.
- Umsizi ungangeza izinto nezimaki kodwa awukwazi ukuhambisa, ukushintsha noma ukususa izimaki ezikhona ngaphambili.
