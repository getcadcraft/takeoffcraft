I-TakeoffCraft iba ngcono ngokuthi usebenza kakhulu ngayo. Ifunda **kuphela kulokho okwamukelayo**:

- izimpawu owaziboxe nge-[Ukubala okuzenzakalelayo](topic:auto-count) bese uzengeza,
- amasethi wezimo ozigcinayo,
- izinhlanganisela ozisebenzisayo (kanye ngomsebenzi),
- amaphethini wezimaki ozipheyo kuwo wonke amakhasi.

Kuwukufanisa okulula kumashidi akho, okufana nokusesha okusetshenziswa yi-Ukubala okuzenzakalelayo. Akuyona imodeli eqeqeshiwe. Konke ayifundayo kugcinwa **kulelifayela lalokhu kubalwa** (`.takeoff.json`), futhi akukho lutho olithunyelwa noma kuphi. Ukuyithatha iye emsebenzini olandelayo, sebenzisa [Njengomsebenzi wokugcina](topic:what-it-learned#same-as-last-job).

Khetha {{ui:Learned}} ku-toolbar (isibani, eduze kwe-{{ui:Auto count}}) ukuze ubone konke.

## Izimpawu

Uma uboxa isimpawu nge-{{ui:Auto count}} bese ukhetha {{ui:Add}}, isimpawu osiboxile siyagcinwa, nezinga lokufanisa owalamukela.

- Ku-{{ui:Learned}}, khetha {{ui:Find on this sheet}} noma {{ui:Find on every sheet}}. Ukubuyekeza kwe-Ukubala okuzenzakalelayo okujwayelekile kuyavulwa: chofoza ibhokisi ukuze uyikhiphe, hambisa isilayidi, bese uzengeza. Akukho okungezwa size wenze lokho.
- Uma uvula ishidi elingenalo isimpawu esifundiwe, ibha esiphezulu inikeza {{ui:Find …}} salo. {{ui:Not now}} iyawufihla lelo shidi.
- Uma ukubala kungenaso isinto segama elifanayo, ukwengeza izimaki kwengeza nento (isinyathelo sokuhlehlisa esisodwa).
- Ukungeza futhi isimpawu sento efanayo kubalwa kulowo esawaziyo; isimpawu esibukeka ngenye indlela sigcinwa njengesesibili.
- Njenge-Auto count, ifumana isimpawu ngama-engeli alungile nasemashidini esilinganiso esihlukile.

## Amasethi wezimo

Isethi igcina izimo zokubalwa (izinto namayunithi, amazinga, ukulahlekelwa nezinhlanganisela), ngaphandle kwezimaki.

1. Ku-{{ui:Learned}}, khetha {{ui:Save this takeoff's conditions as a set…}} bese unikeza igama. Isethi enegama elifanayo iyashintshwa.
2. Ekubalweni okunesethi, {{ui:Add}} ifaka izimo ezingekho kuyo. Izimo ezinegama nohlobo olufanayo ziyashiywa ngaphandle kokuthintwa. Kuyisinyathelo sokuhlehlisa esisodwa.

## Njengomsebenzi wokugcina

Ifayela lomsebenzi wokugcina liqukethe lokho okufundile. Ukuqala umsebenzi omusha kusuka kulo:

1. Vula amapulani amasha, bese ukhetha {{ui:Learned}} › {{ui:Same as last job…}}.
2. Khetha i-`.takeoff.json` yomsebenzi wokugcina.
3. Thika lokho okufanele kuletwe: izimo zawo (ezo kuphela ezingekho kulokhu kubalwa), nalokho okufundile (izimpawu, amasethi, izinhlanganisela, amaphethini). Bese ukhetha {{ui:Bring them in}}.

Izimo zawo zigcinwa njengesethi equmbethe igama lalowo msebenzi. Izimaki nezinani zawo zihlala kwifayela lawo. Konke kuyisinyathelo sokuhlehlisa esisodwa.

## Izinhlanganisela ozisebenzisa kakhulu

Into enenhlanganisela ibalwa njengesetshenziswe kanye ngomsebenzi lapho inenani futhi ulondoloza ukubala. Umsebenzi uyisethi yamafayela eplani okubalwa kuwo, ngakho ukulondoloza kabusha lokubala kuvele akubaliwe kabili. Uhlu oluphansi {{ui:Assemblies you use most}} ibeka ezisetshenziswa kakhulu phambili. Lukhipha ezikhona kakade kulokhu kubalwa. {{ui:Add}} ifaka eyodwa njengoba uyisebenzise ekugcineni, nazo zonke izingxenye zayo.

## Phinda kuwo wonke amakhasi

Kwezimaki ezifanayo kuwo wonke amakhasi esethi, njengenani lebhulokhi yesihloko noma umbhalo ojwayelekile:

1. Khetha izimaki eshidini elilodwa ([Khetha izimaki](topic:copy-paste#select-the-marks)).
2. Ku-{{ui:Learned}}, khetha {{ui:Repeat the selected marks on every page…}}, bese uqamba ipethini bese uqinisekisa.

Zihamba kuwo wonke amanye amakhasi avulekile **anosayizi ofanayo**, endaweni efanayo, ngesinyathelo esisodwa sokuhlehlisa. Ubude nezindawo zihamba kuphela emakhasini anesilinganiso esifanayo; izinani zihamba kunoma yiliphi ikhasi lalolo sayizi. Ikhasi elinazo sele liyadlulwa. Ipethini iyagcinwa, ngakho amakhasi ovula kamuva athola isiphakamiso ebhendeni, noma usebenzise {{ui:Repeat on every page}} ohlwini.

## Bheka, susa

Konke okufundiwe kudweshwe ku-{{ui:Learned}}. Inkomba isusa into eyodwa. {{ui:Clear all}} isusa konke okufundiwe futhi ishiye izinto nezimaki zakho zisendaweni. Womabili angahlehliswa kuze uvale ukubala, futhi womabili agcinwa nokubala.

Ukuze uyeke ukufunda kokubalwa okukodwa, susa ukukhethwa kwe-{{ui:Learn from this takeoff}}. Akukho okusha okufundwayo, kanti okudweshiwe kuhlala kuze ukususe.

## Okufanele uyazi

- Izinguqulo zangaphambili ze-TakeoffCraft zivula ifayela elinento efundiwe ngokujwayelekile. Zinganaki lolo lolwazi, futhi uma zilondoloza ifayela futhi, ziyalususa.
- Isimpawu esifundiwe siyisithombe esincane sento oyiboxile, esigcinwa kulelifayela (ama-KB ambalwa ngamunye).
- Izimpawu ezifundiwe ziyasesha njengoba i-Auto count isesha, ngakho imikhawulo efanayo iyasebenza: [Ukubala isimpawu ngokuzenzakalelayo](topic:auto-count).
