Uhlu {{ui:Preset}} eqenjini le-{{ui:Scale}} linezikali ezingu-32 ezinamaqembu amathathu. Okusethiwe ngaphambili kusetha isikali seshidi elivulekile ngokunembile.

Yonke into esethiwe ngaphambili ithatha ukuthi ikhasi le-PDF yisayizi yephepha yangempela yedrowing: iyinshi eyodwa ekhasini le-PDF iyinshi eyodwa yephepha (amakhasi e-PDF alinganiswa ngamaphuwenti angu-72 ngeyinshi). Uma i-PDF ishintshiwe usayizi, sebenzisa {{ui:Calibrate}}. Sebenzisa {{ui:Check}} emva kokusetha noma yisiphi isikali. Bona [Setha isikali](topic:set-the-scale).

## Imethrikhi

`1:1`, `1:2`, `1:5`, `1:10`, `1:20`, `1:25`, `1:50`, `1:75`, `1:100`, `1:150`, `1:200`, `1:250`, `1:500`, `1:1000`.

Isilinganiso semethrikhi kusho ukuthi iyunithi eyodwa ephepheni ingamayunithi amaningi angempela: ngo-`1:100`, i-10 mm ekhasini ingu-1 m.

## Izikali ezilinganisiwe

Isikali esilinganisiwe sivela njenge "Calibrated 1" = 4.002' on 24'-6"" embhalweni ongaphansi kweshidi: amafiti angempela ngenshi eyodwa yephepha, nobude obuthayiphile. Uma sisezinhlamvu ezingu-0.5% kokusethiwe ngaphambili, ibha yengeza "(matches 1/4" = 1'-0")" futhi uhlu lwamashidi lubonisa igama lokusethiwe ngaphambili.

## Ama-PDF angavamile

Amanye ama-PDF amemezela iyunithi yekhasi elikhulu (i-"user unit"); i-TakeoffCraft iyayicabangela kokusethiwe ngaphambili nakwesikali esibonisiwe. Amakhasi ajikijelwe ku-PDF alinganiswa ngendlela efanayo nangawamakhasi aqondile.
