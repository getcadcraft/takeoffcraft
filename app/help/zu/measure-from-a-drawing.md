{{ui:Measure drawing}} ebhareni yeshidi ye-DWG noma ishidi le-DXF ithatha izinto zomdwebo njengemaki, ngakho awuzilandelelisi ngokucofa. Ubude be-polyline, ubude bejika, indawo yesiyingi kanye nendawo ye-hatch nezimbobo zayo kuphuma eqondile, ngezibalo ezisetshenziswa ukubala yonke imaki.

## Thatha izinto njengento yomugqa noma yendawo

1. Yenza noma cofa into **yomugqa** noma **yendawo** ngaphansi kwe-{{ui:Items}}. Ishidi kudingeka isikali.
2. Khetha {{ui:Measure drawing}}.
3. Cofa izinto esheetini ukuze uzikhethe, noma donsa iwindi ozungeze kuyo: konke okuphelele ngaphakathi kwawo kukhethiwe. {{key:Shift}} yengeza iwindi ekukhethweni; cofa into ekhethiwe futhi ukuze uyisuse. Ibha ibonisa inani eliphelele: "imigqa emi-6: 108'-9 1/8" ngokuphelele."
4. Khetha inkinobho yokungeza, ebiza inani nento, njengo {{ui:Add 6 to Edge}} (noma cindezela {{key:Enter}}). Yonke into iba yimaki eyodwa yento, ngesinyathelo sokuhlehlisa esisodwa. {{key:Esc}} ihlanza lokho okukhethiwe.

## Yini into ngayinye ethatha kuyo

- Into **yomugqa** ithatha imigqa, amajika, ama-polyline, amasongo nama-spline ngobude. Umumo ovaliwe ubalwa ngomjikelezo wawo. I-hatch iyasuswa: inendawo, hhayi ubude, futhi ibha iyasho lokho.
- Into **yendawo** ithatha imumo evaliwe: ama-polyline avaliwe, amasongo, ama-2D solid, namahatch anemibobo yawo. Imigqa evulekile iyasuswa, futhi ibha iyasho lokho.

## Okunembile nokuseduze

Imigqa, amajika (kuhlanganise nawo wonke ama-bulge), ama-polyline anama-bulge namasongo kunembile: ubude nezindawo zisebenzisa irediyasi nokugoba kwejika, hhayi isithombe salo. Ama-ellipse, ama-spline namahatch anemiphetho eyakhiwe ngamaphethelo amelwa ngamachord angu-degree eyodwa noma ngaphansi kwalokho, okuyisilinganiso esiphakathi kwengxenye yekhulu lephesenti kuma-ellipse nengxenye yeshumi kokunye; ibha ithi zingaki zezinto ozikhethile ezingamajika alolu hlobo.

## Ithathwa kusuka kuphi

- **Model**: izinto ku-model space. **A layout**: imigqa yayo, kanye nezinto zemodeli ngaphakathi kwe-viewport, ziyalingiswa ngayo. Into edlula emphethelweni we-viewport ayinikezwa (iyaphrinta iqunyiwe); into ezungeze ama-viewport omabili ithathwa kanye.
- Izendlalelo ezibonwa yileshidi kuphela. Amabhulokhi abalwa, akalinganiswa: bona [Bala kusuka emdwebeni](topic:count-from-a-drawing). Ama-polyline ayi-3D, ama-mesh, ama-solid namadlelo awanikezwa.
