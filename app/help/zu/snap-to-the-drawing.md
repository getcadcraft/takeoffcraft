Amapulani amaningi yi-PDF ezivekthori: izindonga nemiphetho yazo imigqa yangempela, hhayi amaphikseli. Ngenkathi ulinganisa, i-TakeoffCraft ibambelela ukuchofoza kwakho kulowo migqa, ngakho umugqa uqala ngqo ekhoneni lodonga, futhi ukuhlola isikali kufunda isilinganiso umklami asidwebile, hhayi iphikseli oliliphindile.

{{ui:Bambelela}} iyavuleka lapho uqala. Ivulwa ivalwe ngo-{{ui:Bambelela}} kwibha yesimo noma nge-{{key:F3}}; isilungiselelo sigcinwa kulesi siphequluli (sitholakala futhi ku-[Izilungiselelo](topic:settings)).

## Okubambelelayo

Uma inkomba isondela cishe ngamaphikseli esikrini angu-10 komugqa, uphawu lubonisa lapho ukuchofoza kuzofika, negama lalo:

| Uphawu | Igama | Lapho |
|---|---|---|
| Isikwele | **Iphelo** | Iphelo lomugqa, noma ikhona lapho imigqa ihlangana. Amaphelo amabili ejwayezi ayabalwa; amaphoyinti angaphakathi awabalwa. |
| Unxantathu | **Ukuphakathi** | Maphakathi womugqa oqondile. |
| Isiphambano | **Ukuhlangana** | Lapho imigqa emibili ihlangana, noma lapho omunye uphela kolunye. |
| I-hourglass | **Emgqeni** | Iphoyinti eliseduze kakhulu emgqeni, uma kungekho iphelo, ukuphakathi noma ukuhlangana okuseduze. |

Iphelo, ukuphakathi noma ukuhlangana okuseduze kakhulu kuyanqoba. Kuphela uma kungekho lutho oluseduze, iphoyinti liyaya emgqeni uqobo. Sondeza ukuze ukhethe phakathi kwamaphoyinti ahlangene: amaphikseli angu-10 angamaphikseli esikrini, ngakho ngokusondeza kakhulu athatha ingxenye encane yephepha.

## Lapho isebenza khona

Ukubambelela kusebenza kumathuluzi {{ui:Umugqa}}, {{ui:Indawo}}, {{ui:Isiyingi}}, {{ui:Imbobo}}, {{ui:Linganisa}} no-{{ui:Hlola}}, nalapho uhudula ikhona yohlamvu olukhethiwe. Futhi ibeka amaphoyinti ama-arc. {{ui:Bala}}, {{ui:Ukubala okuzenzakalelayo}} nokunamathisela akubambelelwa.

Iphoyinti elibanjelelwe linqoba ukubambelela kwe-45° ngo-{{key:Shift}}: ukuze udwebe ku-45° ngaphandle komugqa, vala ukubambelela nge-{{key:F3}} kuqala.

:::tip
Linganisa ngokubambelela sekuvuliwe. Chofoza eduze kwamaphelo amabili womugqa wesilinganiso noma udonga oluwaziyo: amaphoyinti omabili angena ngokunembile emdwebweni, futhi isikali siphuma sinembile (ibha yesimo ibe isitshela ukuthi yiyiphi i-preset elingana nayo).
:::

## Lapho ingekwazi ukubambelela

- **Amashidi eskeniwe** ngumfanekiso ongenamigqa okungabanjelelwa kuwo. Ibha yesimo ithi "This sheet has no lines to snap to (a scanned page is a picture)." namaphoyinti aya lapho uchofoza khona.
- **Imidwebo emikhulu kakhulu**: ukubambelela kufunda kuze kube ngama-400,000 wezingxenye zomugqa ngeshidi ngalinye. Kishidi elinengaphezu kwalokho, kuyasho lokho futhi kubambelele kwezokuqala kuphela.
- **Umbhalo awubanjelelwa**, ngaphandle uma i-PDF idweba izinhlamvu zayo njengemigqa (ezinye izipuleti zenza kanjalo).

Imigqa yeshidi ifundwa ngokuqala ukulivula, ngasemuva; ishidi elikhulu lingathatha isekhondi ngaphambi kokuba amamaki avele.
