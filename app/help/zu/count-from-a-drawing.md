{{ui:Ukubala kwe-CAD}} ebhalweni leshidi (umyalo **Bala emdwebweni** kwezinye izitayela zemenyu) ibala okutholakala ku-DWG noma i-DXF. Lapho {{ui:Ukubala okuzenzakalelayo}} kufuna isithombe senkomba bese kungaphuthelwa noma kutholwe okungelona, lokhu kubala izinto: yonke ifakwa yebhulokhi enegama elithi `DOOR-36` ingumnyango oyodwa. Vula umdwebo kuqala: [Vula umdwebo we-DWG noma i-DXF](topic:open-a-drawing).

## Bala ibhulokhi

1. Vula ishidi lomdwebo. I-{{ui:Ukubala kwe-CAD}} isebhalweni leshidi.
2. Yikhethe. Iwindi ludweba amabhulokhi nokuthi kangaki kwelinye ngalinye. Sesha ngegama, bese ukhetha elilodwa.
3. Bheka umugqa ongaphansi kohlu: okungu-22 okutholakele, okungu-22 okuzongezwa.
4. Ngaphansi kwe-{{ui:Yengeza ku}}, khetha into yokubala, noma shiya {{ui:Impawu entsha yokubala}} bese unikeza igama.
5. Khetha inkinobho yokungeza, ebala inani: {{ui:Engeza izimpawu angu-22}}. Izimpawu zivela ezindaweni zokufakwa kwebhulokhi, futhi inani lento liyinani lokubala.

Yisinyathelo esisodwa sokuhlehlisa: {{key:Mod+Z}} isusa izimpawu (nento entsha) ndawonye. Isilinganiso sezindleko, ishidithi nei-PDF emakwe ziphatha into njengenye into yokubala.

### Okubalwayo

- **Yonke ifakwa yegama lebhulokhi**, noma ngasiphi isikali nanoma yiliphi i-engeli, kumodeli noma ephepheni. Amagama amabhulokhi awanandaba nezinhlamvu ezinkulu nezincane.
- **Uhlu lwamabhulokhi** (MINSERT): ikhophi ngayinye kuhlu iyodwa.
- **Amabhulokhi angaphakathi kwamanye.** Ibhulokhi elibekwe ngaphakathi kwelinye libalwa ezingeni ngalinye. Uhlu lusho ukuthi "6 ngaphakathi kwamanye amabhulokhi"; susa ukukhetha kwe-{{ui:Bala namabhulokhi angaphakathi kwamanye amabhulokhi futhi}} ukuze ubale kuphela okufakwe ngqo emdwebweni.
- **Amabhulokhi anamandla.** Ibhulokhi elinamandla libalwa ngegama lalo, hhayi ngamakhophi angenamagama (`*U12`) afayela ligcina. Izimo zalo zidwetshwa ngokwehlukana ("Single 12, Double 4"): khetha imo ukuze ubale lezo kuphela, noma {{ui:Yonke imisho}}. Uma ifayela lingasho ukuthi ikhophi ibonisa imo ephi, ziyadwetshwa njenge-"(imo engaziwa)".
- **Isihlungo songqimba.** Ibhulokhi emaqenjini amaningi: khetha elilodwa ngaphansi kwe-{{ui:Ongqimbeni}}.

### Okungabalwa

- **Ongqimba abafihliwe.** Into esendaweni evaliwe noma eqandekile kufayela (noma eyifihla ku-{{ui:Ongqimba}}) iyashiywa, nomugqa uthi ziningi kangakanani: "3 on hidden layers left out". Khetha {{ui:Bala nazo futhi}} ukuze uzifake.
- **Amafayela alinkiwe angavulwanga** awabalwa; iwindi liyatshela lokho.
- **Izinto ezibekwe ngqo phezu kwezinye zohlobo olufanayo** zibalwa njengezimbili (ziyizinto ezimbili), futhi iwindi liyatshela: ngokuvamile kuyiphutha lokudweba elifanele ukubhekwa.

## Yonke yalo shidi, noma umdwebo wonke

- **Lokho ishidi elibonisa** libala izinto ishidi elizibonisayo: ku-ilayawuti, izinto zemodeli ezingaphakathi kwamafasitela alo okubuka (ongqimba obanjiwe kufasitela akubalwa lapho) kanye nezakhiwo zeilayawuti ngokwazo.
- **Umdwebo wonke, into ngayinye kanye** ubala imodeli kanye, nezimpawu ku-ishidi lemodeli, futhi ayibali okubonwa izakhiwo eziningi kabusha.

Ukubala okubonwa yi-ilayawuti nokubala okubonwa yimodeli kunika inani elifanayo ngezinto ezifanayo; ungayengezi zombili kwento eyodwa.

## Bala ukubalwa futhi

Ukubala kabusha ibhulokhi elifanayo ngento efanayo kwengeza okusha kuphela: "Okungu-22 sekubalwe kwangaphambili." Impawu ngayinye imelela into eyodwa.

## Ngohlobo nongqimba

Ku-{{ui:Ngongqimba nohlobo}} khetha ongqimba nohlobo lwento (izindilinga, amaphola-layini, imigqa yokufiphaza…): "zonke izindilinga ku-E-LITE". Khetha {{ui:Bala futhi izinto ezingaphakathi kwamabhulokhi}} ukuze uphinde ufake lezo ezingaphakathi kwezakhiwo zamabhulokhi, endaweni ngayinye lapho ibhulokhi lifakwe khona.

## Ngombhalo

Ku-{{ui:Ngombhalo}} bhala umbhalo, njengo `A3`, bese uchaza ukuthi uphelele, uqukethe lokho, noma ungamagama aphelele. Umbhalo wezinto, umbhalo wokuhola kanye nezinani zesici sebhulokhi zonke ziyabalwa (isici sethegi `ITEM = A3` siyatholakala); khetha {{ui:Kwisici}} ukubala isici esisodwa kuphela. Imibhalo evame kakhulu ibalwa ukuze ichofozwe.

## Lapho kufanele usebenzise Ukubala okuzenzakalelayo

Sebenzisa {{ui:Ukubala okuzenzakalelayo}} kuma-PDF nasezimfanekisweni emdwebweni ezingewona amabhulokhi (ezidwetshwe zikhululekile, noma ezihlukaniswe). Ezidwetshwe njengamabhulokhi, zibale lapha.
