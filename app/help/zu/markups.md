Izimaki zezinto ofuna ukuzikhumbula noma ukuzibuza ngesikhathi uthatha amanani: "qinisekisa uhlobo lwesisekelo", ifu lokubuyekeza elizungeza udonga oshintshile, inothi eqaqanjiswe ipulani. Zihlala esheetini nesimo sazo, zidweywe ohlwini olulodwa, futhi zishicilelwe ku-PDF emakwe. **Azibalwa ngakho**: akukho nani, akukho zinxephezelo.

## Engeza eyodwa

Vula {{ui:Markup}} ebhareni ephezulu kwesheet bese ukhetha:

| Markup | How |
|---|---|
| {{ui:Note}} | Cofa lapho izofika khona, uthayiphe inothi, bese ukhetha {{ui:Add}}. Ibonakala njengephinikhodi ejikelezayo nomugqa wayo wokuqala ecaleni kwayo. |
| {{ui:Cloud}} | Cofa ikhona elilodwa, bese ukhetha elisha elicashile, uthayiphe inothi (noma ushiye lingenalutho). Ifu lokubuyekeza elinemiphetho ejikelezayo lizungeza ibhokisi. |
| {{ui:Highlight}} | Cofa amakhona amabili: ibhokisi eliphuzi elibonakalayo, nenothi uma ufisa. |

{{key:Esc}} emva kokhona lokuqala iyakhansela. Ithuluzi lihlala livulekile okulandelayo; {{key:V}} ibuyela ku-{{ui:Select}}.

## Isimo nokuhlela

Cofa imaki nge-{{ui:Select}} ukuze uyikhethe. Ipaneli yesokudla ibonisa **Markup**: uhlobo nesheet yayo, **Status** yayo — Vulekile, Iphendulwe noma Ivaliwe — kanye nombhalo wayo, ongawushintsha. {{key:Delete}} (noma {{ui:Delete}}) iyayisusa; {{key:Mod+Z}} iyibuyisela.

Ipinikhodi yenothi iba yiorenji uma ivulekile, ibhlu uma iphendulwe, futhi igrey uma ivaliwe; ifu elivaliwe liba igrey.

## Uhlu

**Izimaki** ngakwesokudla zidweba zonke izimaki zomsebenzi wokubala ngesimo sazo, futhi zitshela ukuthi zingaki ezisavulekile. Thikha **Vulekile kuphela** ukuze ubone okusasele ukuthi kuxazululwe. Cofa umugqa ukuze uye esheetini wayo futhi uyikhethe.

## Yabelana ngazo

- {{ui:Marked PDF}} iyazidweba emakhasini: okuqaqambisiwe, amafu nopini, ngalinye nebhokisi elinikeza isimo sayo ngamakhamandla (VULEKILE, IPHENDULIWE, IVALIWE) nombhalo wayo. Ikhasi elinezimaki kodwa elingenazo izimaki zokubala nalo liyafakwa.
- {{ui:XLSX}} yengeza umsebenzi wewebhu **Markups**: ishidi, uhlobo, isimo nombhalo, umugqa owodwa ngamunye.
- Ziyagcinwa ku-`.takeoff.json` nakwi-[PDF enokubala ngaphakathi](topic:save-and-reopen#keep-the-takeoff-inside-the-pdf).
