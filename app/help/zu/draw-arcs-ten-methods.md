## Okuzokwenza

Dweba umphetho ogobile emugqeni, endaweni noma embobweni, usebenzisa indlela yejika ehambisana nobukhulu obunawo.

## Izinyathelo

1. Qala umugqa, umphetho noma imbobo bese ubeka iphoyinti lapho igobolondo liqala.
2. Vula {{ui:Ijika}} ethuluzini noma cindezela {{key:R}}.
3. Vula {{ui:Indlela ye-Arc}} ebhalweni leshidi. Ibonakala kuphela ngenkathi i-{{ui:Ijika}} ivuliwe. Khetha 3-Point, Start, Center, End, Start, Center, Angle, Start, Center, Length, Start, End, Angle, Start, End, Direction, Start, End, Radius, Center, Start, End, Center, Start, Angle noma Center, Start, Length.
4. Landela ibha yesimo. Itshela ukuthi lindeni: isikhungo, iphoyinti lokuphela, i-engeli, ubude, isiqondiso noma iradiyasi. Chofoza iphoyinti, noma uthayiphe inombolo (i-engeli, ubude, iradiyasi nesiqondiso).
5. Uma inkinobho yokugcina inikeziwe, ijika lingezwa emugqeni. {{key:Esc}} ikhansela ijika, {{key:Backspace}} ibuyisela isinyathelo sayo sokugcina, no-{{key:R}} vala i-{{ui:Ijika}}.

## Amanothi

- Ijika liyenqatshwa, nomlayezo kwibha yesimo, uma amaphoyinti ayo ehlanganyela noma eme eqondile, noma uma iradiyasi ingafiki kuphoyinti lokuphela.
- Ubude bayo ubude bejika ngqo, futhi amafayela endala avulwa ngendlela efanayo. Okwengeziwe: [Linganisa imigqa](topic:measure-linear#arcs-and-curved-walls).
