## Amayunithi ezinguquko zezifomula

| Iyunithi yento | L, P, H | A |
|---|---|---|
| LF | amafidi | amafidi aswele (indawo yodonga) |
| m | amamitha | amamitha aswele (indawo yodonga) |
| SF | amafidi | amafidi aswele |
| SY | amafidi | amayadi aswele |
| SQ | amafidi | amaskwele |
| ac | amafidi | amacre |
| m² | amamitha | amamitha aswele |
| ha | amamitha | amahektha |

Ngezinto ze-SY, `A` isezikwele-yadi kuyilapho `P` no `H` kuhlala emafidini. Ivolumu evela kento ye-SY ingu `A * 9 * H / 27` amayadi ayisikwele; ukusebenzisa i-SF kumaslabhu kulula kakhulu.

## Lapho amayunithi emisiwe

Ezinye izinkombandlela zihlala zisebenzisa amafidi:

- umbhalo ophilayo ku-status bar lapho udweba (amafidi-namainshi, SF),
- **Imaki ekhethiwe** (amafidi-namainshi, LF no-SF),
- umphumela wokuhlola (amafidi-namainshi, namamitha),
- ubude bezingxenye nemiphetho ku-Marks worksheet yespredishithi (amafidi-namainshi).

Izinani zento, ithebula le-{{ui:Amanani}}, izifomula kanye ne-Estimate worksheet zisebenzisa iyunithi yento ngayo.

## Izikali namayunithi ahlukene

Isikali seshidi sisho ukuthi umdwebo mkhulu kangakanani; iyunithi yento isho ukuthi kufanele ibikwe kanjani. Umdwebo wemetric ku-`1:100` ungalinganiswa nge-LF, futhi umdwebo we-imperial ungalinganiswa ngo-m². Ukulinganisa kwamukela noma iyiphi iyunithi: `24'-6"`, `7.5 m`, `7500 mm`.

## Ukunemba

I-TakeoffCraft ilinganisa ngokunembile kumaphoyinti oyichofozayo, kufaka phakathi ubude bama-arc bangempela nezindawo, futhi ayijikeleziseli izinani. Ukubonisa kujikeleza ubude ku-1/16 yeyinshi nezinombolo ezinamachashazi ku-amabili. Imali ijikelezwa kuphela ezinanini ezinwetshiwe, kuya esentini.
