## Okuzofunda

Okwenzeka kokubalwa lapho abantu abaningana belinganisa umsebenzi ofanayo ngesikhathi esifanayo. I-CadCraft Studio iqalisa futhi ijoyine isikhathi se-Live TeamWork (Share live, Join); leli khasi likhuluma ngokubalwa uqobo.

## Wonke umuntuulinganisa ngesikhathi esisodwa

Ama-PDF womphathi afika kubo bonke abajoyinile. Engeza izinto, linganisa imigqa, izindawo nezinani, bese usetha izilinganiso nezindleko njengoba bekwenza ngokujwayelekile. Izimaki zakho zivela masinyane; ezabanye zivela ngemva kwesikhashana ezidalwa.

- **Izinani zifanayo kubo bonke:** iwindi ngalinye lisebenza izinani nesilinganiso kusukela ezimakini, ngakho awekho amanani ohlukana.
- **Abantu ababili beshintsha into noma imaki efanayo:** izinto ezihlukile zombili zigcinwa; into efanayo ihamba nenguquko yakamuva.
- **Imaki ekwinto esanda kususwa** iyabuyiswa, futhi ibha yesimo ichaza ngani.
- Izinto nezimaki ezintsha zomuntu ngamunye zithola izinombolo zazo, ngakho abantu ababili abayikaze benze eyodwa.

## Bonana

Wonke umuntu kulesi sikhathi unombala wakhe. Eshidini obheka kulo ubona isikhombisi sakhe ngasinye njengomcibisholo ngombala wakhe negama lakhe eduze kwawo, nebhokisi elinemigqa ngombala wakhe ozungeza izimaki abazikhethile. Abantu abesheshe elinye ishidi bahlelwa phezulu ngakwesokudla kwendawo yokubuka ngegama leshidi lawo.

Izikhombisi, amagama nokukhethwa kuphela kwesikhathi sokuhlangana: akuzange kugcinwe ngaphakathi kwefayela futhi akuzange kungene ekuhlehliseni komuntu. Ziyahamba zibhodle lapho kukhona umuntu ngamunye, ngakho zihlala zibukeka zishelela nakuxhumo oluhamba kancane.

## Ukuhlehlisa kuhlehlisa umsebenzi wakho

{{key:Mod+Z}} ihlehlisa inguquko yakho yokugcina kuphela futhi ishiye eyabanye ibe njalo. Uma umuntu eshintshe into efanayo selokhu, akukho okuhlehliswayo futhi ibha yesimo itshela ukuthi ubani.

## Joyina kusuka kusiphequluli

Ilungu lethimu elingenayo i-Studio lingaskena ikhodi ephezu kwesithombe ngefoni, ithebhulethi noma ikhompyutha ekwinethiwekhi efanayo. I-TakeoffCraft ivula kusiphequluli futhi igcwale ngokubalwa komphathi nama-PDF akhe uma umphathi sekuvumile ukuthi ungene. Isixhumanisi sesimemo singavumela umuntu ukuthi ajoyine kusuka kwenye inethiwekhi: umphathi kufanele abe nesihlalo se-Team Cloud Plan se-app, abanye bonke bajoyina mahhala, futhi isixhumanisi sisebenza kuphela ngenkathi isikhathi somphathi sivulekile.

## Lapho isikhathi siphela

Umphathi kuphela ogcina ukubalwa. Abanye bonke bagcina ikhophi engalondolozwanga: yigcine uma ufuna ukuyigcina.
