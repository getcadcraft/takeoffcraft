TakeoffCraft ivula umdwebo we-DWG noma we-DXF njengoba ivula i-PDF: akudingeki uwuprinte ku-PDF kuqala. Ukubala ngqo kumdwebo kuyanemba, ngoba umdwebo wazi ukuthi yini ngayinye yento. Bona [Bala kumdwebo](topic:count-from-a-drawing).

## Vula umdwebo

1. Khetha {{ui:Vula amapulani}} kweqembu {{ui:Ifayela}}, noma cindezela {{key:Mod+O}}.
2. Khetha ifayela le-DWG noma le-DXF. Ungakhetha eziningi, nama-PDF nawo.
3. Khetha Vula.

Ungawuhudula ufake imidwebo ku-TakeoffCraft, njenge-PDF. Akulayishwa lutho: ibhrawuza lifunda ifayela kukhompyutha yakho.

TakeoffCraft ifunda i-DWG kusukela ku-AutoCAD 2000 kuya ku-2018 ne-DXF ye-ASCII kusukela ku-R12 kuya ku-2018. Yenqaba i-DXF yebinary (yigcine njenge-ASCII, noma njenge-DWG) nofayela olungelona nalolunye, futhi isho ngenxa. Ifayela elonakele livuleka ngalokho okwakwazeka ukufundwa, kuvele umlayezo osho lokho; amanani alo angase angaphelele.

## Amashidi

- **Imodeli** yisikhala semodeli somdwebo.
- **Isakhiwo ngasinye** (ithebhu yesikhala sephepha) liyishidi, eliqanjwe ngethebhu yalo, ngokulandelana kwamathebhu. Isakhiwo sibonisa imodeli ngamafasitele ayo, njengoba iprintwa.

Sondeza, shelelisa, ukubambelela emigqeni yomdwebo, amamaki, amanothi, {{ui:Linganisa}} nokunye kusebenza njengakuPDF. Amashidi omdwebo agcinwa kokubalwa ngegama lefayela nomkhondo wokuqukethwe kwawo, njenge-PDF: vula ifayela futhi ngokuphindiwe, amamaki asemashidini awo.

## Isikali

Ishidi liqala ngesikali somdwebo owunikezayo:

- **Isakhiwo** sithatha isikali sefasitele layo elikhulu kunawo wonke. Isikali njengo-1/2" = 1'-0" sibizwa ngesethi yaso; noma yisiphi esinye sibonakala njengo-"Kusuka kufasitele lomdwebo: 1" = 2.5'". Uma isakhiwo sinamafasitele ezikali ezihlukile, elikhulu kunawo lisetha isikali seshidi: hlola ezinye nge-{{ui:Hlola}}.
- **Imodeli** ithatha amayunithi omdwebo (amayintshi, amamilimitha, amayizinyawo…) njengesikali, njengo-"Amayunithi omdwebo (amayintshi)".
- **Umdwebo ongenamayunithi** (umdwebo ongakasho ukuthi iyunithi iyinch noma imilimitha) awunasikali: ishidi lithi "Akukho sikali", futhi ukulinganisa kulo kuyanqatshwa, njengakuPDF ongenasikali. Ukubala akudingi sikali. Nika esinye nge-{{ui:Linganisa}}.

{{ui:Linganisa}}, {{ui:Hlola}} nohlu lwe-{{ui:Okusethiwe ngaphambili}} lishintsha isikali noma nini futhi liphumelele kokomdwebo. Uhlu lwe-{{ui:Okusethiwe ngaphambili}} luvaliwe ku-Imodeli, ngoba lungenaso usayizi wephepha.

## Ongqimba

{{ui:Ongqimba}} kubha yeshidi ilanda ongqimba bomdwebo nenani lezinto ezikulelo shidi. Ongqimba abavalwe noma abaqandisiwe ifayela bayaqala befihliwe. Khipha itiki kungqimba ukuze uyifihle, noma thikha ongqimba ofihliwe ukuze uyibonise: ishidi liyadwetshwa futhi, okufihliwe kukhishwa ekubaleni nasezintweni ezinikezwa ku-{{ui:Linganisa umdwebo}}, ngaphandle uma ucela ukubala nongqimba ofihliwe. {{ui:Bonisa konke}} ibonisa bonke ongqimba; {{ui:Buyela kongqimba befayela}} ihlehlisa izinguquko zakho. Izinketho zongqimba zingezalo iseshini, hhayi zefayela lokubala.

## Izixhumanisi zemidwebo (xrefs)

Umdwebo oxhumanisa iminye imidwebo uyibonisa lapho ifakwe khona. I-TakeoffCraft ithola umdwebo oxhunyiwe phakathi kwamafayela **avulekile**, ngegama lefayela lawo: vula umdwebo owubambe nomdwebo oxhunyiwe ndawonye (noma ngokulandelana). Kuze kube yilapho, ibha yesimo ithi imidwebo exhunyiwe emingaki engatholakali, okudwetshwa kuwo kushoda, nokubala kukusho lokho.

## Imikhawulo

- Izinto i-TakeoffCraft engazifundi noma engazibeki (izinto ze-proxy, izigaba ezingaziwa) zidwetshwa kuwindi lokubala ngohlobo; azikaze zilahlwe ngaphandle kokukhuluma.
- Imidwebo emikhulu kakhulu idwetshwa ngengxenye, nokubala kumisa ekhawulweni, kusho lokho. Esikhundleni salokho vula i-PDF yokuprinta yalowo mdwebo.
- Umdwebo udwetshwa mnyama nangemibala yawo, ufiphale ephepheni elimhlophe. Amatafula wesitayela sokuprinta nezabelo zepeni akusetshenziswa.
