Ukubala amanani kulondolozwa njengefayela elincane leprojekthi, `(name).takeoff.json`. Lubamba izinto zakho, izinhlanganisela, amarethi, isikali sawo wonke amashidi nazo zonke izimaki. **Ayibambi** ama-PDF; iwathola futhi ngokuqukethwe kwawo.

## Londoloza

1. Khetha {{ui:Save takeoff}} eqenjini le-{{ui:File}}, noma cindezela {{key:Mod+S}}.
   Isiphequluli sakho silanda `(name).takeoff.json`. Ibha yesimo ithi "Kulondolozwe (name).takeoff.json. Ivuleka phezu kwama-PDF afanayo (afanisa ngokuqukethwe kwawo)."
2. Hambisa lelo fayela eduze kwama-PDF omsebenzi, ukuze kuhlale kanye.

Ukulondoloza ngakunye kulanda ifayela elisha. Isiphequluli sakho singalitshela ngokuthi `(name) (1).takeoff.json`; gcina elisha kakhulu.

Igama livela kwi-PDF yokuqala oyivule kulokhu kubala amanani.

:::note
I-{{ui:Save takeoff}} iyona ndlela kuphela yokugcina ukubala amanani njengefayela. Ngenkathi usebenza, i-TakeoffCraft igcina futhi ikopi yokubuyisela kulesi siphequluli (bona ngezansi), futhi ukulondoloza kwigu aikatholakali. Uma uzama ukuvala ithebhu nezinguquko ezingalondolozwanga, isiphequluli siyakubuza ukuthi uyafuna yini ukushiya.
:::

## Gcina ukubala amanani ngaphakathi kwe-PDF

{{ui:Export}} › {{ui:PDF with the takeoff inside}} ilanda ikopi ye-PDF ngayinye evulekile, egcina igama elifana no `Plans (with takeoff).pdf`, futhi ithwele ukubala amanani konke njengesifakelo: izinto, izimaki, izikali, izindawo zokunikezela, konke okugcinwa yi-`.takeoff.json`. Amakhasi awo awaguquki, ngakho abukeka futhi aphrintwe njengokuqala, futhi abafundi be-PDF abanye bababalela ukubala amanani phakathi kwezifakelo zabo.

Vula leyo PDF ku-TakeoffCraft (noma iyiphi indlela yokuvula ama-PDF) futhi izobuza **Le PDF inokubala amanani ngaphakathi**: khetha {{ui:Open the takeoff}} ukuze uyithole, noma {{ui:Just the PDF}} ukuvula idrowing kuphela. Ikopi imele i-PDF eyenziwe kuyo, ngakho i-`.takeoff.json` elondolozwe kwenye yazo ivula kwazo zombili.

Ngama-PDF amaningi, ikopi ngayinye ithwele ukubala amanani konke; vula zonke ndawonye. Ukulondoloza futhi kusuka kwikopi kushintsha ukubala amanani ngaphakathi kwayo, akweqi kwesibili. I-`.takeoff.json` isasele elokugcina: iyincane, futhi yiyo eloga ukulondolozwa ngokuzenzakalelayo nohlelo lwedeskithophu.

## Uma ithebhu noma isiphequluli simi

Imizuzwana embalwa ngemva kwenguquko ngayinye, i-TakeoffCraft igcina ikopi yokubala amanani kusitoreji salesi siphequluli, kanye nama-PDF awo uma ayi-64 MB noma ngaphansi. Akukho okulayishwa. Uma ulondoloza ukubala amanani, uqale elisha noma uvule enye iprojekthi, ikopi iyanyamalala.

Uma ithebhu iwa, isiphequluli noma likhompyutha limi, noma uphuma nezinguquko ezingalondolozwanga, ngokuvula kwakho i-TakeoffCraft okulandelayo izobuza **Buyisa umsebenzi wakho?** bese ikhombisa ukubala amanani ngegama lawo. Chofoza **Buyisa** ukuze uwuthole: izinto, izimaki nezikali ziyabuya, nama-PDF avuleka futhi uma agcinwe. Ibha yesimo ithi "Kubuyiswe (name). Akulondolozwanga efayelini okwamanje: {{ui:Save takeoff}} iyakugcina." Uma ama-PDF ebemakhulu kakhulu ukuba agcinwe, wavule futhi; izimaki zivela emakhasini awo. Okwengeziwe: [Buyisa umsebenzi wakho](common:recover).

## Vula kabusha

1. Vula i-TakeoffCraft.
2. Yehlisa ifayela le-`.takeoff.json` kanye nama-PDF awo ndawonye ekhasini. Noma khetha {{ui:Open plans}} wama-PDF no-{{ui:Open takeoff}} weprojekthi, noma kusiphi isiqalo.
   Ibha yesimo ithi "Kuvuliwe (name): izinto ezingu-12, izimaki ezingu-340." Ishidi ngalinye lithola isikali salo, nezimaki zivela emashidini azo.

Uhlelo lwama-PDF alubalulekile, futhi namagama awo amafayela noma amafolda awo akubalulekile. I-TakeoffCraft ibona ngayinye i-PDF ngesigxivizo sokuqukethwe kwayo.

Uma uvula iprojekthi ngenkathi ukubala amanani okwamanje kunezinguquko ezingalondolozwanga, i-TakeoffCraft iqala ibuze: **Khansela**, **Lahla izinguquko** noma **Londoloza ukubala amanani kuqala**. Ukuvula iprojekthi kuyashintsha izinto nezimaki zamanje; ama-PDF asevulekile ayahlala evulekile.

## Uma i-PDF ingekho

Uma uvula iprojekthi ngaphandle kwama-PDF ayo wonke, ibha yesimo yengeza "Vula ama-PDF ayo ukuze ubone izimaki (zibhalwe ngaphansi kwamashidi)." Ngaphansi kohlu lwamashidi, inothi lithi:

"Lokhu kubala amanani kuphinde kumboze ama-PDF angavulekile. Amanani awo ayabalwa; wavule ukuze ubone futhi uhlele izimaki zawo:"

kulandelwe ngamagama amafayela nosayizi. Izimaki zawo sezisabalwa kuwo wonke amasamanisa nokuthunyelwa, ngezikali abalelwa ngazo. Vula lawo ma-PDF ukuze ubone futhi uhlele izimaki.

## Uma i-PDF ishintshiwe

I-PDF eguqulwe, noma eshintshwe nje ngohlelo lwenye, inokuqukethwe okwehlukile, ngakho ingumfayela **ohlukile** kwi-TakeoffCraft, nakuba inegama elifanayo. Kunothi ngaphansi kohlu lwamashidi, leli fayela lisho ukuthi "kukhona ifayela elinalo igama elifanayo elivuliwe, kodwa okuqukethwe kwalo kuyahluka".

- Izimaki zihlala zibambelele enguqulweni endala. Ziyaqhubeka nokubalwa, ezindaweni zazo zakuqala nasezikalini zazo zakuqala.
- I-PDF eguqulwe ivula njengamashidi amasha angenasikali nezimaki.

Ukuze uhambise ukubala amanani kulokho okuguqulwe, vula zombili i-PDF bese usebenzisa {{ui:Compare}} eshidini elisha: [Qhathanisa ishidi eliguqulwe uhambise ukubala amanani](topic:compare-revisions).

## Ifayela elonakele noma elingelona elakho

I-TakeoffCraft ihlola yonke ifayela leprojekthi ngaphambi kokulisebenzisa. Imilayezo oyibonayo:

- "(file) akuyona iprojekthi ye-TakeoffCraft (akuyona i-JSON esemthethweni)."
- "Lolu akuyona ifayela leprojekthi le-TakeoffCraft."
- "Le projekthi yalondolozwa yi-TakeoffCraft entsha. Yivule khona."
- "Izimaki ezingu-3 zonakele zakhishwa." (okunye kuyavuleka)

Umlando wokuhlehlisa uqala kabusha uma uvula iprojekthi. Ukulondoloza akuwususi.
