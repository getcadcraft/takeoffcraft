## Amashidi (kwesobunxele)

- **Sesha amashidi** ihlunga uhlu ngegama leshidi, ngegama lefayela noma ngelebula yekhasi.
- Ishidi ngalinye linesithombe esincane (sidwetshwa njengoba usuka phansi), igama layo, nalokho okuthi ngesikali sayo noma **Akukho sikali**.
- Ngakwesokudla kwesakhiwo ngasinye kukhombisa amakwe amangaki eshidi linawo. Uma into ikhanyisiwe, ikhombisa amakwe alo mbhalo akulo.
- Chofoza ishidi ukuze liyivule. {{key:PageDown}} no-{{key:PageUp}} ziya kweshidi elilandelayo nelandulelayo kuhlu.
- Uma ukubala kumboza ama-PDF angavulekile, isaziso ngaphansi kohlu siwabiza ngamagama. Bona [Londoloza bese uvula kabusha ukubala](topic:save-and-reopen).

Amagama amashidi avela ku-PDF: ilebula yekhasi njenge-`A-101` iba yigama leshidi. Ikhasi elingenalo ilebula libizwa ngegama lefayela nenombolo yekhasi, njengo "Amapulani, ikhasi 3". Uma kuvulwe ama-PDF amaningi, amashidi anelebula akhombisa negama lefayela.

## Ishidi (phakathi)

- Ibha engaphansi kweshidi ikhombisa igama leshidi nesikali salo, noma "Akukho sikali okwamanje: khetha i-preset noma linganisa". Izinkinobho zayo: {{ui:MarkupCraft}} (lapho lolo hlelo lunganamukela i-PDF; bona [Qala ukubala uvule isethi yamapulani](topic:open-plan-set)), {{ui:Compare}} (inguqulo endala yeshidi ngaphezu kwalo: [Qhathanisa ishidi elibuyekeziwe](topic:compare-revisions)), "(n) ukuhlola" (amakwe akhonjiswe, uma likhona) kanye ne-{{ui:3D}} ([umbono wokuhlola ngo-3D](topic:check-in-3d)).
- Ngaphambi kokuvulwa kwanoma yiPDF, phakathi kubonisa umhlahlandlela omfushane wezinyathelo ezinhlanu.
- Inkonzo yembewu isondeza endaweni yophoyinta. Hudula ukuze ushelele. Bona [Ikhibhodi nemouse](topic:keyboard).
- Lapho usebenzisa i-{{ui:Auto count}}, ibha enezilawuli zayo ivela phezulu kweshidi.

## Izinto (kwesokudla)

Phezulu kukhona uhlu lwe-{{ui:Bid area}}: isakhiwo, uhlelo noma isigaba lapho kuzongeniswa khona amakwe amasha ([Izindawo zezindleko](topic:bid-areas)).

- {{ui:New item}} ivula ifomu elincane: igama, uhlobo ({{ui:Linear (length)}}, {{ui:Area}} noma {{ui:Count}}) kanye ne-{{ui:Add}}.
- Uhlu lubonisa umbala wento ngayinye, isithombe sohlobo, igama nenani. Chofoza into ukuze udwebe ngayo nokuqaphela amakwe ayo kuwo wonke amashidi. Chofoza futhi ukuze uyeke ukuqaphela.
- Ngaphansi kohlu kukhona **Izinto: (igama)**, umhleli wento: igama, uhlobo, umbala, iyunithi, ubude noma ukujula, ukulahleka, amarethi empahla nomsebenzi, izinkulumo ezilinganisiwe, kanye ne-{{ui:Assembly}}. Bona [Dala izinto zokubala](topic:create-items).
- **Iphoyinti elikhethiwe** livela uma ukhetha iphoyinti: ukuthi lingeyaphi into, ishidi lalo, izilinganiso zalo, nezinkinobho ezinjenge-{{ui:Add a cut-out}} ne-{{ui:Delete mark}}. Uma kukhethwe amakwe amaningi lidlulela ku-**Amakwe akhethiwe**: inani lento ngayinye, nezinkinobho ze-{{ui:Copy}}, {{ui:Duplicate}}, {{ui:Cut}} ne-{{ui:Delete marks}}.
- Umhleli wento uphela ngo-{{ui:Copy item}} ne-{{ui:Duplicate item}}: into enenhlanganisela yayo nezindleko, ngaphandle kwamakwe. Bona [Kopisha, namathisela futhi uphinde amakwe](topic:copy-paste).

## Amanani (phansi)

- {{ui:By item}} ibala yonke into nezingxenye zayo zenhlanganisela ngenani, ukulahleka, amarethi nezindleko ezandisiwe. Ungathayipha ukulahleka namarethi ngqo kwithebula.
- {{ui:By sheet}} ibala inani ngalinye lento kuleshidi ngalinye.
- Isexwayiso esiqala ngo-**Okungaphelele:** sivela uma kukhona okushiywe ezinhlanganisweni.
- {{ui:Job total}} isendaweni ekwesokudla kwesihloko.
- Inkinobho enompini ekwesokudla kakhulu ifihla noma ibonise ithebula ukuze kube nendawo eningi yeshidi.

Bona [Funda amanani nesilinganiso](topic:read-quantities).

## Ibha yesimo (phansi)

Kusuka kwesobunxele kuya kwesokudla: ukukala okuphilayo ngesikhathi udwebayo (ubude, indawo, iradiyasi noma umphumela wokuhlola), ibhokisi le-{{ui:Type}} uma uthayipha ubude, imilayezo evela ku-TakeoffCraft, isikhumbuzi esithi "Shift snaps to 45°" (noma "Snaps to 45°; Shift frees the angle" uma lokho kuvulwe kuthi [Izilungiselelo](topic:settings)), inkinobho ye-{{ui:Snap}} (ukubambelela emigqeni yedrowing, ivulekile uma ikhanyisiwe; {{key:F3}}), kanye nephesenti lokusondeza.

:::tip
Funda ibha yesimo uma okuthile kungenzeki. I-TakeoffCraft icacisa lapho ukuthi ikwenqabile ngani ukuchofoza, isibonelo "Leli shidi alikakabi nesikali".
:::

## Ezinye izakhiwo

Konke okusenhla kungakhulunywa ngakho kusakhiwo se-{{ui:CadCraft classic}}, okuwukuqala. {{ui:Settings}} ({{key:Mod+,}}) › {{ui:Global}} › {{ui:Layout}} inikeza ezimbili ezengeziwe; amathuluzi nokhiye bafanayo kuzo zonke ezintathu, futhi ukukhetha {{ui:CadCraft classic}} futhi kubuyisela yonke into lapho yayikhona.

| Isakhiwo | Ku-TakeoffCraft |
|---|---|
| {{ui:Ribbon and project browser}} | I-{{ui:Takeoff ribbon}} phezulu ([Imenyu, izinkinobho nemouse](topic:menus-keys-and-mouse)), ibha yethuluzi elisebenzayo, nethebhu ngalinye leshidi olivulayo (namamodeli nezakhiwo zedrowing nazo ngamashidi), ngamapresethi esikali ne-{{ui:Fit page}} ngaphansi kweshidi. Ipanele elingakwesobunxele linezakhiwo ze-{{ui:Properties}} (into, inothi noma iphoyinti elikhethiwe) ngaphezulu kwe-{{ui:Takeoff browser}}: {{ui:Sheets}} (chofoza eyodwa ukuze uyivule ethebhini), {{ui:Conditions}} (chofoza eyodwa ukuze udwebe ngayo) kanye ne-{{ui:Estimate}} (isamba sento ngayinye nesamba sephrojekthi); izithombe ezincane zingethebhu yayo yesibili. |
| {{ui:Toolbox and info box}} | Ibha yamathuluzi phezu kweshidi, ibhokisi lolwazi nebhokisi le-{{ui:Bid area}}, ithuluzi elisebenzayo noma ukukhethwa, nebha yeshidi, ikholomu yamathuluzi okubala ngakwesobunxele, ithebhu ngayo yonke ishidi, kanye ne-{{ui:Snap}} nokusondeza ngaphansi kweshidi. |

Uma [umbono wokuhlola ngo-3D](topic:check-in-3d) uvuliwe, amathebhu amashidi nebha engaphansi kweshidi ayasuka; vala umbono we-3D ukuze ulinganise futhi. Efonini kusebenza isakhiwo sefoni njalo.

## Isikrini esigcwele nezihloko

Inkinobho ye-{{ui:Full screen}} ebhareni yohlelo, noma {{key:Ctrl+Shift+F}} (i-Control kuzo zonke izinhlelo, nakuMac), ijikeleza phakathi kwejwayelekile, isikrini esigcwele, nesikrini esigcwele nezibha ezifihliwe. Ngezibha ezifihliwe, hambisa iphoyinta ngaphezulu emaphethelweni ukuze uzibuyisele.

I-{{ui:Paper}} iyisihloko esikhanyayo, ne-{{ui:Model}} eyimnyama. Kwi-{{ui:Model}} idrowing iboniswa iphenduliwe (imigqa ekhanyayo ngaphezu kokumnyama) kuskrini kuphela; okuthunyelwayo akusoze kushintshwe.
