{{ui:Ukubala okuzenzakalelayo}} ithola yonke ikhophi yesimbuzo emva kokuthi ubhokise esisodwa. Bese ubuka ukufana kulo shidi, bese wengeza lezo ozamukelayo njengezimpawu zokubala. Kuyisisekelo, hhayi isiqinisekiso: hlala ubuka ngaphambi kokuthi wengeze.

## Thola izimpawu

1. Yenza noma chofoza into yokubala ngaphansi kwe-{{ui:Izinto}}.
2. Vula ishidi lapho kuvela khona isimbuzo.
3. Khetha {{ui:Ukubala okuzenzakalelayo}} ebhareni lamathuluzi, noma cindezela {{key:S}}.
   Ibha ivela phezulu kweshidi: "Hudula ibhokisi ozungeze isimbuzo esisodwa kuleshidi."
4. Ukuze useshe wonke amashidi avulekile, hhayi leli kuphela, tshekisha {{ui:Sesha wonke amashidi avulekile}}.
5. Sondeza ukuze isimbuzo sibonakale kahle, bese uhudula ibhokisi eliqinile ozungeze ikhophi eyodwa yaso. Faka isimbuzo sonke; amaphepha amhlophe azungezile ayasikwa.
   Ibha ikhombisa "Iyasesha…" nebha yenqubekela phambili nenkinobho {{ui:Khansela}}. Ikhasi liyaphendula: ungasondeza, ushelelise, futhi uhambe phakathi kwamashidi. Ukukhetha elinye ithuluzi, noma {{key:Esc}}, kuyayeka ukusesha.
6. Lapho isiphela, izimpawu zivela kwishidi njengamabhokisi anombala wento, nebha ikhombisa isifinyezo esifana ne-"Izimpawu ezingu-22, ezingu-19 zokungeza (ezingu-3 zesivele zibaliwe). Chofoza ibhokisi ukuze uliyenqabe noma ulibuyisele."

Ku-{{ui:Ukubala okuzenzakalelayo}}, ukuhudula kwishidi kudweba ibhokisi esikhundleni sokushelelisa. Ukuze ushelelise, bamba {{key:Space}} uhudule, noma hudula ngenkinobho ephakathi.

## Buyekeza izimpawu

- **Chofoza ibhokisi** ukuze uliyenqabe. Ibhokisi elenqatshiwe libonakala lifakwe imigqa yokudonsa futhi lihlakaniswe. Chofoza futhi ukuze uliyibuyisele.
- **Hambisa isilayidi se-{{ui:Ukufana}}** (50% kuya ku-95%, siqala ku-75%). Okuphezulu kukhombisa okufanayo okuseduze kuphela; okuphansi kukhombisa okungaqinile kangako. Isilayidi sihlunga kuphela lokho okutholakele; asiseshi futhi.
- **Esesivele sibaliwe**: izimpawu endaweni esivele inophawu lokubala lalo kuleli thuluzi ziqala zinqatshiwe, ukuze ungabali okufanayo kabili.
- **Amanye amashidi**: uma useshe wonke amashidi avulekile, hamba phakathi kwamashidi ngohlu lwamashidi noma nge-{{key:PageDown}} bese ubuyekeza ngalinye. Isifinyezo sikhombisa amashidi amaningi anezimpawu.

## Yengeza

Khetha {{ui:Engeza}}. Inkinobho ikhombisa inani, njengo "Engeza izimpawu ezingu-19".
Izimpawu zokubala zingezwa endaweni ephakathi kwebhokisi ngalinye elamukelekile: "Kungezwe izimpawu zokubala ezingu-19 ku-\"Duplex receptacle\". Ctrl+Z ususa zonke."

Lonke iqoqo liyisinyathelo esisodwa sokuhlehlisa, ngakho i-{{key:Mod+Z}} eyodwa iyasusa konke.

Ukuze uyeke ukubuyekeza ungengezi lutho, khetha {{ui:Lahla}} noma cindezela {{key:Esc}}. Ukukhetha elinye ithuluzi nakho kuyilahla.

## Okuyithola nokuyiphuthelwa

I-TakeoffCraft iqhathanisa umdwebo, ngamaphikseli, nesimbuzo obhokishe sona.

- Ithola isimbuzo esiphendukile ngama-0°, 90°, 180° no-270°. Tshekisha {{ui:Noma yiliphi iangle (kancane)}} ebhareni le-auto count ngaphambi kokudweba ibhokisi ukuze ithole nanoma iyiphi i-angle: ihlola yonke ngama-15°, bese iqinisa ngayinye ibe ngu-1.5°. Lokhu kuthatha cishe izikhathi eziyisithupha isikhathi eside. Ayitholi isimbuzo esiphendukile njengesibuko (ngaphandle uma isimbuzo sifana nakho njengesibuko).
- Uma {{ui:Sesha wonke amashidi avulekile}} etshekishiwe, ishidi elinesikali esihlukile liseshwa ngosayizi ofanele lesimbuzo kuleyo ndawo: isifanekiso esidwetshwe ngesikali kwi-plan ye-1/8" singu-ingxenye yesibili yaso kwi-plan ye-1/4", futhi sisatholakala. Leli shidi liseshwa futhi ngosayizi webhokisi lalo, kumathegi nezimpawu ezishicilelwe ngosayizi ofanayo kuwo wonke amashidi. Lokhu kudinga isikali kuzo zombili amashidi; ishidi elingenaso isikali liseshwa ngosayizi webhokisi kuphela.
- Izimpawu ezifana kakhulu zingafana zodwa: i-duplex nesiquad sokufaka, noma amathegi amabili ezinto ahlukaniswa ngohlamvu olulodwa. Phakamisa isilayidi kuze kusale okufanele kuphela, bese unqenya okunye ngesandla.
- Isimbuzo esivinjelwe yimigqa, ukufakwa kwemigqa noma umbhalo sithola amaphuzu aphansi futhi kungadinga isilayidi esiphansi.
- Amabhokisi amancane kakhulu ayenqatshelwa: "Leli bhokisi lincane kakhulu. Hudula ozungeze isimbuzo sonke." Ibhokisi elingenalutho nalo liyenqatshelwa: "Leli bhokisi lingenalutho cishe. Hudula ozungeze isimbuzo."
- Ishidi elingaba likhulu kakhulu ngosayizi odingekayo walo simbuzo liyadlulwa, futhi isifinyezo sithi "ishidi eli-1 libe likhulu kakhulu ukuze lisheshwe ngosayizi walo simbuzo." Hudula isimbuzo esikhulu, noma ubale leli shidi ngesandla.

Uma kungatholakali lutho, ibha yesimo ithi "Azikho izifanele. Nciphisa isilayidi sokufana, noma bhoksisa isimbuzo ngokuqinile."

## Isivinini nama-browser

Ukusesha kusebenza ngesisebenzi esingemuva, ngakho ikhasi liyaphendula futhi ungayeka noma nini. Ishidi ngalinye liqala ukudwetshwa ngosayizi wokusebenza ekhasini ngokwalo, okungabangela ukuthi ishidi elikhulu kakhulu limise umzuzwana. Lisebenza kuChrome, Edge, Firefox ne-Safari yamanje. Ukusesha amashidi amaningi kuthatha isikhathi eside; ibha yenqubekela phambili ihamba ishidi ngeshidi.
