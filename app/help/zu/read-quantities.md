Ithebula {{ui:Quantities}} ngaphansi kwewindi yisilinganiso sakho sezindleko esiphilayo. Lishintsha ngokushesha lapho udweba, usule, ushintsha isikali noma uhlele irethi. Amanani awagcinwa; alinganiswa kabusha kusukela ezimakini njalo, ngakho awanakuba ngaphandle kwesikhathi.

## Ngento

Khetha {{ui:By item}} (okuzenzakalelayo). Into ngayinye inomugqa wayo, nezingxenye zayo zomhlangano zinemigqa ngaphansi kwayo.

| Ikholomu | Incazelo |
|---|---|
| Into | Igama, nombala wento. "+(n) unscaled" kusho ukuthi ezinye izimaki zisemashidini angenaso isikali. |
| Inani | Isamba esilinganisiwe ngeyunithi yento (izingxenye: umphumela wefomula). |
| Iyunithi | LF, m, SF, SY, m², EA, noma iyunithi yengxenye. |
| Ukulahleka % | Kuthayipha lapha noma kumhleli wento. |
| Ngokulahleka | Inani × (1 + ukulahleka). |
| Impahla/iyunithi, Ukusebenza/iyunithi | Amarethi; ungawathayipha lapha. |
| Impahla, Ukusebenza | Ngokulahleka × irethi, kuzungezwe kusenti. |
| Isamba | Impahla + ukusebenza. |

Umugqa wokugcina, **Isamba sembangi**, wengeza amakholomu empahla, okusebenza nesamba. Leli samba libonakala futhi ngakwesokudla kwesihloko sethebula.

Thayipha kuseli lokulahleka noma lerethi bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}} noma uchofoze endaweni ukuze usebenzise. Inani ingxenye ye-TakeoffCraft engayifunda liyogxilwa ngomugqa obomvu, futhi ibha yesimo ichaza isizathu, isibonelo "Thayipha inani njengo-12.50."

Chofoza umugqa wento ukuze ugqamise izimaki zayo kuwo wonke amashidi bese uya kushidi elinazo. Lokhu kufana nokuchofoza kuyo ngaphansi kuka-{{ui:Items}}.

## Ngeshidi

Khetha {{ui:By sheet}} ukubona inani lento ngalinye kuleshidi ngalinye, nenani lezimaki. Chofoza umugqa ukuze uvule leshidi nento egqanyisiwe. Sebenzisa lokhu ukuhlola ukuthi wonke amashidi abalwe, noma ukwehlukanisa amanani ngomgangatho noma ngendawo yomsebenzi.

## Ngendawo

Uma ukubala amanani kunezindawo zokunikezela, {{ui:By area}} ibala izinto zendawo ngayinye nesamba esincane. Bona [Izindawo zokunikezela](topic:bid-areas).

## Isexwayiso sokuthi Akuphelele

Uma kukhona okushiywe emasamanisweni, isihloko sibonisa isexwayiso, isibonelo:

"Akuphelele: iphutha elingu-1 lefomula, izimaki ezingu-6 emashidini angenasikali (azifakiwe emasamanisweni)"

- **Amaphutha efomula**: ifomula yengxenye yomhlangano ayikwazi ukusetshenziswa. Umugqa wayo uthi "Iphutha lefomula: …". Bona [Ukuhlangana nefomula](topic:assemblies#when-a-formula-is-wrong).
- **Izimaki emashidini angenasikali**: nika lawo mashidi isikali. Bona [Setha isikali](topic:set-the-scale#why-unscaled-sheets-are-not-counted-as-zero).

Lezi zincazelo zifakwa nasespredshithi ethunyelwe, ngakho isilinganiso sezindleko esingaphelele asisoze sibonakale siphelele.

## Gqamisa into

Chofoza into ngaphansi kuka-{{ui:Items}} noma ethebulini. Izimaki zayo zihlala zingokugcwele kuwo wonke amashidi, nezinye zifiphale. Uhlu lwamashidi lubonisa "(n) egqanyisiwe" kuleshidi ngalinye elinezimaki zayo. Chofoza into futhi ukuze uyeke ukuyigqamisa.

## Amasamanisa ento ngaseceleni

Ngayinye into ohlwini lwe-{{ui:Items}} nayo ibonisa inani layo. Ibhlokhi ethi **Measured** kumhleli wento ibonisa amanani afomula zayo zisebenzisa: ubude, izigijimi nendawo yodonga; indawo, ubude bomphetho nenani lezindawo; noma inani.

## Izimaki kuma-PDF angavulwanga

Ukubala amanani kungafaka izimaki kuma-PDF ongawavulanga kuleli sikhathi. Zisabalwa kuwo wonke amasamanisa; uhlu lwamashidi luqamba la ma-PDF. Bona [Londoloza futhi uvule kabusha ukubala amanani](topic:save-and-reopen).

## Igumbi elengeziwe

Inkinobho yompondo ngakwesokudla kwesihloko se-{{ui:Quantities}} ifihla ithebula ukuze ishidi libe nendawo engaphezu. Chofoza futhi ukuze ubuyisele ithebula.
