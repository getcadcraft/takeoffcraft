Isakhiwo sivame ukuphindeka: amagumbi ehhotela ayi-24 afanayo, izitezi ezi-6 ezivamile, izindawo zokupaka ezingu-40 ezinemigqa efanayo. **Iqembu elivamile** likuvumela ukuthi uhlole elilodwa kwalezo bese uliba izikhathi zalo zokuphindeka, ngaphandle kokudweba amakhophi.

## Yenza iqembu elivamile

1. Hlola iyunithi eyodwa: izindonga zayo, ifulowa layo, izinsimbi zayo; noma yiziphi izinto.
2. Khetha izimaki zayo ngethuluzi {{ui:Khetha}}: chofoza enye, bese {{key:Shift}} + chofoza ezinye ([Khetha izimaki](topic:copy-paste#select-the-marks)).
3. Ngaphansi kwe-**Imaki ekhethiwe**, vula {{ui:Okuvamile}} bese ukhetha {{ui:Iqembu elivamile elisha…}}.
4. Nika igama (`Unit A`) nokuthi liphindeka kangaki (`24`), bese ukhetha {{ui:Yenza iqembu}}.

Yonke inani lalezo zimaki manje libalwa izikhathi ezingu-24: udonga lwe-10 ft lunika i-240 ft, ifulowa le-80 SF linika i-1,920 SF, izimpande ezimbili zinika 48. Izindleko ziyalandela, futhi izifomula zama-assembly zibona izinani eziphindiwe (`N` nayo ibala umugqa ngamunye izikhathi ezingu-24).

Esikrinini, ibhokisi elimpunga lizungeza izimaki zeqembu ngegama nenani lalo, njengo **Unit A ×24**.

## Shintsha kamuva

Amaqembu afakwe ohlwini ngaphansi kwe-**Amaqembu avamile** kwipaneli yesokudla:

- Shintsha inombolo ngemuva kwe-**×** ukuze ushintse ukuthi ibalwa kangaki (1 kuya kwangu-10,000). Yonke inani liyavuselelwa.
- Ngegama libhokisini layo legama, liyashintsha.
- Isithonjana sebhokisi sikhetha izimaki zalo (futhi sibonise ishidi lazo).
- Igaleji ikhipha iqembu. Izimaki zalo zihlala, zibalwa kanye.

Ukuze wengeze izimaki eqenjini noma uzikhiphe, zikhethe bese ukhetha iqembu (noma {{ui:Akuvamile (×1)}}) ngaphansi kwe-{{ui:Okuvamile}}.

## Okufanele ukwazi

- Izimaki zeqembu zingaba kumashidi amaningi, futhi zibe zezinto eziningi. Imaki ngayinye ingaba eqenjini elilodwa kuphela.
- Amakhophi owanamathisela awasebenzi eqenjini: ikhophi eyanamathiselwa yeyunithi evamile ibizobalwa izikhathi ezingu-24 futhi. Sebenzisa iqembu lokuthi "into efanayo N izikhathi", bese ukopisha futhi unamathisele amakhophi odwebayo.
- Izimaki zidwetshwe kanye, ngakho ukubuka kwe-3D nePDF emakwe kubonisa iyunithi eyodwa; amanani, ukuthunyelwa nethebula ngalinye leshidi kubala ngu-×N. Ishidi elithi **Marks** lokuthunyelwa kwe-Excel lisho ukuthi yiziphi izimaki ezivamile nokuthi zibalwa kangaki.
- Ngezindawo zethenda, izimaki zeqembu zibalwa ×N endaweni yazo yethenda.
