Imiyalo ye-TakeoffCraft ifinyelelwa ngendlela efanayo noma ngabe uyifinyelela kanjani. **Isitayela semenyu** sinquma kuphela lapho ihlala khona esikrinini, ukuthi ibizwa ngani khona, nokuthi yiziphi izikhiye ezayisebenzisayo. Khetha esinye ku-{{ui:Settings}} ({{key:Mod+,}}) › {{ui:TakeoffCraft}} › {{ui:Menus, keys and mouse}}; ushintsho luyasebenza ngokushesha, futhi lesi sisekeli sesiphequluli siyakukhumbula.

## Izitayela ezintathu

| Style | What you get |
|---|---|
| {{ui:TakeoffCraft}} | Ibha yamathuluzi yayo: {{ui:File}}, {{ui:Export}}, {{ui:Scale}}, {{ui:Takeoff}}, {{ui:Edit}} kanye {{ui:View}}, nezikhiye zezinhlamvu ezodwa zamathuluzi. Isiqalo. |
| {{ui:Takeoff desk}} | Ibha yemenyu ({{ui:File}}, {{ui:Edit}}, {{ui:View}}, {{ui:Scale}}, {{ui:Takeoff}}, {{ui:Items}}, {{ui:Markup}}, {{ui:Export}}) phezu komugqa owodwa wamathuluzi okubala, njengendlela izinhlelo zokubala ezisekhompyutheni ezihlela ngayo amathuluzi azo. Izinto zibizwa ngokuthi izimo kulo mugqa. Okhiye abafanayo, kanye ne-{{key:N}} yesinto esisha. Izinkomba zesikali ziya ebhareni yeshidi, eduze kwesikali seshidi. |
| {{ui:Takeoff ribbon}} | Amathebhu eribhoni aqanjwe ngomsebenzi: {{ui:Takeoff}} (linganisa, yenza umumo, isikali, kusuka emdwebeni, izimo), {{ui:Edit}}, {{ui:View}}, {{ui:Markup}} kanye ne-{{ui:Estimate}} (incwadi yokusebenza, ama-PDF amakwe, ithenda), nemenyu ye-{{ui:File}} nomugqa osheshayo wokuvula, ukulondoloza, ukuhlehlisa nokuphinda. Amathuluzi osebenzisa usuku lonke ayakhulu, eziseduze zincane futhi zigxunyiwe. Okhiye abafanayo; izinkomba zesikali ziya ebhareni yeshidi. |

Zonke izitayela zintathu zigcina okhiye be-TakeoffCraft: {{key:V}} khetha, {{key:L}} umugqa, {{key:A}} indawo, {{key:O}} isiyingi, {{key:B}} igumbi, {{key:P}} indlela (khetha imigqa ye-PDF), {{key:X}} imbobo, {{key:C}} bala, {{key:S}} ukubala okuzenzakalelayo, {{key:K}} linganisa, {{key:D}} hlola, {{key:R}} imodi yejika, {{key:E}} imodi yokukhipha, {{key:F}} no-{{key:W}} ukulingana, {{key:3}} umbono wokuhlola we-3D, {{key:F3}} ukuncamelana, kanye no-{{key:Mod+Z}}, {{key:Mod+Y}}, {{key:Mod+S}}, {{key:Mod+O}}, {{key:Mod+C}}, {{key:Mod+X}}, {{key:Mod+V}}, {{key:Mod+D}}, {{key:Mod+A}}.

Amathuluzi ebha yeshidi (amanothi, amafu, okuqaqambisiwe, Compare, 3D) alinde kuze kuvulwe i-PDF; uma akhethwa ngaphambi kwalokho, umugqa wesimo uyasho.

:::tip
Ukhiye okungowe-layout yeTakeoffCraft kodwa isitayela sakho sawunikeze umyalo ohlukile akwenzi lutho, nomugqa wesimo utshela ukuthi umyalo wawo uphi ("Kuma-My keys, Count ingu-Ctrl+Alt+C"). {{key:Delete}}, {{key:Esc}}, {{key:Enter}}, {{key:Tab}}, {{key:Space}} nezimpondo zihlala zinencazelo yazo. Uma udweba umugqa noma isakhiwo, unamathisela noma ubuyekeze ukusesha okuzenzakalelayo, izinhlamvu nezinombolo zobude othayiphile zihlala nomdwebo.
:::

## Yenza eyakho

1. Khetha isitayela esiseduze kakhulu nalokho ofuna, bese ucofa {{ui:Customize…}}.
2. Ku-{{ui:Keys and menus}}, thola umyalo (ibhokisi lokusesha liyasiza), bese {{ui:Add a key}} ucindezele ukhiye, noma izikhiye ezimbili ngokulandelana ukuze uthole ukhiye wezinhlamvu ezimbili. Cofa ukhiye ukuze uwususe.
3. Ngebha yemenyu, {{ui:Where it sits}} ihambisa umyalo kwenye imenyu, noma kwi-{{ui:Keys only}}.
4. Ku-{{ui:Mouse}}, setha ivili nezinkinobho ezihambisa ishidi.
5. Yinike igama bese ucofa {{ui:Save}}.

Ukulondoloza isitayela esakhelwe ngaphambili kwenza ikhophi yakho ("My Takeoff desk"); eyakhelwe ngaphambili ayishintshi ngokuphelele. Ukhiye onikezwe imiyalo emibili ubonakala ebomvu futhi kufanele ulungiswe ngaphambi kokuthi ulondoloze.

## Imouse

- **Ivili le-mouse**: {{ui:Scroll up zooms in}} (isiqalo) noma {{ui:Scroll up zooms out}}, nokuthi lisheshe kangakanani. Ukuhlanganisa kwe-trackpad kusondeza njalo ngendlela eminwe yakho ehamba ngayo.
- **Hambisa ishidi nge-**: inkinobho ephakathi, inkinobho yesokudla, noma zombili. Ukudonsa kwesokunxele ephepheni elingenalutho kanye no-{{key:Space}} nokudonsa kushelelisa njalo; ukucofa ngesokudla ngaphandle kokudonsa kuqeda njalo umugqa noma isakhiwo.
- **Donsa kumabhuyu e-3D**: {{ui:Each view's own}} igcina izinkinobho zombono wokuhlola we-3D (zesokunxele nezephakathi zizungeza, zesokudla zishelelisa). Noma misa izinkinobho eziphakathi nezesokudla zizungeze, zishelelise, zisondeze noma zingenzi lutho; {{key:Shift}} nenkinobho iguqula ukuzungeza nokushelelisa. Inkinobho yesokunxele njalo izungeza futhi ikhethe.

## Yithathe nawe

{{ui:Export}} ilondoloza isitayela esisetshenziswayo njengefayela elincane le-`.ccinput.json`; {{ui:Import…}} iyengeza, kusuka kwenye ikhompyutha noma kumngane wakho. Ifayela elingeniswe lingasho imiyalo ye-TakeoffCraft kuphela: okunye kukho kuyasuswa futhi kubalwe kumugqa wesimo. {{ui:Delete this style}} isusa eyakho.

:::note
Lezi zilungiselelo zihlala kulesi sisekeli sesiphequluli. {{ui:Use this style in every app}} ngaphansi kwamakhadi wezitayela yenza ukuthi zonke ii-app zivule esitayelweni sazo esifanayo (ibha yamathuluzi yazo, ibha yemenyu noma iribhoni), nezinguquko ozenzile kwi-app ngayinye. I-TakeoffCraft ayinaso isitayela esinomugqa womyalo: uma kukhethwa esinye sezinhlelo zonke, i-TakeoffCraft igcina ibha yayo yamathuluzi.

Iribhoni yokubala ({{ui:Takeoff ribbon}}) iyiribhoni yesakhiwo se-{{ui:Ribbon and project browser}} ({{ui:Settings}} › {{ui:Global}} › {{ui:Layout}}): bona [Isikrini se-TakeoffCraft](topic:workspace).
:::
