Isethi yamapulani ivame ukuba yi-PDF eziningi: ezakhiwo, ezesakhiwo, ezomshini. I-TakeoffCraft izivula ndawonye njengohlu olulodwa lwamashidi, futhi ukubala okulodwa kungafaka zonke.

## Vula ama-PDF

1. Khetha {{ui:Vula amapulani}} kweqembu {{ui:Ifayela}}, noma cindezela {{key:Mod+O}}. (Ezinye izitayela zemenyu zibiza ngokuthi {{ui:Vula ama-PDF…}}.)
2. Ku-dayalogi yefayela, khetha yonke i-PDF yesethi. Bamba {{pick:mac=Command|windows=Ctrl|linux=Ctrl}} ukuze ukhethe eziningi, noma {{key:Shift}} ukuze ukhethe uhla.
3. Khetha Vula.
   Umlayezo othi "Ivula (igama)…" ubonakala lapho ifayela ngalinye lifundwa. Bese wonke amakhasi avela ngaphansi kwe-{{ui:Amashidi}}, nekhasi lokuqala le-PDF entsha yokuqala livulwe.

Ungawuhudula ufake ama-PDF esuka ku-{{pick:mac=Finder|windows=File Explorer|linux=your file manager}} bese uwawisa noma kuphi kwi-TakeoffCraft. Uhlaka oluthi "Drop PDFs, DWG or DXF drawings, or a .takeoff.json" luvela ngenkathi uhudula.

Ukuze wengeze ama-PDF amanye kamuva, yenza okufanayo futhi. Ama-PDF asevele evulekile ayadlulwa.

Isikrini sokuqala siphinde sibonise ama-PDF nokubala okuvulwe kamuva, kumabhrawuza avumela ikhasi ukuvula ifayela futhi (i-Chrome ne-Edge). Chofoza eyodwa ukuyivula; ibhrawuza lingacela imvume kuqala. Bona [Amafayela akho](common:your-files).

:::note
Akulayishwa lutho. Ibhrawuza lifunda ama-PDF kukhompyutha yakho, futhi ayanyamalala ku-TakeoffCraft uma uvala ithebhu noma ukhetha {{ui:Entsha}}.
:::

## Imidwebo ecaleni kwama-PDF

{{ui:Vula amapulani}} ithatha futhi imidwebo ye-DWG ne-DXF, yodwa noma nama-PDF: isikhala semodeli nezakhiwo zomdwebo ngamunye ziba ngamashidi ohlwini olulodwa. Bona [Vula umdwebo we-DWG noma we-DXF](topic:open-a-drawing), bese [Bala kumdwebo](topic:count-from-a-drawing).

## I-PDF edluliselwe kolunye uhlelo

Enye i-app yalolu hlelo ingakunikeza i-PDF eyayivulwe kuyo: ivula i-TakeoffCraft kuthebhu elisha futhi i-PDF ivula khona njengoba uyiwisile. Ibha yesimo ithi "Ivuliwe (igama), idluliselwe kusuka ku-(app). Ifayela lokuqala alishintshiwe." Akulayishwa lutho; ifayela lidlula kusitoreji sale bhrawuza futhi liyanyamalala kuso lapho livuleka.

Uma ibha yesimo ithi i-PDF "ayifikanga", ithebhu ilinde isikhathi eside, noma i-PDF isivele ivuliwe kwenye ithebhu: yivule nge-{{ui:Vula amapulani}}.

Okuphambene: uma i-MarkupCraft isebenza lapho ukhona futhi ingathatha ifayela, ibha yeshidi ngaphansi komdwebo inenkinobho ye-{{ui:MarkupCraft}}: ivula i-PDF yeshidi elikuskrini ku-MarkupCraft, kuthebhu elisha, ukuze uyibuyekeze futhi uyimake.

## Thola ishidi

- Skrola uhlu lwe-{{ui:Amashidi}} ngakwesobunxele bese uchofoza ishidi.
- Bhala ku-**Sesha amashidi** phezulu kohlu ukuze uhlunge ngenombolo yeshidi, igama lefayela noma ilebula yekhasi. Inani eliseduze ne-{{ui:Amashidi}} likhombisa "3 kwangu-40" uma isihlungi sivuliwe.
- Cindezela {{key:PageDown}} ishidi elilandelayo kanye {{key:PageUp}} elidlule. Zilandela uhlu njengoba luhlungiwe.

:::mac
Ku-khibhodi ye-Mac engenazo izinkinobho ze-Page Up ne-Page Down, cindezela fn nomcibisholo ophezulu noma ophansi.
:::

## Ukuqanjwa kwamashidi

- Ikhasi le-PDF elinelebula, njengo `A-101`, liqanjwa ngalelo lebula.
- Ikhasi elingenalebula (noma ilebula okuyinombolo yekhasi kuphela) liqanjwa "(igama lefayela), ikhasi (n)".
- Uma kuvulwe ama-PDF amaningi, amashidi anamalebula aphinde athwale igama lefayela: "A-101 — Architectural".

Amagama amashidi awakwazi ukubhalwa ngesandla okwamanje.

## Hamba ezungeze ishidi

- Phendula ivili le-mouse ukuze usondeze ngasendaweni yesikhombi.
- Donsa ngebhathi lesobunxele noma eliphakathi ukuze ushelelise. Nge-{{ui:Ukubala okuzenzakalelayo}}, noma uma uqala ekhoneni lephoyinti lamamaki, bamba {{key:Space}} udonse, noma donsa ngebhathi eliphakathi.
- Cindezela {{key:F}} noma khetha {{ui:Lingana nekhasi}} ukubona lonke ishidi; {{key:W}} noma {{ui:Lingana nobubanzi}} ilingana nobubanzi balo.
- Cindezela {{key:Plus}} noma {{key:=}} ukuze usondeze, {{key:Minus}} ukuze wehlise isondezo, maphakathi nombono.

Ishidi ngalinye likhumbula isondezo nendawo yalo ngenkathi livulekile.
