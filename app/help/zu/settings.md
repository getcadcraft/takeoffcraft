Vula {{ui:Settings}} ngegiya ebhala lokusebenza noma ngo-{{key:Mod+,}}. Ithebhu yokuqala, {{ui:TakeoffCraft}}, ibamba izilungiselelo zalolu hlelo; {{ui:General}} ibamba ezabelwana yiyo yonke i-CadCraft (itimu, amaphaneli, ibha yamathuluzi). Izilungiselelo ze-TakeoffCraft zikhona kuhlelo kuphela: ziguqulwa lapha, futhi lesi siphequluli siyazikhumbula. {{ui:Reset TakeoffCraft settings}} ziyabuyisela ekuqaleni.

## Amayunithi amafiti namayintshi

Bonisa ubude kuya eduze kwe-**1/16"** (okokuqala), **1/8"** noma **1/4"**. Lokhu kushintsha indlela ubude obufundeka ngayo eshidini, emaphaneli, emananini nasebhalini lesimo.

Ukubonisa kuzungezwa kuphela. Amanani, izindleko nokuthunyelwa kubalwa ngobude obunembile, futhi ukuthunyelwa kubonisa njalo u-1/16".

## Ukulahleka okuqalayo

Iphesenti lokulahleka ngalinye into entsha eqala ngalo: 0% (okokuqala), 2%, 3%, 5%, 7.5%, 10%, 15% noma 20%. Usakwazi ukuthayipha ukulahleka okunye ngento ngayinye, kumhleli wento noma ngqo kuthebula lamanani. Izinto osezinazo zigcina ukulahleka kwazo.

## Auto count: lapho isilayidi sokufana siqala khona

Lapho isilayidi se-{{ui:Match}} sihlala khona ngesikhathi ukusesha kwe-[Auto count](topic:auto-count) kuphela, kusukela kuma-50% kuya kuma-95%; 75% kuyokuqala. Okuphansi kunikeza abafanayo abaningi ukuze ubenqabe; okuphezulu kunikeza abambalwa. Usakwazi ukuhambisa isilayidi ngesikhathi sokubuyekeza ngasinye.

## Bambelela emigqeni yedrowing

Ivulekile ekuqaleni. Ngesikhathi ulinganisa noma ulinganisa, indawo eseduze komugqa we-PDF iyagxumela esiphethweni sawo, eyiphakathi, kumaphuzu okuhlangana noma endaweni eseduze kuwo. {{key:F3}} no-{{ui:Snap}} ebhalweni lesimo nazo ziyakuvula noma zikucime. Okwengeziwe: [Bambelela emigqeni yedrowing](topic:snap-to-the-drawing).

## Room fill: ububanzi bokuvala obuvalwa

{{ui:Room fill closes openings up to}} isetha ukuthi umnyango noma igebe elibanzi kangakanani okuthathwa ithuluzi le-{{ui:Room}} njengelivaliwe: 3'-0" kuya ku-8'-0", 4'-0" ekuqaleni. Okubanzi kunomnyango wakho omkhulu kakhulu kuvumela igumbi lidlule kelinye; okuncane kunomnyango kushiya igumbi livulekile. Bona [Gcwalisa igumbi ngokuchofoza kanye](topic:measure-areas#fill-a-room-with-one-click).

## Bambelela ku-45° ngaphandle kokubamba i-Shift

Icimile ekuqaleni. Uma ivuliwe, imigqa, izinkomba, ukusikwa nezikhona ezidonsiwe ziya ku-0°, 45°, 90° noma 135° kusukela esiphethweni sokugcina ngaphandle kokubamba {{key:Shift}}; bamba {{key:Shift}} ukuchofoza ngeyiphi ingosi. Ibha yesimo ifunda "Ibambelela ku-45°; Shift ikhulula ingosi". Indawo ochofoza kuyo echibini lesiyingi nemibhoxo ayisoze ibanjwe; isiphetho sesiyingi siyabanjwa.

## Usayizi wophawu lokubala

Amachashaza okubala akhulu kangakanani: {{ui:Extra small}}, {{ui:Small}}, {{ui:Medium}} (okokuqala: usayizi amachashaza ayehlale enawo) noma {{ui:Large}}. Usayizi uhlala esikrinini kuzo zonke izinga lokukhulisa, futhi i-PDF emakiwe idweba ichashaza ngalinye ngosayizi ofanayo ephepheni, noma ngabe ishidi likhulu kangakanani, ngakho i-PDF ku-100% ibukeka njengesikrini. Okwengeziwe: [Bala ngesandla](topic:count-by-hand#dot-size-on-screen-and-in-the-pdf).

## Isikhombi sekhona

Ye-[ukubuka kwe-3D ukuhlola](topic:check-in-3d) kuphela: {{ui:View cube}} (okokuqala: ikhyubhu ekhoneni eliphezulu kwesokudla lokuchofoza, lokudonsa nokujikeza), {{ui:Axis icon}} (uphawu olunkulu lwe-X, Y, Z ekhoneni elingezansi kwesokudla) noma {{ui:None}}. Ishintsha ngokushesha futhi igcinwa kulesi siphequluli. Ishidi elilingeneyo alisoze libonise lutho.

## Ithuluzi lendlela: igebe elikhulu kakhulu lokuhlanganisa

{{ui:Path tool: largest gap to join}} kuyisimo sokuthi imiphetho yemigqa emibili ingaba kanye kangakanani futhi ibaleke njengendlela eyodwa, ngamaphuwenti e-PDF (0.05 kuya ku-60; 1.5 ekuqaleni). Imigqa yedrowing ivame ukuyeka ingasaphelele eduze komunye. Okukhulu kuhlanganisa kakhulu; okuncane kugcina imigqa ihlukene. {{key:[}} no-{{key:]}} ziyayishintsha ngenkathi ukhetha, futhi lolu hlu luyalandela. Bona [Khetha imigqa](topic:pick-the-lines).
