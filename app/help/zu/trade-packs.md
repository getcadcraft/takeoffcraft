**Iphakhi lokuhweba** yisethi yezinto ezilungele ukusetshenziswa zokuhweba eyodwa: ngayinye inayo iyunithi yayo, ubude noma ukujula kwayo, ukulahleka kwayo, kanye ne-**assembly** yayo, izingxenye nezifomula ezishintsha umugqa, indawo noma inani elilinganisiwe libe izinto zokwakha. Udonga lwe-drywall, isibonelo, lulethe ireyili, izinkonzo, amashidi, izikulufo, ithephu kanye ne-compound.

Amaphakhi angezwa ku-[umtapo wamanani](topic:price-library), futhi kusuka lapho wengeza izinto zawo kunoma yimuphi umbalo.

## Engeza iphakhi

1. Vula {{ui:Umtapo}} phezulu kwepaneli {{ui:Izinto}}, bese {{ui:Amaphakhi wokuhweba…}}.
2. Khetha {{ui:Amayunithi}}: imperial (LF, SF), metric (m, m²) noma zombili. Yonke i-assembly iza kuzo zombili izinhlelo.
3. Khetha {{ui:Engeza}} eduze kwephakhi.

Izinto zephakhi zivela emtapweni, zibekwe ithegi ngegama lephakhi. Ukuzisebenzisa, zikhethe emtapweni bese ukhetha {{ui:Engeza kumsebenzi}}.

:::note
**Iphakhi alilethi amanani.** Izinto zalo zibonisa "set your rate" emtapweni. Amazinga angokwakho: zibhale emtapweni noma ku-item editor, noma ulethe uhlu lwentengo lwakho njenge-CSV usebenzise {{ui:Buyekeza izintengo}}.
:::

Uma usuvele unento efanayo ngegama nangohlobo emtapweni wakho, iphakhi lishiya eyakho njengoba kunjalo. Ukungeza kabusha iphakhi kuthatha indawo yezinto zalo ngenguqulo entsha.

## Susa iphakhi

Ngaphakathi kwe-{{ui:Amaphakhi wokuhweba…}}, khetha {{ui:Susa}} eduze kwephakhi oliwengezile, bese kanye futhi ukuqinisekisa. Yonke into eyavela kulo ishiya umtapo ngesinyathelo esisodwa. Izinto osezengeziwe embalweni wokubala zihlala kuleyo mbala.

## Okusemphakhini

Khetha {{ui:Imithombo nemininingwane}} ngaphansi kwephakhi ukubona ukuthi yayibhalwe kuphi ngayinye i-assembly: amakhodi wokwakha, izindinganiso namahhovisi emibutho yokuhweba, nemihlahlandlela yokufaka. Ku-umtapo, bamba iphoyinti phezu kwento ukuze ufunde imithombo nezimo zayo, njengesikhala sezinkonzo noma izinga lokusakaza lopende. Hlola lokho ngomsebenzi wakho nedatha yemikhiqizo yakho, bese ushintsha izifomula lapho zingafani (bheka [Ama-assembly nezifomula](topic:assemblies)).

Yonke iphakhi imakwe **Starter: not reviewed by an estimator yet** kuze kube yilapho abalinganisi abasebenzayo sebeliphenyile.

Yonke into iphinde ithwale inombolo yayo ye-CSI MasterFormat, njengo `09 21 16`.

## Yabelana ngomtapo wakho njengephakhi

{{ui:Thumela njengephakhi…}} emtapweni iyawulondoloza njengefayela lephakhi (`.ccpack.json`) okungeziwa yinoma ubani nge-{{ui:Vula ifayela lephakhi…}}. Ngokuzenzakalelayo ishiya izinto ezivela emaphakhini owengezile, futhi ayinawo amanani. Khetha {{ui:Faka amazinga ami njengamazinga wesampula}} ukuthumela namazinga akho: aphuma enelebula yesifunda nosuku owunikezayo, futhi noma ngubani owengeza iphakhi uyakhetha ukuthi uwasebenzise yini.

Amafayela ephakhi ayidatha evamile, hhayi izinhlelo. I-TakeoffCraft ihlola yonke ingxenye yefayela ngaphambi kokuyisebenzisa futhi ishiye noma yini engayifundi.
