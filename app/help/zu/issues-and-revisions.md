## Okuzokwenza

Sebenza ngomlando wezikhishwa ofanayo nalowo wonke ithimba. Bona ukuthi yimaphi amashidi aphume ngayiphi inguqulo, bese ugcina okwenzeka esayithini ngokuxhumana nesikhishwa okuphethe kuso.

## Izinyathelo

1. Vula **Izikhishwa nezinguqulo**.
2. Khetha **Funda umlando wezikhishwa** bese ukhetha ifayela `.issues.json` elilondoloziwe ku-BimCraft noma kwenye i-app. Izikhishwa nezirekhodi zalo ziyengezwa kowakho. Akukho okusulwayo.
3. Khetha **Engeza irekhodi** lomyalo wesayithi, idwebo lokukhiqiza, isethi yezimaka, imodeli exhunyiwe noma inothi. Khetha isikhishwa okuphethe kuso nesimo sayo. Ifayela olikhethile liqoshwa ngegama lalo, usayizi kanye ne-SHA-256; ifayela lihlala lapho lisemi khona.
4. **Ilogi yokushintsha komdwebo** ibonisa umugqa owodwa weshidi ngalinye nekholomu eyodwa yesikhishwa ngasinye. Inguqulo ephakathi kwamabhulakethi ikhishwe futhi ayishintshiwe.
5. **Landa ilogi yokushintsha (CSV)** iyalondoloza izikhishwa, ithebula leshidi kanye namarekhodi. **Londoloza umlando wezikhishwa** ibhala ifayela ezinye ii-app ezilifundayo.

## Okuhle ukukwazi

- **Rekhoda isikhishwa** sikhipha amashidi wakho alinganisiwe, isibonelo nge-uhlu lwamanani. **Izinguquko kusukela** bese kutshela ukuthi yiziphi amashidi alinganiswe kabusha emva kwalesosikhishwa.
- Umlando ulondolozwa ku-takeoff file. Lowo nalowo mshintsho uyi-{{ui:Undo}} eyodwa.
- Ukufunda ifayela elifanayo kabili akungezi lutho kabili. Uma amakhophi amabili enerekhodi elifanayo, elisha liyagcinwa.
