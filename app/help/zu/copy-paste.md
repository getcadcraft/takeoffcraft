Iqembu le-{{ui:Hlela}} lethuluzi libamba {{ui:Hlehlisa}}, {{ui:Phinda wenze}}, {{ui:Kopisha}} kanye ne-{{ui:Namathisela}}. Imenyu yalo ethi {{ui:Okunye}} inezinye, nokhiye bayo: {{ui:Sika}}, {{ui:Kopisha}}, {{ui:Namathisela}}, {{ui:Namathisela endaweni}}, {{ui:Phinda}}, {{ui:Susa}}, {{ui:Khetha konke}} kanye ne-{{ui:Khetha lutho}}. Konke lokhu kunesinyathelo esisodwa sokuhlehlisa nge-{{key:Mod+Z}}.

## Khetha izimpawu

1. Khetha {{ui:Khetha}} ({{key:V}}) bese uchofoza impawu.
2. Bamba {{key:Shift}} uchofoze ezinye izimpawu ukuze uzengeze. {{key:Shift}} + chofoza impawu ekhethiwe iyayisusa futhi.
3. Noma cindezela {{key:Mod+A}} ({{ui:Khetha konke}}) ukuthatha zonke izimpawu eshidini elivulekile.

Uma kukhethwe izimpawu eziningi, iphaneli esokudla ibonisa **Izimpawu ezikhethiwe**: zingaki zento ngayinye, nezinkinobho ze-{{ui:Kopisha}}, {{ui:Phinda}}, {{ui:Sika}} kanye ne-{{ui:Susa izimpawu}}. Izibambi zamakhona zivela kuphela uma kukhethwe impawu eyodwa. {{key:Esc}} ({{ui:Khetha lutho}}) ucisha ukukhetha.

## Kopisha namathisela lapho uchofoza

1. Khetha izimpawu bese ucindezela {{key:Mod+C}} ({{ui:Kopisha}}). Izimpawu zikopishwa nezinto zazo, kuhlanganise nomhlanganisela wento ngayinye, ukulahleka nezindleko.
2. Iya eshidini ofuna ukuzibeka kulo (lingaba yishidi elifanayo).
3. Cindezela {{key:Mod+V}} ({{ui:Namathisela}}). Ikhophi eyisicaba-ngamachashaza ilandela isikhombi.
4. Chofoza lapho okufanele khona. Maphakathi ezimpawu ezikopishiwe afika esikhombini.

{{key:Esc}} ikhansela futhi ayengezi lutho. Izimpawu ezinamathiselwe zikhethwa ngemva kwalokho, ngakho {{key:Delete}} noma {{key:Mod+Z}} kuzisusa futhi.

Ku-takeoff efanayo, izimpawu ezinamathiselwe zihamba kwi-**izinto ezifanayo**, ngakho amanani azo engeza kulezo zinto. Kwenye takeoff (ithebhu enye, noma ngemva kwe-{{ui:Entsha}}), kusetshenziswa into enegama nohlobo olufanayo; ngaphandle kwalokho into yengezwa nomhlanganisela wayo. Uma igama selithathwe yinto yohlobo oluhlukile, into engeziwe ibizwa ngokuthi "(igama) (ikhophi)".

Izimpawu ezinamathiselwe zihamba endaweni yezindleko ekhethwe phezulu ephaneli ye-{{ui:Izinto}} (noma kungekho). Bona [Izindawo zezindleko](topic:bid-areas).

## Namathisela endaweni

{{key:Mod+Shift+V}} ({{ui:Namathisela endaweni}}) ibeka ikhophi endaweni efanayo ngqo eshidini elivulekile, ngokuma kwayo ekhasini. Yisebenzise ifloro elijwayelekile: bala izinga 2, kopisha, vula izinga 3, namathisela endaweni.

Namathisela endaweni udinga ishidi **elifanayo ngosayizi** nelakopishwa kulo. Kushidi losayizi ohlukile i-TakeoffCraft iyatshela futhi ayinamathisi lutho; sebenzisa {{ui:Namathisela}} uchofoze. Eshidini elakopishwa kulo, ikhophi ifika phezu kwezimpawu zokuqala.

## Isikali kufanele sifane

Ubude nezindawo kuncike esikalini seshidi. Umugqa okala u-10' ngo-1/4" = 1'-0" wawuzokala u-20' eshidini elingu-1/8" = 1'-0". Ngakho i-TakeoffCraft inamathisela imigqa nezindawo kuphela eshidini elinesikali **esifanayo** neshidi elavela kulo, futhi iyatshela uma lingenjalo:

- Ishidi elivulekile alinasikali okwamanje: linike okokuqala ([Setha isikali](topic:set-the-scale)), bese unamathisela.
- Ishidi elivulekile linesikali esehlukile: umlayezo ubiza zombili izikali. Namathisela eshidini elinesikali elifanayo.
- Izinani azidingi sikali: izinani ezikopishiwe zinamathiselwa noma kuphi.

## Phinda

{{key:Mod+D}} ({{ui:Phinda}}) ikopisha izimpawu ezikhethiwe ibeke ikhophi esikhombini ngokushesha; chofoza lapho okufanele khona. Akukho okungezwa kuze uchofoze, futhi {{key:Esc}} akushiyi lutho. Ukuphinda akushintshi lokho {{ui:Namathisela}} alunamathisela.

## Sika nokususa

- {{key:Mod+X}} ({{ui:Sika}}) ikopisha izimpawu ezikhethiwe bese iyazisusa. Zinamathisele lapho zifanele, noma uhlehlise ukusika.
- {{key:Delete}} noma {{key:Backspace}} ({{ui:Susa}}) isusa izimpawu ezikhethiwe ngaphandle kokuzikopisha.

## Kopisha into

Umhleli wento (ngaphansi kwe-**Izinto**) uphela ngalokhu:

- {{ui:Kopisha into}}: ikopisha into nomhlanganisela wayo, ukulahleka nezindleko, ngaphandle kwezimpawu. {{ui:Namathisela}} bese yengeza ikhophi yayo kule takeoff, noma kwenye.
- {{ui:Phinda into}}: yengeza ikhophi ngokushesha, ibizwa ngokuthi "(igama) (ikhophi)".

:::tip
Ikhophi ihlala kulo kompyutha: igcinwa kule thebhu nasesitoreji sesiphequluli, ukuze ithebhu enye ye-TakeoffCraft ikwazi ukunamathisela. Akulayishwa lutho, futhi i-clipboard yesistimu ayisetshenziswa, ngakho awukwazi ukunamathisela izimpawu ezinhlelweni zanoma yini.
:::

:::note
Ikhophi ivela kishidi elilodwa ngesikhathi. Izimpawu zinamathiselwa njengoba zinjalo: akukakhona ukujikeleza noma ukuzibonisa ikhophi.
:::
