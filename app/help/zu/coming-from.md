Uma usuke wenza ukubala amanani nge-PlanSwift noma ngezamathuluzi okulinganisa e-Bluebeam Revu, iningi le-TakeoffCraft lizokuzwakala lijwayelekile: isethi yamapulani, isikali ngeshidi ngalinye, izinto ozilinganisayo, nesamba esibalwe ngamanani. Indlela izingxenye ezihlanganiswa ngayo ihluke kwezinye izindawo, nalezo zindawo zilandelayo.

Uma wazi i-PlanSwift noma i-On-Screen Takeoff, khetha isitayela semenyu {{ui:Ideskithophu yokubala}} ku-{{ui:Izilungiselelo}} › {{ui:TakeoffCraft}} › {{ui:Imenyu, okhiye nemouse}}: ibha yemenyu enoFayela, Hlela, Ukubuka, Isikali, Ukubala amanani, Izinto, Ukumaka kanye ne-Thumela phezu komugqa wamathuluzi okubala amanani, nezilungiselelo zesikali ebhalweni leshidi. Bona [Imenyu, okhiye nemouse](topic:menus-keys-and-mouse).

## Umehluko odida abantu

**Ukubala amanani akusoze kube ngaphakathi kwe-PDF yakho.** Akukho okwenzayo okushintsha ama-PDF akho. Londoloza ifayela le-`.takeoff.json` ugcine lona eduze kwemidwebo. Ukuthumela omunye umuntu i-PDF emakwe akumthumeli ukubala amanani okuhleleka: thumela ifayela lephrojekthi namaPDF oqobo, noma i-[I-PDF enomsebenzi ongaphakathi](topic:save-and-reopen#keep-the-takeoff-inside-the-pdf).

**Ama-PDF ahlanganiswa ngokuqukethwe, hhayi ngegama.** Ishidi elibuyekeziwe elilondolozwe phezu kwegama lefayela elidala liyifayela elisha ku-TakeoffCraft, elingenasikali nezimpawu. Izimpawu zakho ezindala zihlala kunguqulo endala futhi ziqhubeka zibalwa. Bona [Londoloza uphinde uvule ukubala amanani](topic:save-and-reopen#when-a-pdf-has-changed).

**Izimpawu ezingenasikali azishiywa ngaphandle, azibalwa njengo-zero.** Uma ishidi lingenasikali, imigqa nezindawo zalo azibalwa, nethebula lithi "Akuphelele". Emaphrogramu amaningi wawuzobona u-0 kuphela. Bona [Setha isikali](topic:set-the-scale#why-unscaled-sheets-are-not-counted-as-zero).

**Akukho ukuqagela usayizi wephepha.** Okusethiwe ngaphambili kuthatha ukuthi i-PDF iphrintwe ngosayizi ogcwele. I-PDF engosayizi wengxenye noma e-"fit to page" idinga {{ui:Linganisa}}. Sebenzisa {{ui:Hlola}} njalo.

**Into eyodwa, uhlobo oluyinhloko olulodwa, eminingi uma ucela.** Into iyumugqa, indawo noma inani, futhi uhlobo lwayo luyavaleka uma seluneziimpawu. Imo enikeza indawo nomgangatho wayo ingumbandela wendawo: umgangatho wayo uyinguqukayo `P`. Udonga olunobude nendawo luyinto yomugqa enokuphakama. Ngento enezimo eziningi, njengegumbi elinephansi, ibhezi nezimnyango, khetha ezinye izinhlobo ngaphansi kwe-**{{ui:Futhi ukale}}**: ngayinye ibhalwa ngemugqa wayo. Bona [Into eyodwa, izinhlobo eziningi zezimpawu](topic:create-items#one-item-several-kinds-of-marks).

**Amafomula asebenza ngamasamba.** Amafomula omhlanganisela asebenzisa amasamba ento ezindaweni zonke, hhayi umugqa ngamunye ngokwawo. I-`N` (inani lemigqa noma lezindawo) ikhona ngezengezo zomugqa ngamunye, njengesitudi esengeziwe ngodonga ngalunye.

**Umgangatho uhlanganisa imbobo.** I-`P` yendawo yengeza imiphetho yezimbobo zayo. Bona [Linganisa izindawo ezinembobo](topic:measure-areas#read-the-area).

**Ukudonsa kushelelisa.** Ukudonsa ngenkinobho yesokunxele eshidini kushelelisa, kuwo wonke amathuluzi ngaphandle kwe-{{ui:Ukubala okuzenzakalelayo}}. Akudingeki ushintshe ube uthuluzi lokushelelisa. Ivili lisondeza; ayisikroli.

**Ikhophi yokubuyisa, hhayi i-cloud.** I-TakeoffCraft igcina ikhophi yomsebenzi wakho kulesi siphequluli futhi iwubuyisele ngemva kokuhlehla ([Uma ithebhu noma isiphequluli simisa](topic:save-and-reopen#if-the-tab-or-the-browser-stops)), kodwa ukubala amanani kuwe ukulondoloza: {{key:Mod+S}}. Ukulondoloza ku-cloud akukatholakali okwamanje.

**Konke kusesiphequluli.** Akukho okufakwayo, namapulani awakaze alayishwe. Ngohlelo lwe-desktop, i-TakeoffCraft itholakala ku-CadCraft Studio, namanye ama-app ayisikhombisa.
