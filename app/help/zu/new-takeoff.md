{{ui:New}} ivala yonke i-PDF bese iqala ukubala okungenalutho ekhasini elifanayo.

1. Londoloza ukubala kwamanje uma ufuna ukukugcina: {{ui:Save takeoff}} noma {{key:Mod+S}}.
2. Khetha {{ui:New}} eqenjini le-{{ui:File}}.
3. Uma kukhona izinguquko ezingalondoloziwe, i-TakeoffCraft ibuza **Unsaved takeoff**: khetha {{ui:Save takeoff first}} (ilanda iphrojekthi, bese isula), {{ui:Discard changes}}, noma {{ui:Cancel}}.
   Iwindi libuyela esimweni salo esingenalutho: akukho ma-PDF, akukho zinto, akukho zimaki, akukho zikali. Ibha yesimo ithi "Ukubala okusha. Vula ama-PDF ukuze uqale."

Okusulwayo:

- yonke i-PDF evuliwe, amashidi nezithonjana zawo,
- izinto, izinhlanganisela, izimaki nezikali,
- izoom nendawo yeshidi ngayinye,
- umlando wokuhlehlisa (awukwazi ukuhlehlisa i-{{ui:New}}),
- imodi yejika, nanoma yiluphi usesho lokubala okuzenzakalelayo noma ukubuyekeza.

Amafayela akho ediskini awathintwa. Ukuze uthole kabusha ukubala kwakudala, vula i-`.takeoff.json` yakho kanye ne-PDF: bona [Londoloza futhi uvule kabusha ukubala](topic:save-and-reopen).

:::note
Ukubala okusha ngakunye kuqala ngaphandle kwezinto. Ukuze uzise izinto, zikopishe kuqala: {{ui:Copy item}} ngaphansi kwe-{{ui:Items}} (noma kopisha izimaki, ezithwele izinto zazo), bese {{ui:Paste}} ekubaleni okusha. Bona [Kopisha, namathisela futhi phinda izimaki](topic:copy-paste). Izinto osebenzisa emsebenzini wonke, zigcine ku-[umtapo wentengo](topic:price-library) wakho bese uzengeza kuwo, noma engeza [iphakethe lomkhakha](topic:trade-packs).
:::
