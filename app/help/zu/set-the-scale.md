Ishidi ngalinye linesikali salo. Wena uyasisetha, noma uvumela i-TakeoffCraft ukuthiilifunde emibhalweni yamashidi (ngezansi), bese uhlola. Kuze kube yilapho ishidi linaso, {{ui:Linear}}, {{ui:Area}}, {{ui:Circle}} no-{{ui:Cut-out}} zenqaba ukulinganisa kulo. {{ui:Count}} no-{{ui:Auto count}} zisebenza ngaphandle kwesikali.

Uhlu lwamashidi lubonisa isikali ngasinye, noma **No scale**. Ibha ngaphansi kweshidi elivulekile ikhuluma okufanayo.

## Sebenzisa okusethiwe ngaphambili (uma i-PDF iphrintwe ngobukhulu obugcwele)

1. Vula ishidi.
2. Vula uhlu {{ui:Preset}} eqenjini le-{{ui:Scale}}.
3. Khetha isikali esiphrintiwe kudrowing, isibonelo `1/8" = 1'-0"` ngaphansi kwe-Architectural.
   Ibha yesimo ithi "Isikali salo shidi: 1/8" = 1'-0"."
4. Hlola (ngezansi).

Okusethiwe ngaphambili kuthatha ukuthi i-PDF yisayizi yangempela yephepha edrowing yenziwelwe yona. Uma othile enze i-PDF nge-"fit to page", noma kuyisethi esiyingxenye-yesibili, okusethiwe ngaphambili akulungile. Linganisa esikhundleni salokho.

Uhlu olugcwele kukhona ku-[Okusethiwe ngaphambili kwesikali](topic:scale-presets).

## Funda izikali emibhalweni yamashidi

Idrowing eningi isho isikali sayo: `SCALE: 1/4" = 1'-0"` ebhokisini lesihloko, `1:100` ngaphansi kwesibonakalo. I-TakeoffCraft ingakufunda futhi isethe wonke amashidi ngesikhathi esisodwa.

1. Vula uhlu {{ui:Preset}} bese ukhetha {{ui:From the sheets' text…}} (phezulu).
2. Uhlu lubonisa wonke amashidi: isikali sawo manje, nokutshiwo umbhalo wawo — **Says 1/4" = 1'-0"**, **No scale in its text** noma **Says not to scale** (NTS).
3. Amashidi angenasikali okhombisa elinye athiswa. Thisa noma susa noma yiliphi, bese ukhetha {{ui:Set the ticked scales}}. Ukucindezela {{key:Mod+Z}} kukodwa kubuyisela konke.
4. Hlola ukuhlukaniswa kunye ngalinye ishidini nge-{{ui:Check}} (ngezansi): okusethiwe ngaphambili kulungile kuphela uma i-PDF iphrintwe ngobukhulu obugcwele.

Ifunda izinhlobo zokubhala zokwakha (`3/16" = 1'-0"`, `1 1/2" = 1'-0"`), zezinjiniyeli (`1" = 20'`) nezemethrikhi (`1:50`) ezihambisana [nokusethiwe ngaphambili](topic:scale-presets). Uma ishidi lisho izikali ezingaphezu kwelilodwa (ipulani enemininingwane emikhulu), ithatha leyo elisho kakhulu, futhi eyabhalwa ngemva kwegama elithi SCALE ibalwa kabili; ezinye ziboniswa ngombala obomvu ukuze uzihlole. Ishidi eliskenisiwe alinambhalo: lisethe ngesandla.

## Linganisa ngobukhulu obaziwayo

Sebenzisa lokhu uma ungakholwa isikali esiphrintiwe, uma isethi ishintshiwe usayizi, noma ishidi lithi "not to scale" kodwa linobukhulu.

1. Vula ishidi bese ukhulisa ibukhulu elide oludlule kahle, njengendawo yegridi noma ubukhulu bakhiwo bonke. Lilide, lilinganiseka kakhulu.
2. Khetha {{ui:Calibrate}} eqenjini le-{{ui:Scale}}, noma cindezela {{key:K}}.
3. Chofoza ukugcina kwesinye isiphetho sobukhulu. Khulisa kuso sonke isiphetho ukuze unembe; ungakhulisa nokuhambisa phakathi kokuchofoza.
4. Chofoza esinye isiphetho. Bamba {{key:Shift}} ukugcina umugqa ku-0°, 45° noma 90°.
   Ingxoxo **Calibrate this sheet** iyavula.
5. Thayipha ibanga langempela ku-**Real distance**, isibonelo `24'-6 1/2"`, `24.5` (amafiti ayizinhlamvu), `30"` noma `7.5 m`. Bona [Ukufakwa kobude, irethi nephesenti](topic:entry-formats).
6. Khetha {{ui:Set the scale}}.
   Ibha yesimo ibonisa isikali njenge "1" = (n)'", uma sisezinhlamvu ezingu-0.5% kokusethiwe ngaphambili ithi yiliphi, isibonelo "elihambisana ne-1/4" = 1'-0" (khetha leso sethingo ukuze uthole isikali esinembile)".
   I-TakeoffCraft ibuyela kuthuluzi le-{{ui:Select}}.

Uma ukulinganisa kuhambisana nokusethiwe ngaphambili, ukukhetha lokho kunikeza isikali esinembile esikhundleni salokho okuncike kulapho ochofoze khona.

## Hlola isikali

Hlola isikali esisha ngobukhulu besibili njalo.

1. Khetha {{ui:Check}} eqenjini le-{{ui:Scale}}, noma cindezela {{key:D}}.
2. Chofoza zombili iziphetho zobukhulu obuphrintiwe kudrowing.
   Ibha yesimo ithi "Check: 12'-6"" ngobude obufanayo ngamafiti ayizinhlamvu nangamamitha, nomugqa wokuhlola uhlala ushiye ishidini ngobude bawo.
3. Qhathanisa nobukhulu obuphrintiwe. Uma bungalingani, linganisa ngobukhulu obude kakhulu.

Cindezela {{key:Esc}} ukuze usule umugqa wokuhlola.

## Shintsha isikali

Khetha esinye isikali esisethiwe ngaphambili noma linganisa kabusha. Uma ishidi sele linokulinganiswa, i-TakeoffCraft ibuza **Change this sheet's scale?** futhi ikhombise ukuthi malinganiswe kangaki kabusha. Khetha {{ui:Change the scale}}. Yonke imugqa nendawo eshidini kulinganiswa kabusha ngesikali esisha. Ungakuhlehlisa ngo-{{key:Mod+Z}}.

## Ukungabalwa njengo-zero kwamashidi angenasikali

Isimaki eshidini elingenasikali alinabude noma indawo yangempela. I-TakeoffCraft ayisoze iyibale njenge-zero, okungenza isilinganiso sakho sibe sephansi ngokungaziwa. Esikhundleni salokho:

- ishiya lezo zimaki ngaphandle kwamasamanisa,
- ibonisa "+(n) unscaled" eceleni kwento ku-{{ui:Quantities}},
- ibonisa "Akuphelele: (n) izimaki emashidini angenasikali (azifakiwe emasamanisweni)" ekhanda lethebula,
- ibhala inani kwikholomu yamanothi kwisipredshithi esithunyelwe.

Lokhu kungenzeka uma uvula iprojekthi lapho izikali zayo zingazange zisethwe khona. Nika ishidi isikali bese izimaki zibalwa ngokushesha. Izimaki zokubala zihlala zibalwa; azidingi sikali.

## Amathiphu

- Setha isikali kuwo wonke amashidi ozowalinganisa ngaphambi kokuqala. Skena uhlu lwamashidi ufuna **No scale** ukuze uthole asele (ibhokisi losesho lubheka amagama amashidi, hhayi izikali).
- Imininingwane nama-pulani akhuliswe eshidini elifanayo ngokuvamile asebenzisa esinye isikali. I-TakeoffCraft inesikali esisodwa ngeshidi ngalinye; bona [Okungakakhiwa](topic:not-built-yet).
- Idrowing yemethrikhi: khetha isikali semethrikhi njenge `1:100`, noma linganisa ngebanga lemethrikhi njenge `6000 mm`.
