Ku-PDF yevekitha izindonga nemiphetho ngumugqa wangempela. {{ui:Indlela}} ({{key:P}}) iyawathatha njengoba edwetshwe: ukuchofoza kanye komugqa kulinganisa konke okuxhunyiwe kuwo, kusuka ekuqaleni kuya ekugcineni, ngakho awudingi ukulandela udonga ngokuchofoza amakhona. Ishidi kudingeka libe nesikali. Ikhasi eliskenisiwe alinamigqa okuyikhetha; sebenzisa [Okuqondile](topic:measure-linear) noma [Indawo](topic:measure-areas) kulo.

## Khetha indlela noma umbandela

1. Khetha {{ui:Indlela}}. Umugqa ongaphansi kwesikhombi uyanqunywa, nezinye izindlela ezingakhethwa zidwetshwe ngokuncane.
2. Chofoza umugqa: yonke indlela exhunyiwe iyakhethwa. Ibha ephansi kwethuluzi inikeza izigaba zayo, ubude, ukubekezelela kwesikhala kanye nokuthi zingaki izikhala ezixhunyiwe. Indlela ebuyela esiqalweni sayo iyavaleka futhi nobude bayo bayabikwa.
3. Noma chofoza ngaphakathi kombandela ovaliwe, kude nanoma yimuphi umugqa: umbandela ozungeze leliphoyinti ukhethwa njengendawo. Eyincane kakhulu iza kuqala; {{key:Tab}} uzama olukhulu olulandelayo.
4. Cindezela {{key:Enter}} (noma chofoza ngokwesokudla, noma chofoza kabili) ukuze wengeze amamaki. Indlela ekhethiwe iba ngamamaki wento **ye-linear**, umbandela ube ngamamaki wento **ye-area**: into esebenzayo uma inalolo hlobo, kungenjalo eyengezwe kamuva yalolo hlobo. Uma kungekho lutho, ifomu lento entsha livuleka futhi akukho okwengezwayo uze ucindezele {{key:Enter}} futhi. Konke okukhethiwe kwengezwa ngesinyathelo esisodwa sokuhlehlisa.

## Ukubekezelela kwesikhala

Abadwetshi bashiya izikhala ezincane lapho imigqa kufanele ihlangane. Imigqa ixhumana uma izinhlangothi zayo zisondele kunokubekezelela kwesikhala. Siqala ngamaphoyinti angu-1.5 eshidini (kuboniswa ngamayunithi wesikali sesheet), futhi indilinga engamachashaza ngobubanzi obufanayo ilandela isikhombi, ihlale phezu kwesiphetho ngasinye sendlela ephansi kwayo. Isikhala ngasinye esixhunyiwe sizungezwa ngoluhlobo oluyiorenji endleleni ekhethiwe, nebha iyasibala.

{{key:]}} yenza ukubekezelela kube banzi, {{key:[}} kube mncane. Indlela ekhethiwe ixhunywa kabusha masinyane ngokubekezelela okusha. Esihlanganweni indlela iqhubeka iqonde, ngendlela ephendukayo kancane, futhi ishiye igatsha.

## Okhiye

| Ukhiye noma isenzo | Okwenzayo |
|---|---|
| Chofoza | Khetha indlela ephansi kwesikhombi, noma umbandela ozungezile. |
| {{key:Shift}} + chofoza | Engeza enye indlela noma umbandela kokukhethiwe. |
| {{key:Tab}} | Zama indlela elandelayo ephansi kwesikhombi, noma umbandela omkhulu olandelayo. |
| {{key:Backspace}} noma {{key:Mod+Z}} | Susa isigaba sokugcina endleleni ekhethwe ngokugcina, isigaba ngesigaba. Indlela engasasigaba lutho iyasuswa. Uma kungekho okukhethiwe, {{key:Mod+Z}} iwukuhlehlisa okuvamile. |
| {{key:[}} no-{{key:]}} | Ukubekezelela kwesikhala okumncane nokubanzi. |
| {{key:Enter}} | Engeza amamaki. |
| {{key:Esc}} | Sula okukhethiwe. |

## Okuhle ukukwazi

- Ijika esidwetshweni liyiketango lezingcezu ezimfishane ezigqamile, ngakho ubude balo buncane kancane kunalobo lejika langempela. Ukuze uthole ijika elinembile, linganisa ifayela lomdwebo ngqo ([Linganisa kumdwebo](topic:measure-from-a-drawing)).
- Isikhala esixhunyiwe sibalwa njengokuhamba okuqondile kuso, ngakho ububanzi bombandela ovaliwe buyisiqukatha.
- Kuphela ama-stroke nokugcwaliswa ezindleleni zalelo khasi ngokwalo kuyimigqa. Umbhalo odwetshwe njengezinhlamvu, nemifanekiso akuyona.
