**Into** ingento oyilinganisayo futhi oyibiza: uhlobo lodonga, ukuqedwa kwesitezi, uphahla, umnyango, isibani. Yonke impawu oyidwebayo ingeyento. Ezinye izinhlelo zibiza lezi ngokuthi izimo.

Ngayinye into inohlobo (futhi ingathatha nezinye izinhlobo zezimpawu, bona [Into eyodwa, izinhlobo eziningi zezimpawu](topic:create-items#one-item-several-kinds-of-marks)):

| Uhlobo | Udweba | Ibika | Iyunithi |
|---|---|---|---|
| Umugqa | Imigqa exhunyiwe yamaphuzu | Ubude, inani lemigqa, nendawo yodonga uma inokuphakama | LF noma m |
| Indawo | Imiphetho evaliwe, enezimbobo | Indawo, umjikelezo, inani lezindawo | SF, SY, SQ, ac, ha noma m² |
| Bala | Ukuchofoza kanye ngento ngayinye | Ukuthi zingaki | EA |
| Inani elibhalwe | Akukho okudwetshwayo: ubhala inani | Inani obhale lona (noma elilethwe kusuka ku-CivilCraft) | LS, EA, CY, m³, lb, TON, kg, t, LF, m, SF, SY, SQ, ac, ha noma m² |

## Amanani abhalwe (akukho okudwetshwayo)

Ezinye izinto kwisilinganiso azikaze zidwetshwe: ukuhamba, izimvume, isiphepho, inkontileka yenkulumo eyodwa, ukumba nokugcwalisa okuphethwe yi-CivilCraft. Zenze **Inani elibhalwe**:

1. Khetha {{ui:Into entsha}}, bhala igama, khetha {{ui:Inani elibhalwe (akukho mdwebo)}} bese ukhetha {{ui:Engeza}}.
2. Kumhleli wento khetha i-{{ui:Iyunithi}} (LS yenkulumo eyodwa) bese uthayipha {{ui:Inani}}: `1`, `2.5` noma `1,250`.
3. Setha ukulahleka namazinga njengakunoma iyiphi enye into. Izifomula zomhlanganisela zisebenzisa `Q`, inani, njengo `ceil(Q / 12)` imithwalo yamalori.

Inani elibhalwe alithathi zimpawu futhi alenzi isilinganiso sibe sesingaphelele. Uma isilinganiso sinezindawo zezindleko, umhleli uphinde ube nento ethi {{ui:Indawo yezindleko}}: inani selo liyangena kuleyo ndawo, kumazinga ayo, nasemaphelele ayo, njengokuthi kwenziwe impawu kuleyo ndawo. Amanani alethwe kwenye i-app akhombisa lapho avela khona, bona [Ukumba nezinsimbi zokuphakama](topic:earthwork#cut-and-fill-from-civilcraft).

## Yenza into

1. Ku-panel ethi {{ui:Izinto}} ngakwesokudla, khetha {{ui:Into entsha}}.
2. Bhala igama, njengo `Udonga lohlobo A langaphakathi` noma `Ithayela lekhaphethi`.
3. Khetha uhlobo: {{ui:Umugqa (ubude)}}, {{ui:Indawo}} noma {{ui:Bala}}.
4. Khetha {{ui:Engeza}} (noma cindezela {{key:Enter}}).
   Into ivela ohlwini ngombala wayo, ibe yinto oyidwebisa ngayo, bese umhleli wayo uvula ngaphansi kohlu. Ibha yesimo ikutshela ithuluzi okufanele ulikhethe ngokulandelayo.

Cindezela {{key:Esc}} efomini ukuze uyivale ngaphandle kokwengeza into.

:::tip
Uma ukhetha ithuluzi lokulinganisa kuqala, i-TakeoffCraft isebenzisa into yokugcina yohlobo olufanele, noma ivule ifomu le-{{ui:Into entsha}} nohlobo selukhethiwe kade.
:::

## Khetha into oyidwebisa ngayo

Chofoza into ohlwini. Iba yinto izimpawu ezintsha ezingena kuyo, nezimpawu zayo zikhanyiswe kuwo wonke amashidi kanti ezinye zifiphale. Uma ishidi elivuliwe lingenazo izimpawu zayo, i-TakeoffCraft ikuyisa kwelinye ishidi elinazo. Chofoza into futhi ukuze uyeke ukuyikhanyisa; isahlala iyinto oyidwebisa ngayo.

Chofoza impawu ngethuluzi le-{{ui:Khetha}} nalo lenza into yayo ibe yamanje.

## Setha into

Umhleli wento (**Into: (igama)**) unezinkambu ezilandelayo. Ngayinye iyagcinwa uma ushiya inkambu noma ucindezela {{key:Enter}}, futhi noma iyiphi inguquko ingabuyiselwa emuva.

| Inkambu | Yenza ini |
|---|---|
| {{ui:Igama}} | Ibonakala ohlwini, emathebuleni nasekuthumeleni. |
| {{ui:Thayipha}} | Umugqa, Indawo noma Bala. Iyavalwa uma into sekunezimpawu ("Into enezimpawu igcina uhlobo lwayo"). |
| {{ui:Umbala}} | Umbala wezimpawu zayo esikrinini nasePDF enezimpawu. |
| {{ui:Iyunithi}} | Inani elibikwa ngayo: LF noma m zomugqa; SF, SY, SQ, ac, ha noma m² zendawo; EA zokubala. |
| {{ui:Futhi ukale}} | Ezinye izinhlobo zezimpawu into eyithathayo, ngayinye neyunithi yayo. Bona ngezansi. |
| {{ui:Ukuphakama}} (umugqa) | Ukuphakama kodonga. Into-ke ibuye ibike indawo yodonga (ubude × ukuphakama). Imigqa evela kumodeli ye-BimCraft ithwala iminyango nezifasi zayo: zisuswa kanye, futhi into ibika indawo ephelele, izikhala nendawo esele. Ukubalwa kusuka kuprojekthi ye-BimCraft okukhethwe kuyo indlela yokulinganisa kulinganiswa ngaleyo ndlela nalapha (inothi liyibiza, kanye nomthombo wayo, lithi ayihlolwa yi-quantity surveyor); le ndlela ayishintshwa ku-TakeoffCraft. Izitini noma amabhulokhi nodaka oluvela kuhlobo lodonga lufika njengezingxenye ezimbili: iyunithi zokuyalwa nevolumu kadaka. |
| {{ui:Ukujula}} (indawo) | Ukujula noma ubukhulu, kusetshenziswa ezifomulini zomhlanganisela (isibonelo ivolumu yekhonkolo). |
| {{ui:Ukulahleka %}} | Kwengezwa enanini ngaphambi kokubiza: 10 kusho inani × 1.10. |
| {{ui:Impahla $/LF}} | Izindleko zempahla ngeyunithi. Ilebula ilandela iyunithi: $/SF, $/EA njalo njalo. |
| {{ui:Umsebenzi $/LF}} | Izindleko zomsebenzi ngeyunithi. |
| {{ui:Inombolo ye-CSI}} | Inombolo yayo ye-CSI MasterFormat, njengo `09 21 16` (noma umnyango nje, `09`). Umsebenzi we-workbook othi By division uqoqa izindleko ngayo. Izinto ezivela kuma-[trade packs](topic:trade-packs) zinayo sekuyiyo. |

Ukuphakama nokujula kuthatha amafiti namaduyima (`9'-0"`, `8' 6"`) noma amafiti ahlukaniswe ngamadesimali (`9`) yamayunithi e-imperial, namamitha (`2.7`, `2700 mm`) wamayunithi e-metric. Amazinga athatha amanani njengo `12.50` noma `$1,250`, kuze kube amadesimali amane. Bona [Ukufaka ubude, izinga nephesenti](topic:entry-formats).

Ngaphansi kwenkambu, **Okulinganisiwe** ikhombisa amanani ezifomula zento angazisebenzisa, namachashazi azo: Ubude (L), Imigqa (N), Indawo yodonga (A) zomugqa; Indawo (A), Umjikelezo (P), Izindawo (N) zendawo; Bala (N) zokubala.

## Into eyodwa, izinhlobo eziningi zezimpawu

Ezinye izinto zilinganiswa ngendlela ngaphezu kweyodwa: igumbi linesakhiwo (indawo), ukugcina ngaphansi kodonga lwalo (umugqa) namasango (ukubala). Esikhundleni sezinto ezintathu, yenza eyodwa:

1. Yenza into ngohlobo lwayo oluyinhloko, njengento yendawo `Igumbi 101`.
2. Ngaphansi kwe-{{ui:Futhi ukale}} kumhleli wayo, tshekisha {{ui:Umugqa}}, {{ui:Bala}} noma zombili. Ngayinye ithola iyunithi eseceleni kwayo.
3. Dweba ngalo nalo luphi na uthuluzi uhlobo lwalo olwamukelwa yinto: nge-`Igumbi 101` lamanje, {{ui:Indawo}} idweba isakhiwo sayo, {{ui:Umugqa}} idweba ukugcina kwayo phansi, bese {{ui:Bala}} idweba iminyango yayo. I-TakeoffCraft ayicele enye into.

Uhlu lwezinto lubonisa inani lonke (`50.00 SF + 2 EA`). Kuthebula lamanani, umugqa wento uhlala nenani layo elikhulu, bese ngayinye yezinye izinhlobo ithola umugqa wayo ngaphansi kwawo, njenge **Igumbi 101 (bala)**, nezinga layo lokulahleka nempahla nomsebenzi. Ukuthunyelwa, ithebula lalo shidi nencwadi yencazelo ye-PDF enezimpawu zibhalwe ngendlela efanayo.

Izinkambu zohlobo oluyinhloko zihlala zizo: ukuphakama noma ukujula, ukuthambeka, ukususwa emuva kanye nesimo se-3D kusebenza ezimpawini zalo kuphela. Umugqa wokugcina odwetshwe ngento yendawo ulinganiswa ngokuthi uhamba endaweni ku-plan, futhi ubonakala uyisicaba ku-3D. Izifomula zomhlanganisela zisebenzisa amanani ohlobo oluyinhloko.

Uhlobo lungasuswa kuphela uma into ingenazo izimpawu zalo; zisuse, noma uzidlulisele kwenye into ngaphansi kwe-**Impawu ekhethiwe**, kuqala. Izimpawu zingadluliselwa kunoma iyiphi into ethatha uhlobo lwazo.

Ngezansi kukhona i-{{ui:Umhlanganisela}}: izingxenye ezifana nezinduku noma ibhodi, ngayinye ineyayo ifomula namazinga. Bona [Umhlanganisela nezifomula](topic:assemblies).

Ekugcineni: {{ui:Yisa phezulu}} no-{{ui:Yisa phansi}} ishintsha indawo yento ohlwini nakokuthunyelwa, bese {{ui:Susa into}} isusa yona nazo zonke izimpawu zayo (kuphela emva kokubuza; ungayihlehlisa).

Izinto zendawo zinokuthambeka (izindiza zophahla ezilinganiswa ngokuthambeka: [Uphahla nokuthambeka](topic:roofs-and-slopes)) kanye nokususwa emuva (izimpawu zazo zisuswa endaweni yezinye izinto: [Linganisa izindawo](topic:measure-areas#back-outs-columns-shafts-and-openings-taken-out-of-several-items)). Izinto zomugqa zinesimo sokuthambeka (Ilevu, Rake, Hip noma valley) futhi, kwama-rakes nama-hips, nokuthambeka.

## Amazinga emathebulini

Ungabuye uthayiphe {{ui:Ukulahleka %}}, namazinga empahla nomsebenzi ngqo ethebuleni elingaphansi {{ui:Amanani}} ({{ui:Ngento}}). Kuyisimiso esifanayo nasemhleli.

## Ukubiza kusebenza kanjani

Ngayo yonke into nangayo yonke ingxenye yomhlanganisela:

1. **Nokulahleka** = inani × (1 + ukulahleka %).
2. **Impahla** = nokulahleka × impahla ngeyunithi, ijikelezwe kwisenti.
3. **Umsebenzi** = nokulahleka × umsebenzi ngeyunithi, ujikelezwe kwisenti.
4. **Isamba** = impahla + umsebenzi.

I-{{ui:Isamba somsebenzi}} yengeza yonke into nayo yonke ingxenye. Into ingaba nezinga layo nomhlanganisela ngesikhathi esisodwa; zombili zibalwa, ngakho shiya amazinga alo ku-0 uma izingxenye zawo zithwala izindleko.

## Umsebenzi ngeqembu

Esikhundleni sezinga lomsebenzi ngeyunithi, umsebenzi wento ungavela eqenjini: liyakwazi kangakanani ngehora, linabantu abangaki, nokuthi ngamunye uthola ini. Ngaphansi kwe-{{ui:Umsebenzi ngeqembu}} kumhleli wento, tshekisha {{ui:Bala umsebenzi ngeqembu}} bese ugcwalisa:

| Inkambu | Isibonelo |
|---|---|
| {{ui:Ukukhiqiza}} | `20` LF ngehora (kuyunithi yento) yeqembu lonke |
| {{ui:Inani leqembu}} | `2` abantu |
| {{ui:Umholo $/ihora ngamunye}} | `45` |

I-TakeoffCraft ibala, kusukela enanini elinokulahleka:

- **amahora eqembu** = inani ÷ ukukhiqiza (110 LF ÷ 20 = 5.5),
- **amahora abasebenzi** = amahora eqembu × inani leqembu (11),
- **izinsuku zeqembu** = amahora eqembu ÷ 8 (0.69),
- **umsebenzi** = amahora abasebenzi × umholo (\$495.00).

Umhleli ukhombisa umphumela nezinga elifika kulo ($4.50/LF). Izinga lomsebenzi lento lisesimweni sokufunda kuphela, emhleli nasethebulini. Ikhanda lethebula lamanani {{ui:Amanani}} lengeza amahora abasebenzi omsebenzi nezinsuku zeqembu. Ukuthunyelwa kubonisa amahora eqembu ngalinye, nencwadi ye-workbook isebenzisa ifomula yeqembu (`ROUND(F2/20*2*45,2)`). Susa ibhokisi ukuze ubuyele ezingeni ngeyunithi.

## Izinto zemetric

Khetha m (umugqa) noma m² (indawo) njengeyunithi. Amanani, ukuphakama namanani ezifomula kuke kube ngamamitha. Isikali sishidi singaba noma yiliphi iseti noma i-calibration; iyunithi nezikali zizimele.
