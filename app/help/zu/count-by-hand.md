Into yokubala ibala izinto: izimnyango, izinto zokukhanyisa, amasokhethi, izinto zepulambing. Ngayinye ochofoza eshidini yengeza impawu yokubala. Ukubala akudingi isikali.

## Bala

1. Yenza noma chofoza into yokubala ngaphansi kwe-{{ui:Izinto}} (uhlobo lwe-{{ui:Bala}}).
2. Khetha {{ui:Bala}} ethuluzini, noma cindezela {{key:C}}.
3. Chofoza ngayinye eshidini.
   Ukuchofoza ngakunye kubeka ichashaza ngombala wento, bese inani lento lenyuka ngo-1.
4. Hambela eshidini elilandelayo ({{key:PageDown}}) uqhubeke nokubala. Ithuluzi lihlala ku-{{ui:Bala}}.
5. Cindezela {{key:Esc}} uma uqedile. I-TakeoffCraft ibuyela ku-{{ui:Khetha}}.

Ukudonsa kusashelelisa nevili lisasondeza uma ubala, ngakho ungazulazula eshidini ngaphandle kokwengeza amachashaza.

:::tip
Chofoza into yokubala ukuze uqaqamise amachashaza ayo kuwo wonke amashidi. Uhlu lwamashidi bese lubonisa ukuthi zingaki kwelinye ngalinye, okusiza ukuthola amashidi ongawabalanga.
:::

## Lungisa ukubala

- **Susa ichashaza**: khetha {{ui:Khetha}} ({{key:V}}), chofoza ichashaza, bese ucindezela {{key:Delete}} noma khetha {{ui:Susa uphawu}}. Noma cindezela {{key:Mod+Z}} ngemva kokuchofoza okungalungile.
- **Hambisa ichashaza**: likhethe, bese ulidonsa.
- **Ibalwe ngaphansi kwento engalungile**: khetha ichashaza bese ukhetha into yokubala efanele ohlwini lwe-**Into** ngaphansi kwe-**Impawu ekhethiwe**.

## Usayizi wamachashaza, esikrinini nasePDF

Amachashaza agcina usayizi munye esikrinini noma ukusondeza kangakanani. Ukuze uwashintshe, vula {{ui:Izilungiselelo}}, ithebhu ye-TakeoffCraft, bese ukhetha **Usayizi wophawu lokubala**: Ncane kakhulu, Ncane, Maphakathi (okuzenzakalelayo) noma Enkulu.

Ukuthumela ({{ui:Thumela}} › {{ui:I-PDF emakwe}}) kudweba ichashaza ngalinye ngosayizi ofanayo ephepheni, kungakhathaliseki usayizi weshidi: isibukeli se-PDF esibonisa ngo-100% sibonisa amachashaza njengoba ekhona esikrinini. Lapho amachashaza eshidini angathintana, ukuthumela kwenza amachashaza alelo shidi ancane ukuze ngayinye ifundeke.

## Izimfanekiso eziningi ezifanayo

Ngesimfanekiso esiphindaphindeka kakhulu eshidini, zama [Ukubala okuzenzakalelayo](topic:auto-count): bopha esisodwa, bheka okutholakele, bese ukwengeza konke ngesinyathelo esisodwa.

## Ukusebenzisa izinani ezifomula

Inani lento yokubala ngu-**N**. Izingxenye zomhlanganisela zingayisebenzisa, njengamahinji `N * 3` ezimnyangweni. Bona [Imihlanganisela namafomula](topic:assemblies).
