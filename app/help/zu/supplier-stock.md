{{ui:Inikezelo}} phezu kwethuluzi (ilori) ihlola okudingwa ukubalwa kwakho ngokumelene nohlu lomgcinimali womhlinzeki. Imigqa yomhlinzeki ebalulekile igcinwa efayelini lokubala lakho. Akukho okuthunyelwa noma yikuphi, futhi akukho okucela umhlinzeki: wena uletha ifayela.

## Ifayela

Ifayela le-CSV elinomugqa wesihloko. Lidinga ikholomu yegama noma ye-SKU, nekholomu yomgcinimali noma yesikhathi sokulethwa. Lezi zihloko ziyavunywa (noma yiliphi uhlelo, noma yimaphi amacala): Item, Name, Description noma Product; SKU, Part noma Item #; In stock, On hand, Available noma Quantity; Lead time noma Delivery days; Unit noma UOM; Price noma Cost; Supplier noma Vendor.

- Umgcinimali ungaba inombolo (1,200), "In stock" noma "Out of stock".
- Isikhathi sokulethwa singaba "5 days", "2 weeks", "2-3 weeks" (oluthathwe olude), "3 months", "same day" noma "backorder". Inombolo evamile ingeyesihloko (izinsuku, ngaphandle uma isihloko sisho amaviki noma izinyanga).
- Amafayela e-Excel awafundwa: gcina ishidi njenge-CSV kuqala.

## Hlola umbalo

1. Khetha {{ui:Inikezelo}}, bese {{ui:Engeza ifayela lomhlinzeki…}} bese ukhetha i-CSV. Engeza amafayela amaningi: kuze kube ngamafayela amahlanu, izimuva ezingu-2,000 kulonke, lezo ezihambisana nezinto zakho kuqala.
2. Yonke into, isimo sesibili kanye nezingxenye zesakhiwo ezinenani ihlanganiswa nomugqa ngegama, noma nge-SKU egameni layo. Igama elifanayo (elisesinye) lisetshenziswa uma kungekho elifanayo ngokuphelele, futhi kuyasho lokho.
3. Ngaphansi kwe-{{ui:Okudingwa umbalo}}, ezimfushane zivela kuqala: "Short by 120 EA", "Lead time 21 days", "On backorder", "Unit differs", "No stock figure" noma "In stock". Ukudinga kubandakanya ukulahleka. Iyunithi yomhlinzeki engafani neyesilinganiso (iton ngokuchasene nefithi) iyabikwa futhi ayiqhathaniswa ngalutho. Umugqa ngamunye unento {{ui:Khetha into}}, eyakuyisa kulolo hlobo.
4. Setha {{ui:Isikhathi eside kakhulu esifunwayo (izinsuku)}} (okuzenzakalelayo kungu-14). Into engekho emgcinimalini futhi elinda ngaphezu kwalokho imakwa, nesifinyezo sitshela ukulinda okude okubalulekile.
5. {{ui:Uhlu lokuyala (CSV)}} ilanda izinto ezishoda, nenani elishodayo, umhlinzeki, nesikhathi sokulethwa. {{ui:Shiya ngaphandle}} isusa ukudinga ekuhloleni (kuyisinyathelo sokuhlehlisa).

## Amafayela eshidi nethala lamanani

Uhlu lomhlinzeki lungaba incwadi ye-Excel (.xlsx) njenge-CSV: ishidi lokuqala liyafundwa, ngamagama wamakholomu afanayo. Izifomula azisebenzi; inani elibanjwe yifayela ngokwalo liyafundwa.

Uma ifayela lamanani sele lengeziwe, {{ui:Amanani angena emtapweni}} kuleli bhokisi isetha intengo yempahla yayo yonke into ku-[Umtapo](topic:price-library) elinamanani ifayela: igama elifanayo, neyunithi efanayo (noma engakhulunywanga). Umsebenzi, ukulahleka, ubude nezinhlanganisela kuhlala njengoba kunjalo, futhi inothi lento lisho ukuthi intengo yaphuma kuphi ifayela. Into ngamamitha ayipriciwe ngohlu lwamafithi, futhi into engenagama elifanayo ishiywa ngaphandle; umugqa wesimo utshela ukuthi kuyangana kangakanani okushiywe ngenxa yeyunithi. Phinda ukupriciwa kusuka ku-Umtapo ukuze ufake amanani amasha kulo mbalo.

## Ku-ipaneli Yezinkinga

Ukushoda kuyisexwayiso, nokulinda isikhathi eside kuyinothi, ku-ipaneli eyabelanwa yezinkinga ("Supplier stock"), ngakho zihlala nezinye izinhlolo zonke. Ungayamukela ngesizathu, futhi ukwamukela kugcinwa embalweni.

## Nomsizi wakho

Uma uvumela abasizi ku-{{ui:Izilungiselelo}}, bangafunda ukuhlola (supply_check). Ukungeza ifayela lomhlinzeki kungokwakho.

## Okufanele ukwazi

Izibalo zomgcinimali zilungile njengalo ifayela oliwengezayo. Engeza ifayela futhi ukuze uvuselele: ifayela elinegama elifanayo liyawushintsha owangaphambilini.
