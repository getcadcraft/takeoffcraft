Into yendawo ilinganisa indawo evaliwe. Umugqa ngamunye ungaba nezimbobo ezisikwe kuwo (i-shaft, insika, ipulanta), nemiphetho yawo ingaba eqondile noma ejikelezayo.

Ngaphambi kokuqala: ishidi kudingeka isikali ([Setha isikali](topic:set-the-scale)) futhi udinga into yendawo ([Dala izinto zokubala](topic:create-items)).

## Phonononga indawo

1. Cofa into yendawo ngaphansi kwe-{{ui:Items}}.
2. Khetha {{ui:Area}} ebhareni yamathuluzi, noma cindezela {{key:A}}.
3. Cofa ikhona ngalinye legumbi noma leslebhu ngokulandelana. Bamba {{key:Shift}} ngamaphethelo angu-0°, 45° kanye no-90°.
   Kusukela ephointini lesithathu, umugqa uyagcwaliseka, futhi ibha yesimo ibonisa "Indawo" kanye "nomjikelezo". Indilinga encane ivela izungeze iphoyinti lokuqala.
4. Vala umugqa: cofa iphoyinti lokuqala (ngaphakathi endilingeni yalo), cindezela {{key:Enter}}, cofa kabili, noma cofa ngesokudla.
   Indawo igcwaliswa kancane ngombala wento, ibhalwe ngezikwele zayo ze-feet, bese ibe yimaki ekhethiwe.

{{key:Backspace}} isusa ikhona lokugcina; {{key:Esc}} ilahla umugqa.

Umugqa oziqhumayo uyenqatshwa: "Umugqa uziqhumile, ngakho indawo yawo izoba engalungile. Wudweba futhi ungaziqhumi." Dweba indawo efana nesibalo esingu-8 njengezindawo ezimbili.

## Imiphetho ejikelezayo

Ngenkathi uphonononga, vula imodi yejika ({{ui:Arc}} noma {{key:R}}). Emva kwekhona, cofa iphoyinti emgqeni wejika, bese ucofa lapho ijika liphela. Ukuvala umugqa ngejika, yenza leso sicofo sokugcina ephointini lokuqala. Indawo nomjikelezo kusebenzisa ijika eliqondile. Cindezela {{key:R}} futhi ukuze uthole imiphetho eqondile. Ukuguqula ijika ngemva kwalokho, khetha indawo bese udonsa isibambo esizungezile esiphakathi nejika. Bona [Linganisa imigqa emide](topic:measure-linear#arcs-and-curved-walls).

## Isiyingi

1. Ngento yendawo, khetha {{ui:Circle}} noma cindezela {{key:O}}.
2. Cofa isikhungo.
3. Cofa noma iliphi iphoyinti emaphethelweni.
   Ngenkathi uhambisa, ibha yesimo ibonisa irediyasi nendawo. Isiyingi sengezwa njengemaki yendawo enendawo eqondile nomjikelezo.

## Gcwalisa igumbi ngokucofa kanye

Uma amagumbi edwetshwe njengezindonga ezivaliwe, {{ui:Room}} ({{key:B}}) ithatha ifloor ngokucofa kanye:

1. Khetha into yendawo (isibonelo ifinishi lefloor) bese unikeza ishidi isikali.
2. Khetha {{ui:Room}} eqenjini le-{{ui:Takeoff}}, noma cindezela {{key:B}}.
3. Cofa ngaphakathi kwegumbi, endaweni evulekile (hhayi emugqeni noma kulebula).

I-TakeoffCraft ifunda imigqa yeshidi, ithole isikhala esizungeze ukucofa bese ilinganisa ngokulandela izindonga zangaphakathi, njengendawo yento yamanje. Ukuvulwa kwemnyango kuya kuthi **4'-0"** kuthathwa njengokuvaliwe, ngakho umugqa uqonda ngqo phezu komnyango; **Izilungiselelo › TakeoffCraft › Room fill closes openings up to** iguqula lobubanzi (3'-0" kuya ku-8'-0"). Amagama amagumbi, izinto zasendlini kanye nezixhobo ngaphakathi akwenzi izimbobo.

Iyenqaba, isho ngenxa yalutho, uma:

- ukucofa kumugqa ("Lokho kusemgqeni");
- isikhala asivaliwe: ukuvulwa okubanzi kunesilungiselelo kuvumela ukuthi kuphume kumphetho weshidi. Phakamisa isilungiselelo, noma linganisa leligumbi ngesandla nge-{{ui:Area}};
- isikhala sincane kakhulu (isimbulo, hhayi igumbi).

Uma umnyango ubanzi kunesilungiselelo, ukugcwaliswa kungena egumbini elilandelayo bese kuphonononga bobabili ndawonye: hlehlisa ({{key:Mod+Z}}), phakamisa isilungiselelo, bese ucofa futhi. Hlola umugqa njengendawo nanoma iyiphi: donsa amakhona ukuze uwulungise. Ishidi eliskenwe lisebenza ngendlela efanayo, kodwa imigqa yalo kufanele ibe mnyama ngokwanele; imigqa egreyi ekhanyayo ingenza ukugcwaliswa kungame.

## Sika imbobo endaweni

1. Khetha {{ui:Select}} ({{key:V}}) bese ucofa indawo. Iveza izibambo ezisikwele emakhoneni ayo.
2. Khetha {{ui:Cut-out}} ebhareni yamathuluzi (noma cindezela {{key:X}}), noma {{ui:Add a cut-out}} ngaphansi kwe-**Selected mark**.
3. Cofa amakhona embobo, ngaphakathi endaweni. Imodi yejika isebenza nalapha.
4. Yivale njengendawo: cofa iphoyinti layo lokuqala, cindezela {{key:Enter}}, cofa kabili, noma cofa ngesokudla.
   Imbobo ishiywe ingagcwaliswanga, nebha yesimo ithi "Imbobo yengeziwe. Dweba enye, noma cindezela Esc."
5. Dweba ezinye izimbobo endaweni efanayo, noma cindezela {{key:Esc}}.

Imbobo ezungezile: khetha {{ui:Add a round cut-out}} ngaphansi kwe-**Selected mark**, cofa isikhungo sayo, bese ucofa iphoyinti emaphethelweni ayo.

Imbobo kufanele ihlale ngokuphelele endaweni futhi ingagqumi enye imbobo. Uma kungenjalo, i-TakeoffCraft iyenqaba: "Imbobo kufanele ihlale ngokuphelele endaweni." noma "Imibobo ayikwazi ukugqumana."

Ukususa imbobo yokugcina, khetha {{ui:Remove last cut-out}}.

## Ukukhipha kwezinto eziningi: izinsika, ama-shaft nezimbobo

Imbobo ingumaki wendawo oyodwa. **Back-out** idwetshwa kanye bese ikhishwa kuyo yonke indawo ehlangana nayo, kwizinto ozikhethile: dweba izinsika kanye, zikhishwe ku-floor finish, esileyilini nasekuslebhuleni ndawonye.

1. Yenza into yendawo yazo, njengo "Izinsika".
2. Ezilungiselelweni zento, thikha **Back-out: its marks take their area out of other items**. Uhlu lwezinye izinto zendawo luyavela, zonke zithikiwe: susa ukuthikwa kwezo ezingafanele ukuthintwa yi-back-out (isibonelo upende wezindonga).
3. Thatha ama-back-out ngokusebenzisa i-{{ui:Area}} noma {{ui:Circle}}, njengoba kujwayelekile. Ngayinye idwetshwa ngamachashazi.

Yonke imaki yendawo yento ethikiwe esheetini efanayo ilahlekelwa yingxenye ye-back-out eyihlangana nayo: i-back-out yonke uma ingaphakathi, ingxenye ehlangana kuphela uma idlula emphethelweni, nalutho ngaphakathi kwembobo. Ipaneli yendawo ekhethiwe ibonisa **Back-outs** nendawo esele. Inani lento, izifomula zayo zenhlanganisela, izindleko, ukubuka kwe-3D nokuthunyelwa konke kusebenzisa indawo esele; amanothi okulinganisa asho ukuthi ama-back-out akhiphe malini. Into ye-back-out ibala indawo yayo (shiya izindleko zayo ziyi-zero uma ungayibiza).

I-back-out kufanele ibe **convex**: i-rectangle, isiyingi noma noma yimuphi umugqa ongenaye ikhona elingaphakathi. I-TakeoffCraft iyenqaba uma ungayidweba kanjalo; dweba isinsika esinesimo se-L njengama-back-out amabili. Ama-back-out awanazimbobo, futhi ama-back-out amabili agqumanayo bayabalwa bobabili.

Umjikelezo (`P`) awushintshi: ama-back-out akhipha indawo kuphela.

## Funda indawo

Khetha indawo. **Selected mark** ibonisa:

| Line | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Area | Indawo esele: umugqa ngaphandle kwezimbobo. Yilokho okubalwa yilento. |
| Outline | Indawo ephelele ngaphakathi komugqa (ibonakala uma kukhona izimbobo). |
| Cut-outs | Izimbobo ezingaki, nendawo yazo ephelele. |
| Perimeter | Ubude bomphetho womugqa, **kanye** namaphethelo ayo yonke imbobo ("nezimbobo"). |

Umjikelezo ufaka amaphethelo embobo ngenhloso, njengezimo zomphetho noma isisekelo esizungeza imbobo. Uma udinga umphetho wangaphandle kuphela, gcina imibobo ngaphandle kwaleyo nto noma linganisa umphetho wangaphandle njengomugqa omude.

Isici se-{{ui:Unit}} sento sinquma ukuthi umphumela ubikwa kanjani: SF, SY noma m². Ipaneli ye-**Selected mark** nebha yesimo njalo zibonisa izikwele ze-feet.

## Lungisa indawo

- **Hambisa ikhona**: khetha indawo bese udonsa isibambo sekhona (izimbobo nazo zinezibambo). Uma isimo esisha sigqumana noma sisunduza imbobo ngaphandle, ikhona liyabuyiswa: "Iphoyinti libuyiselwe."
- **Hambisela kwenye into**: khetha enye into yendawo ohlwini lwe-**Item** ngaphansi kwe-**Selected mark**.
- **Susa**: {{key:Delete}}, {{ui:Delete}} ebhareni yamathuluzi ngaphansi kwe-{{ui:More}}, noma {{ui:Delete mark}}.

## Ukujula kuma-volume

Nika into yendawo {{ui:Depth}} (isibonelo `0'-4"` yeslebhu) bese usebenzisa `A * H / 27` kwisingxenye ye-assembly ukuthola ama-cubic yards. Bona [Inhlanganisela nezifomula](topic:assemblies).
