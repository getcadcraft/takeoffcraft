Lokhu kuguquke ku-TakeoffCraft 0.9, uma kuqhathaniswa no-0.8.1. Ikhishwa elincane kule app: manje ubude bungafundwa ngamamitha, ngamasentimitha noma ngamamilimitha, izindawo nezikali zilandela kahle umsebenzi we-metric, futhi i-Arc inezindlela ezilishumi zokudweba. Izikhathi ze-Live ziphinde zibonise ukuthi ubani ukuphi.

## Okusha

### Funda ubude ngamamitha, amasentimitha noma amamilimitha

- **Bonisa ubude njenge.** {{ui:Settings}} › {{ui:TakeoffCraft}} unayo {{ui:Show lengths as}} enezinketho: {{ui:Follow Global}}, {{ui:Feet-inches}}, m, cm no-mm. {{ui:Follow Global}} isebenzisa iyunithi yobude ye-metric oyiphawule kuthi {{ui:Settings}} › {{ui:Global}} › {{ui:Units}}, futhi amafiti namainshi uma kungekho iphawuliwe. Iguqula indlela ubude olufundeka ngayo eshidini, ebhaleni lesimo nakumaphaneli, kufaka phakathi ukuphakama, ukujula, ukuqina nezinga. Inombolo engenayo iyunithi, etshalwe enkambweni yokuphakama, ifundwa ngeyunithi ebonisiwe.
- **Ukubonisa kuphela.** Amanani, izindleko nokuthunyelwa kubalwa kusuka kubude obunembile. Ukukhetha kwe-1/16, 1/8 no-1/4 inch kuvela kumafiti namainshi kuphela. Bona [Bonisa ubude ngamamitha](topic:show-lengths-in-meters).
- **Amayunithi wesisindo.** Into {{ui:Typed quantity (no drawing)}} manje ingaba ngamayunithi lb, TON, kg noma t, futhi inganikezwa amanani ngeyunithi ngayinye.
- **Izinga lokushisa.** Yonke into inensimu ekhethwayo {{ui:Temperature}} emhleli wento, ibhalwa ngamadigri C noma F. {{ui:Show temperatures as}} kuthi {{ui:Settings}} › {{ui:TakeoffCraft}} inikeza {{ui:Follow Global}}, °C noma °F.

### Izindawo nezikali zomsebenzi we-metric

- **Izindawo nge-m2 emdwebweni.** Indawo elinganiswe ngento ye-metric manje ithi m2 ethegini yomdwebo nasephaneli yemaki, kanye nasethebuleni, esikhundleni se-SF. Umthetho uthi m2 uma into inikezwe amanani nge-metric noma ubude bubonisiwe ngamamitha, amasentimitha noma amamilimitha, kanti kwenye imo ithi SF.
- **Indawo yodonga.** Into eqondile enokuphakama nayo ibonisa indawo yodonga wayo (ubude ephindwe ngokuphakama) ohlwini lwezinto nanjengomugqa wenothi ngaphansi kwethebula lamanani ngento.
- **Izilungiselelo zesikali 1:75 no-1:150.** Zisohlwini {{ui:Preset}} eliseqenjini {{ui:Scale}}, elinamanje izikali ezingu-32. Bona [Izilungiselelo zesikali](topic:scale-presets).

### I-Arc enezindlela eziyishumi

- Uma {{ui:Arc}} ivulekile (inkinobho yebha yamathuluzi noma {{key:R}}), ibhokisi {{ui:Arc method}} livela ebhalini leshidi: 3-Point, Start, Center, End, Start, Center, Angle, Start, Center, Length, Start, End, Angle, Start, End, Direction, Start, End, Radius, Center, Start, End, Center, Start, Angle and Center, Start, Length. I-Angle, ubude, iradius nendlela kungachofozwa noma kubhalwe. Isebenza emizileni eqondile, ezindaweni nasezimbobeni ezisikiwe. Amafayela alondolozwe ngaphambi kuka-0.9 avula ngendlela efanayo. Bona [Dweba ama-arc ngezindlela eziyishumi](topic:draw-arcs-ten-methods).

### Ukusebenza ndawonye

- **Bona izikhombi zabanye.** Esikhathini se-Live isikhombi somuntu ngamunye sibonakala eshidini ngegama nombala wakhe, nebhokisi elincane ezungeze izimaki abazikhethile. Abantu eshidini elihlukile bahlwanyelwa ngegama lalelo shidi. Bona [Sebenza ndawonye ku-Live nabanye](topic:work-live).
- **Landela umuntu, nohlu lwabantu.** Inkinobho {{ui:People}} esikhathini se-Live ibhala ngohlu lomuntu ngamunye ngethuluzi asisebenzisayo, ishidi aphezulu kulo nesikhathi okokugcina ahlela ngaso. {{ui:Follow}} ishintsha ukubuka kwakho kube kuhambisana nowabo, futhi umphathi angasebenzisa {{ui:Ask everyone to follow me}}. Bona [Landela umlingani esikhathini se-Live](topic:follow-a-teammate).

## Kwangaphambili: 0.8 no-0.8.1

Lokhu kuguquke ku-TakeoffCraft 0.8 (kukhishwe ngo-2026-11-01) no-0.8.1 (2026-11-02), uma kuqhathaniswa no-0.7. Ukhiye wezimpawu ungabalwa yonke isethi ngesikhathi esisodwa, ibalo elisha lingaqala ngamanani emisebenzini yakho yangaphambili, futhi ibalo lingahlolwa ngempahla yomhlinzeki. I-0.8.1 ingukulungisa okukhishiwe.

### Okusha

**Bala konke esethini**

- **Bopha ukhiye kanye.** {{ui:Count everything}} kwibha yamathuluzi ifunda ukhiye wezimpawu: hudula ibhokisi ezungeze izimpawu namagama abhalwe eduze kwazo. Impawu ngayinye ibhalwa ngesithombe negama layo, bese leli gama libe yigama lento. Susa utiki lokho ongakufuni, bese ukhetha {{ui:Count on N sheets}}. Ibha ikhombisa ishidi elisesekiwe, {{ui:Cancel}} imisa ukusebenza, bese ikhasi lisasebenziseka.
- **Ishidi ngalinye ngokusebenza kunye.** Impawu ngayinye ibalwa kuwo wonke amashidi avuliwe. Ishidi elidwetshwe ngesikali esehlukile lisesha ngalowo sikali, nezimpawu zitholakala zijikelezwe noma yiliphi i-engeli elilungile. Izimpawu zokhiye ngokwazo azibalwa eshidini lazo.
- **Hlola ngaphambi kokungeza noma yini.** Inani ngalinye linesixhumanisi kuleshidi ngalinye ("E-103: 4") eliya kuleyo shidi. Chofoza ibhokisi kokutholakele ukuze uliphuce, bese uchofoza futhi ukuze uliphindisele. Izinga lokufana (50% kuya ku-100%) lihlunga kuphela lokho okutholakele; akukho okusesha futhi.
- **Isinyathelo esisodwa sokuhlehlisa.** {{ui:Add N marks}} yenza into yokubala ngayinye yempawu, nemaki kuko konke okutholakele. Igama elisuvele eyinto yakho yokubala liyasetshenziswa kabusha. {{key:Ctrl+Z}} ibuyisela zonke izinto nemaki. Lokho okwamukelwe nakho kuthela ku-{{ui:Learned}}.
- **Izimpawu kuyo yonke i-engeli.** Khetha {{ui:Symbols at any angle}} ngaphambi kokubala ukuze uphinde uthole izimpawu ezijikelezwe ngengelosi engeyona eyilungile. Lokhu kusesha kujikeleze impawu ngayinye njalo ngama-degree ayi-15, futhi impawu engeyona ejikelezayo ingathatha kuze kube izikhathi eziyisithupha isikhathi eside.
- **Yini.** Ukufanisa izimpawu okulula emashidini akho, ngaphandle kwemodeli eqeqeshiwe, futhi akukho okushiya ikhompyutha yakho. Esethini elenziwe lamashidi angu-200 kwathatha imizuzwana engu-9.4 kusuka kuqala kuya kokugcina ku-Chrome kudeskithophu esheshayo; amasethi wangempela wamapulani azoba nzima. Bona [Bala konke esethini](topic:count-everything).

**Inkumbulo yamanani emisebenzini yakho**

- **I-TakeoffCraft ikhumbula amazinga akho.** Uma ulondoloza ibalo, yonke into enamanani esetshenzisiwe ikhunjulwa ngegama nohlobo: izinga lempahla, izinga lomsebenzi, ukuchitheka neyunithi. Imisebenzi yokugcina engu-12 igcinwa ngegama ngalinye. Igcinwa kule khompyutha ecaleni komtapo wamanani, hhayi ngaphakathi kwefayela lebalo, futhi akukho okuthunyelwa noma kanjani.
- **Qala ibalo elisha kuyo.** {{ui:Prices}} kwibha yamathuluzi ibonisa, ngento ngayinye, amazinga omsebenzi wokugcina, amazinga ajwayelekile nobubanzi. {{ui:Use last}} noma {{ui:Use typical}} linika into eyodwa amanani, bese {{ui:Price N unpriced items from memory}} igcwalisa zonke izinto ezingenamanani. Ukusetshenziswa ngakunye kuyisinyathelo esisodwa sokuhlehlisa.
- **Umsebenzi wakudala.** {{ui:Learn from a past takeoff…}} ikhumbula izinto ezinamanani zebalo elilondoloziwe (`.json`).
- **Bona, shintsha, khohlwa.** {{ui:Everything remembered}} ibala ihlu lwawo wonke amagama; inkinobho yotshani ikhohlwa elilodwa, bese {{ui:Clear all prices}} ikhohlwa lonke. Vala {{ui:Remember my prices when I save a takeoff}} ukuze umise. Amanani ngamanani angeke asoze abekwe into ngayinye ngefiti. Bona [Inkumbulo yamanani emisebenzini yakho yangaphambili](topic:price-memory).

**Impahla yomhlinzeki nezikhathi zokufika**

- **Hlola ngohlu lomhlinzeki.** {{ui:Supply}} kwibha yamathuluzi ithatha uhlu lwempahla yomhlinzeki ({{ui:Add a supplier file…}}), i-CSV noma ishidi lezibalo, bese ihlola okudingwa yibalo, nokuchitheka, ngalo. Kugcinwa kuze kube amafayela amahlanu. Akukho okuthunyelwa futhi akukho okucela umhlinzeki: wena uletha ifayela.
- **Okushoyo.** Izinto eziphuzile zivela kuqala: "Kuphelelwe ngo-120 EA", "Isikhathi sokufika: izinsuku ezingu-21", "Ku-odolo olusalindile", "Iyunithi ihlukile", "Akukho inani lempahla" noma "Iyatholakala". {{ui:Longest wait wanted (days)}} iqala ku-14.
- **Uhlu lokuyala.** {{ui:Order list (CSV)}} ilanda izinto ezipheleyo nenani elipheleyo, umhlinzeki kanye nesikhathi sokufika.
- **Amanani angena emtapweni.** Uma ifayela linamanani, {{ui:Prices into the Library}} isetha inani lempahla yalo lonke ingenelo yomtapo enegama neyunithi efanayo. Buyekeza amanani emtapweni ukuze uwafake ebalweni.
- **Ephaneleni Yezinkinga.** Ukushoda yisexwayiso, nokulinda isikhathi eside kuyinothi, ngaphansi kwe-"Impahla yomhlinzeki". Ungayamukela eyodwa ngesizathu, futhi lesisizathu siyagcinwa kwibalo. Bona [Hlola impahla yomhlinzeki nezikhathi zokufika](topic:supplier-stock).

**Abasizi be-AI**

- **Amathuluzi wabo bobathathu.** Uma abasizi bevunyelwe kuthi {{ui:Settings}}, umsizi angafunda ukhiye (`legend_open`), enze ukubala (`legend_count_start`, bese `legend_count_status` no `legend_count_result`) bese ekwengeza (`legend_count_add`, okungabonisa kuqala). Ungafunda amanani akhunjuliwe (`prices_list`) futhi abeke amanani ezintweni kuwo (`prices_apply`, okungabonisa kuqala), bese efunda ukuhlolwa komhlinzeki (`supply_check`). Ukungeza ifayela lomhlinzeki kusalokhu kungowakho ukukwenza. Bona [Sebenzisa i-TakeoffCraft nabasizi be-AI](topic:use-with-ai-assistants).

**Kuzo zonke izinhlelo**

- **Izilimi.** Izilimi ezingamashumi amahlanu nane ngokubheka; eziyishumi zintsha (isiLithuania, isiLatvia, isiBosnia, isiMarathi, isiTelugu, isiAlbania, isiAfrikaans, isiFilipino, isiSwahili no-isiUzbek). Izilimi ezingeyona isiNgisi ziyizinhlaka zomshini kuze kube yilapho umuntu owayo ulimi eyifunda.
- **Amadivayisi okufaka.** {{ui:Settings}} › {{ui:Global}} › {{ui:Input devices}} isetha ukuthi i-trackpad, izinkinobho zohlangothi lwe-mouse, ipeni, i-3D mouse, ilawuli lemidlalo noma ilawuli le-MIDI lenza ini, kuyo yonke i-app noma kwenye. Bona [Sebenzisa i-trackpad, i-3D mouse noma ilawuli](common:input-devices).
- **Imigqa yebha yamathuluzi.** {{ui:Settings}} › {{ui:General}} › {{ui:Toolbar rows}} inikeza {{ui:Wrap}} (okokuqala), {{ui:2 rows}} no-{{ui:1 row}}; amaqembu angafaneli askrola ngasohlangothini. Bona [Hlela amaphaneli](common:arrange-panels).
- **Iphaneli yezinkinga.** Iphaneli eyabelanwa yezexwayiso nezinothi manje iphethe ukuhlola kwempahla yomhlinzeki okungenhla, eduze kwezinye izihlolo.
- **Okwengeziwe.** Okwengeziwe kulandela uhlu lwe-akhawunti yakho. Bona [Okwengeziwe nohlu lwe-akhawunti yakho](common:addons-basics).
- **Xhuma i-AI yakho.** CadCraft Studio ingumbhangqwana: xhuma umsizi wakho kanye kuzo zonke izinhlelo eziyisishiyagalombili. Studio ayinayo i-AI yayo.

### Okulungisiwe ku-0.8.1

- **Isigaba sama-DWG.** Isigaba samakilasi efayela le-DWG sifundwe njengohlobo olungalungile. Lokhu kulungisiwe ku-TakeoffCraft, nasezinhlelweni ezinye ezine.
- **Studio ingashintsha ulimi isasebenza.** Ngaphambili, Studio nezinguqulo zayo ezipakishiwe bekusisiNgisi kuphela. Lokhu akuhlolwanga kwisakhiwo sedeskithophu kuwo wonke amapuleti; sitshele uma ulimi lungashintshi.

### Akukatholakali kule nguqulo

- **Akusayinwanga ikhodi.** I-Windows ingase isexwayise, futhi kwi-Mac ucofa ngesokudla bese ukhetha {{ui:Open}} ngokuqala. Izakhiwo zesitolo (Microsoft Store, Google Play, App Store) zimisiwe, futhi ukwakhiwa kwe-iPad ne-iPhone akuzange kwakhiwe kabusha ku-0.8.1.
- **Izibuyekezo ze-Studio.** Umfakeli we-Windows namaphakheji e-Linux ahlola kanye ngosuku futhi abuze kuqala. I-zip eyiphathebula ikutshela kuphela, kanti i-macOS ne-APK azibuyekezi ngokwazo. I-.dmg ye-macOS ye-0.8 ilandela ukukhishwa.
- **Xhuma i-AI yakho.** Ukuxhuma ngokucindezela kanye ngodwa ngaphakathi kwe-Studio, isixhumi esibhalwe kwiwebhu se-AI kusiphequluli, ukhiye wakho we-API ngaphakathi kwe-Studio kanye nephakheji ye-MCP eshicilelwe akwethenjiswa kule nguqulo.
- **Bala konke.** Idinga izimpawu ezidwetshwe ngendlela efanayo nakukhiye, futhi impawu enodonga noma umugqa omde odlula kuyo ingase ingatholakali. Izimpawu ezibonakalisiwe azitholakali, neshidi elingenasikali elidwetshwe ngosayizi ohlukile lisesha ngosayizi wokhiye. Hlola okutholakele eshidini lokuqala ngaphambi kokuthembela kokunye.
- **Impahla yomhlinzeki.** Izinombolo zempahla zisasha njengefayela oyengezayo kuphela. Ukuhlola akusoze kucele umhlinzeki.
- **Izilimi.** Ibha yohlelo ngesiTamil iyagcwala ngaphezu kwayo.

Konke okunye kukuthi [Akukakwakhiwa](topic:not-built-yet). Ngenguqulo edlule kulena, bona [Okusha ku-0.7](topic:whats-new-0-7).
