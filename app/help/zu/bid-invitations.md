{{ui:Export}} › {{ui:Bid packages and leveling…}} › {{ui:Send for bids…}} ithumela iphakheji elilodwa kubantu obakhethile. Iwebhusayithi imemela ngayinye ngemeyili ngexhumanisi; bavula ikhasi, babone umsebenzi bese bathayipha inani lomugqa ngamunye. Ubona amanani esheethini lokulinganisa, ilungele ukuqhathaniswa ([Amaphakheji wethenda nokulinganisa kwethenda](topic:bid-leveling)).

:::note
Ukuthumela **akuvulekele wonke umuntu okwamanje.** Ngesikhathi isici sisahlolwa, sisebenza kumaakhawunti avunyelwe yithimba le-CadCraft; uma eyakho engenjalo, ingxoxo iyakutshela. Ikhasi lombhidi lisebenza kuyo yonke imemo esiyithumele.
:::

## Ngaphambi kokuthumela

- Udinga i-akhawunti ye-CadCraft enekheli eliqinisekisiwe. I-TakeoffCraft esiphequluleni icela ukuthi **Xhuma**: ithebhu ivuleka ku-getcadcraft.com, ucindezela Xhuma, bese leyo thebhu ithola ukungena okusebenza izinsuku ezingama-30, kuphela kumaphakheji wethenda, kuze uvale ithebhu ye-TakeoffCraft. Ungayisusa noma nini ekhasini lakho lokuphepha. Iwindi le-Studio alikwazi ukuthumela okwamanje.
- Nika into ngayinye inombolo ye-CSI: iphakheji ingumsebenzi wezinto zesigaba esisodwa se-CSI ([Dala izinto zokubala](topic:create-items)).
- Linganisa amashidi aphethe amamaka ephakheji, ukuze amanani ababhidi babona abalinganiswe ngokukala.

## Thumela iphakheji

1. Khetha {{ui:Send for bids…}} ngaphansi kwephakheji.
2. Gcwalisa isihloko, iphrojekthi nomlayezo ongakhethwa, nesikhathi **sokuphela** ngesikhathi sendawo yakho.
3. Shiya **Keep prices sealed** ithikiwe ukuze ufihle wonke amanani kuze kufike **isikhathi sokuvula** (hhayi ngaphambi kwesikhathi sokuphela), noma ususe ukuthikwa ukuze ubone amanani njengoba efika.
4. Shiya ibhokisi lamashidi lithikiwe ukuze uthumele i-PDF yamashidi aphethe amamaka ephakheji, nemaka adwetshwe kuwo. Amanani akho awasoze afakwe kulokho okuthunyelwayo: amagama ezinto, izinombolo ze-CSI, izinani, iziyunithi namagama amashidi kuphela.
5. Thayipha abantu obamemayo, ngabanye ngomugqa, njenge `Igama <name@company.com>` noma ikheli kuphela, noma uthike abantu ezincwadini zakho zabakhandisi. Kuya kwabangu-60 ngesikhathi, kuya kwangu-100 ngephakheji.
6. Khetha {{ui:Send invitations}}. Ngamunye uthola imeyili eyodwa esobala enegama lakho nekheli leimeyili yakho, isikhathi sokuphela nexhumanisi; izimpendulo ziya kuwe.

Ingxoxo itshela ukuthi bangaki abathunyelwe, futhi iqamba noma yimuphi owenqatshiwe: ababemenyiwe kuleli phakheji, abacelile ukungathunyelwa imemo yethenda, ikheli elingelona elisemthethweni, noma kudlule umkhawulo wokuthumela.

## Okubonwa ngumbhidi

Ikhasi ngexhumanisi elide eliyimfihlo, ngaphandle kokungena. Likhombisa ukuthi ubamemile ngubani, isikhathi sokuphela, uma amanani evaliwe, umlayezo wakho, ne-PDF yamashidi, bese kuba yonke imugqa nenani layo. Ngomugqa ngamunye uthayipha inani noma athike **Not included**, futhi angeza inothi; bangeza amanothi ephakheji lonke nemifayela engu-3 (isiphakamiso, incwadi yomsebenzi, isitifiketi). Bangalondoloza futhi bashintshe amanani kuze kufike isikhathi sokuvula, noma **bayenqabe**, kukhona ibhokisi lokucela ukuthi i-CadCraft ingabathumeli futhi imemo yethenda.

## Amathenda avaliwe nokuvulwa

Ngaphambi kwesikhathi sokuvula **akekho** obona inani, inothi noma ifayela: wena awukwazi, akukho ku-TakeoffCraft, akukho ekhasini lakho lamaphakheji wethenda. Ubona ukuthi ubamemile yini nokuthi baphendule yini. Ngesikhathi sokuvula zonke izinhlanganiso zivela ngesikhathi esisodwa. Ithenda elilondolozwe ngemva kwesikhathi sokuphela liyamukelwa lichazwe njenge **ngemva kwesikhathi**; uhlu lokulinganisa lusalibala, nokufika kwalo ngemva kwesikhathi kuyisimaka, hhayi isinqumo. Ngemva kokuvula umbhidi akakwazi ukushintsha inani. Ngaphandle kokuvala, ithenda liyaqedeka lapho lifika isikhathi sokuphela.

## Thola amanani

{{ui:Get prices}} icela iwebhusayithi bese yengeza impendulo ngayinye ephakheji njengethenda, limakwe ngokuthi **web**. I-TakeoffCraft nayo icela ngokuthula njalo lapho uvula ingxoxo uma ixhunyiwe. Ithenda umbhidi aliguqule ngalilondolozile lisethwa kabusha endaweni yalo; ithenda olihlelile wena lihlala njengoba wawushiya kuze kuthumele ngubani inguqulo entsha. Ukudonsa ngakunye kuyisinyathelo esisodwa esihlehlisa ngaso {{key:Mod+Z}}. Imigqa eshiywe iza njengomsebenzi ithenda elishiya ngaphandle, ngakho ukulinganisa kuyigcwalisa ngezindleko zakho.

## Ewebhusayithini

Ngemva kokuthi usuvumelekile ukuthumela, i-akhawunti yakho inamakhasi amabili engeziwe. **Amaphakheji wethenda** ludweba okuthunyelwe kwakho, abaphendulile, amathenda emuva kwesikhathi nemifayela, futhi buvumela ukuthumela isikhumbuzo (ixhumanisi elisha; elidala liyeka ukusebenza), ukuvala, ukuvula kabusha noma ukukhansela iphakheji. **Abakhandisi** bagcina irekhodi lenkampani ngayinye: oxhumana nabo, ukuhweba, izimpendulo zokufaneleka (iminyaka ebhizinisini, amandla okubopha, i-EMR, irekhodi lokuphepha, i-union noma i-open shop, izinkomba), usuku lokuphela kwelayisense nenshulansi, okumakwa uma sekudlule noma kusele izinsuku ezingama-30, kanye namathenda akhe adlule. Ngenisa nokuthumela amarekhodi njenge-CSV: umugqa owodwa ngoxhumana naye ngamunye, namakholomu ekhombisa ukuthumela; inkampani osuyinayo iyabuyekezwa, ayisoze iphindwe.

## Ubani obona ini, nokuthi isikhathi esingakanani

Amanani, amanothi nemifayela yombhidi ayaya kuwe nakwiqembu le-CadCraft elisebenzisa ikhasi. Zigcinwa izinyanga ezingama-24 kusukela osukwini lokuphela, imifayela izinsuku ezingama-180; bese ziyasuswa. Ikheli lemeyili lombhidi ngelowo ofake wena; lisetshenziselwa kuphela ukuthumela imemo nesikhumbuzo sayo. Ikheli elilodwa lithola imemo engaba ngu-3 ngosuku kusukela kubo bonke ndawonye, futhi ungathumela kuze kufike kwangu-200 ngosuku.
